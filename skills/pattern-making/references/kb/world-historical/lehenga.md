# Lehenga {#lehenga}

- **Aliases**: ghagra, lehnga, ghaghra choli (the set), pavada (southern); fr: lehenga
- **Family**: South Asian skirts
- **Recognize**: full floor-length skirt with a flared hem, often embroidered, drawstring or fitted waistband, worn with a choli and dupatta.
- **Pieces**: kali (gore) (6-16 depending on flare), waistband (1), lining (matching gores), can-can or underskirt (as needed)
- **Shape**: A gored (kali) skirt: tall narrow trapezoids that widen toward the hem, or a circular or semi-circular panel. The panel count varies with the flare. The waist is tapered and finished with a drawstring casing or a fitted waistband with a zip. Source: general South Asian tailoring practice; no drawn pattern read. Unverified numerically.
- **Drafting**: 1. Waist, hip, length. 2. With n gores: waist width per gore = waist/n plus allowance; hem width per gore = sweep/n. 3. Sweep is a design choice (not known). 4. Blend the waist-to-hip side curve.
- **Sewing order**: 1. embellish the panels flat before sewing. 2. join the gores. 3. add lining. 4. fit the waistband. 5. hem and trim.
- **Finishes and notions**: lining, can-can or horsehair braid at the hem for volume, zip, hook and eye, drawstring.
- **Fabrics**: silk, brocade, georgette, net; cut each gore on straight grain along its centre so the hem hangs evenly.
- **Fit faults**: seams spiral -> check grain on every gore; skirt drags at the waist -> use a firm waistband.
- **Variants**: #gored-skirt, #flared-skirt, #full-circle-skirt, #anarkali.
- **CLO3D**: gores as trapezoids; heavy silk preset; add a base petticoat for volume.
