# Anarkali {#anarkali}

- **Aliases**: Anarkali suit, frock-style kurta; fr: anarkali
- **Family**: South Asian dresses
- **Recognize**: fitted bodice to the underbust or waist, then a long full flare to knee or floor made of many panels, worn with churidar or salwar and dupatta.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (2, princess), bodice back (2), flare panel / kali (6-12), sleeve (2), neck facing (1), lining (matching)
- **Shape**: A princess-seam or empire bodice joined at the underbust or waist to a skirt of many gores that flares to a very wide hem (the umbrella cut). Each flare panel is a trapezoid. Some versions use a circular skirt cut instead. Source: general South Asian tailoring practice; no drawn pattern read. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Bodice as #saree-blouse, extended to the waist. 2. Skirt: n gores, waist width = bodice circumference/n, hem width = sweep/n. 3. Sweep: design choice (not known).
- **Sewing order**: 1. bodice seams. 2. join skirt gores. 3. skirt to bodice. 4. lining. 5. neckline and sleeves. 6. closure at the back or side. 7. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip, lining, hem facing for body.
- **Fabrics**: georgette, chiffon, silk crepe with a lining; sheer fabrics need lining to hold the shape.
- **Fit faults**: waist seam sits too high -> adjust at the underbust line; flare collapses -> add a hem facing or can-can.
- **Variants**: #lehenga, #kameez.
- **CLO3D**: bodice princess panels and gores sewn; tune bending stiffness to hold the flare.
- **Confidence**: from memory; numbers unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
