# 18th-century breeches {#breeches-18c}

- **Aliases**: knee breeches, fall-front breeches, fly-front breeches; fr: culotte
- **Family**: Historical men's garments
- **Recognize**: close-fitting trousers ending at or just below the knee, a fall-front (broad flap buttoned at the waist) from about the 1760s-70s, or a fly front earlier; a knee band closed by buttons or a buckle; a waistband with a cord or lacing at the centre back; side pockets.
- **Pieces**: front leg (2), back leg (2), fall (1), waistband (1), knee band (2), pocket bag (2), lining (matching), gusset in the back opening (1)
- **Shape**: Documented in museum records. V&A: the front closure changed from a fly front in the first half of the century to a fall front between 1750 and 1800; the back legs are cut higher than the fronts and gathered into the back of the waistband; the knee bands fasten with a length of woven lace with a worked opening to hold a buckle in one example. A Colonial Williamsburg pattern based on Arthur Middleton's breeches (1770s): fully lined in tightly woven white linen, adjustable back waistband with a cord threaded through four eyelets at the centre back, centre back seam open for 7 in (including 4 in of waistband) but covered by a gusset for 5 of those inches; four buttons on the side seam at the knee with a single button on the other side. Dimensions of each leg piece not given. Source: V&A breeches record (O15596); Colonial Williamsburg pattern description; Reconstructing History (dating of fall fronts to the late 1760s). Arnold, Patterns of Fashion 1 has drafts.
- **Drafting**: Not given in numbers by the sources read; use a close-fitting breeches block (see #trouser-block) shortened to the knee, with the back higher than the front. Waistband adjustment by lacing; knee band length to the calf.
- **Sewing order**: 1. line each leg; 2. side and inseam; 3. crotch; 4. fall; 5. waistband; 6. back opening and gusset; 7. knee bands; 8. buttons.
- **Finishes and notions**: buttons covered in the same cloth, eyelets, lining of linen, cord.
- **Fabrics**: wool broadcloth, silk, leather, fustian.
- **Fit faults**: seat bags -> shorten the rise at the back; knees pinch -> loosen the band.
- **Variants**: #trunk-hose (earlier), #waistcoat-18c, #mens-trousers (modern).
- **CLO3D**: four main panels; fall as a separate flap; knee band by buttons by hand.
- **Confidence**: documented (V&A; Colonial Williamsburg) for features; dimensions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
