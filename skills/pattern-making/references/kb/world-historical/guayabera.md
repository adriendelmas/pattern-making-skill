# Guayabera {#guayabera}

- **Aliases**: Cuban shirt, Mexican wedding shirt, Havana shirt, camisa de lino; fr: guayabera
- **Family**: Caribbean and Latin American shirts
- **Recognize**: untucked shirt with two vertical rows of narrow pin tucks (alforzas) on the front (and often the back), four patch pockets (two at the chest, two at the hips), short side vents with decorative buttons, shoulder yoke, short or long sleeves.
- **Pieces**: front (2, with tucks), back (1 or 2, with tucks), yoke (2), sleeve (2), cuff (optional), collar (1, with stand), patch pocket (4), placket (2)
- **Shape**: A casual shirt block with a front yoke, vertical pin tucks stitched through the front panels, four patch pockets; the side seams carry a short vent trimmed with buttons. Source: general descriptions of the garment; no dimensioned pattern was read. Unverified. The number of tucks varies by maker.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft a casual shirt block (see #shirt-block). 2. Add fabric for the tucks (each tuck uses double its width in cloth). 3. Place the pockets symmetrically. 4. Add short side vents of 10-15 cm (typical).
- **Sewing order**: 1. tucks (before cutting the front shape); 2. pockets; 3. yoke; 4. shoulders; 5. collar; 6. sleeves; 7. side seams and vents; 8. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: pearl or plain buttons, pin-tuck stitching, double needle or hand tucks.
- **Fabrics**: linen, cotton, light poplin; linen wrinkles but breathes.
- **Fit faults**: shirt rides up -> lengthen at the hem; tucks pucker -> press with steam and use a lighter fabric.
- **Variants**: #camp-collar-shirt, #casual-shirt-mens, #hawaiian-shirt.
- **CLO3D**: front panels with tucks as pleat fold lines; patch pockets as separate pieces.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
