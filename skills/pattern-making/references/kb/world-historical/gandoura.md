# Gandoura {#gandoura}

- **Aliases**: gandora, ghandoura, qandoura; fr: gandoura
- **Family**: Maghreb tunics
- **Recognize**: loose sleeveless or short-sleeved long tunic in light fabric, round or V neckline, straight cut, worn at home or over trousers.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), side gusset (2, optional), sleeve (2, optional), neck facing (1)
- **Shape**: A rectangle-based tunic cut on the fold at the shoulder, side seams straight or slightly flared; sleeves, if present, are short rectangles. Regional forms differ (it is described as a light long dress in Morocco and as a sleeveless tunic elsewhere in the Maghreb; check the specific region). Source: general knowledge; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length. 2. Width = chest + ease. 3. Neck depth and width by the style (not known).
- **Sewing order**: 1. shoulders. 2. neck facing. 3. side seams. 4. armhole finish. 5. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: bias binding, embroidered neck panel.
- **Fabrics**: lightweight cotton, voile, chiffon.
- **Fit faults**: armholes gape -> raise the underarm; neckline slips -> narrow the neck width.
- **Variants**: #kaftan, #djellaba, #dashiki.
- **CLO3D**: two rectangles with a neck opening, side seams sewn.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
