# Trunk hose (and round hose) {#trunk-hose}

- **Aliases**: slops, venetians (long), round hose, pumphose, paned hose, canions; fr: haut-de-chausses, grègues
- **Family**: Renaissance men's garments
- **Recognize**: padded, bag-shaped short breeches reaching the upper thigh, often made of vertical strips (panes) over a lining bag, tied to the doublet, worn with stockings or canions.
- **Pieces**: pane strips (many; the count varies by garment and is not known here), lining bag (2 legs), waistband (1), codpiece (optional), canions (2, if present)
- **Shape**: Described in Arnold, Patterns of Fashion 3. The hose are a lined, padded shell with decorative panes (strips) and under-lining; the panes are sewn to each other at the waist and hem and slashed to show the lining. No dimensions read here. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: Not known here.
- **Sewing order**: unverified.
- **Finishes and notions**: points (ties), lining, padding of horsehair or wool.
- **Fabrics**: silk, velvet, linen.
- **Fit faults**: unverified.
- **Variants**: #doublet, #breeches-18c.
- **CLO3D**: panes as strips sewn over a lining; padding by a quilted mesh layer.
- **Confidence**: documented by title only; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
