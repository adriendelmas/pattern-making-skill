# Kaftan (caftan) {#kaftan}

- **Aliases**: caftan, qaftan, Moroccan kaftan, Ottoman kaftan; fr: caftan
- **Family**: Middle Eastern and Ottoman robes
- **Recognize**: long robe with a straight T-silhouette, long sleeves, usually open at the front or with a neck opening; the Ottoman court form has a front opening with ties, and the Moroccan women's form is often closed with a waistband.
- **Pieces**: body (front and back, on the fold at the shoulder), sleeve (2), underarm gusset (2), side gore (2-4, godets), front facing or band (2), neck binding (1)
- **Shape**: Documented in museum cataloguing of the Ottoman kaftan: a T-form cut from straight lengths, with sleeve panels, underarm gussets and side gores (the garment is wider at the hem than at the chest). Court kaftans were cut from the narrow loom widths of woven silk, so the body is made of several joined panels (loom width not verified). Source: general museum descriptions; no dimensioned drawing was read here (Tilke and museum catalogues of the Topkapı collection should be checked). Numbers unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length to the ankle or floor. 2. Chest width = (chest + ease)/2. 3. Add gores at the side seams for the hem sweep. 4. Sleeve length from the shoulder to the wrist; the cuff may be a separate cuff.
- **Sewing order**: 1. shoulders. 2. neck and front facings. 3. sleeves with gusset. 4. side seams with gores. 5. hem. 6. ties.
- **Finishes and notions**: braid on the edges, ties or buttons, lining for the Ottoman form.
- **Fabrics**: silk brocade for court kaftans, cotton or silk crepe for light versions.
- **Fit faults**: flare drags at the sides -> reduce the gore width; sleeve restricts -> widen the gusset.
- **Variants**: #abaya, #djellaba, #thobe-kandura, #boubou.
- **CLO3D**: T-form with gussets and gores; large bending length; front opening free or tied.
- **Confidence**: from memory; documented in words; numbers unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
