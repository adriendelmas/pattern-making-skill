# Sherwani {#sherwani}

- **Aliases**: achkan (close relative), long coat; fr: sherwani
- **Family**: South Asian tailored coats
- **Recognize**: close-fitting knee-length coat with a high band collar, buttons to the neck (concealed placket or exposed), tailored shoulder and sleeve, side slits, worn over churidar or pyjama.
- **Pieces**: front (2), side front (2, if panelled), back (2), side back (2), sleeve (2), band collar (1), front facing (2), lining (as pieces), front placket (1)
- **Shape**: Constructed as a tailored coat (see #suit-jacket-single-breasted and #coat-block) with a stand collar, extended to knee length and closed to the neck. Princess or panel seams give a fitted torso; the skirt flares below the hip. No sherwani-specific drawn pattern was found. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take a coat block and extend to knee length. 2. Replace the lapel with a band collar (height typical 3-4 cm). 3. Add flare below the hip by pivoting the side seam. 4. Mark buttons at equal spacing (6-9 typical, by style).
- **Sewing order**: 1. interface fronts and collar. 2. panel seams. 3. shoulders. 4. collar. 5. sleeves. 6. facings and lining. 7. buttonholes.
- **Finishes and notions**: canvas or fusible interfacing, shoulder pads, lining, decorative buttons.
- **Fabrics**: silk brocade, raw silk, wool, jacquard; stiff fabrics need a firmer canvas.
- **Fit faults**: collar rises -> lower the neck curve; fronts flare open -> add overlap.
- **Variants**: #nehru-jacket (waist-length), #mao-jacket, #kurta.
- **CLO3D**: build as a coat with panel seams; collar as a stand; wool-silk preset.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
