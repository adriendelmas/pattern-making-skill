# Thobe / kandura {#thobe-kandura}

- **Aliases**: thawb, dishdasha, kandura (Emirati), dishdasha (Kuwait, Oman variants), jalabiya (Egypt, women's); fr: thobe, kandoura
- **Family**: Arabian Peninsula and Gulf robes (men's)
- **Recognize**: ankle-length straight robe with long sleeves and a front opening (placket) from the neck to the chest; the Saudi thobe has a collar and cuffs, the Emirati kandura has a round collarless neck with a perfumed tassel (tarboosha) hanging from the neck opening; a breast pocket on the left.
- **Pieces**: body (1, front and back cut as one with a neck hole), sleeve (2), side gusset or gore (2-4, at the hem), collar or neck band (1), placket (1), cuff (2), pocket (1-2)
- **Shape**: A T of rectangles. The body is cut from a long rectangle folded at the shoulder line; the sleeves are straight rectangles sewn to the body (or cut in one with it); the hem width at the side is widened by gussets or triangular gores set into the side seams. The neck opening is a round or keyhole cut with a short front placket. Source: general Gulf tailoring practice; no drawn pattern was read. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length from shoulder to ankle. 2. Width across the chest from the chest + ease; the width of cloth sets the body width. 3. Sleeve a straight rectangle, length to the wrist; a wide armhole. 4. Gusset width by the hem width wanted (not known).
- **Sewing order**: 1. shoulder fold and neck slit. 2. placket and collar. 3. sleeves. 4. side seams with gussets. 5. cuffs. 6. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: covered buttons, cufflink holes in Saudi style, a stiffened collar or interfacing on the Emirati neck band, a tassel on the placket.
- **Fabrics**: white cotton or polyester blends in summer, wool in winter; light shades are sheer, so the seams and hems need clean finishes.
- **Fit faults**: sleeves pull at the underarm -> lengthen the gusset; hem rides up when sitting -> add side hem width.
- **Variants**: #kaftan, #abaya, #djellaba, #gandoura.
- **CLO3D**: rectangular body, gussets as triangles, collar band; cotton preset.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
