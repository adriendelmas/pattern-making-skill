# Djellaba {#djellaba}

- **Aliases**: jellaba, jilaba, burnous (cloak relative), qob (the hood); fr: djellaba
- **Family**: Maghreb robes (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia)
- **Recognize**: long hooded robe with a pointed hood (qob or qannoura), long sleeves, front opening on the chest closed with buttons or toggles and loops, side slits near the hem, loose straight shape.
- **Pieces**: body front (1), body back (1), sleeve (2), hood (1, one rectangle or two panels), front facing (1), hem and slit finishes
- **Shape**: A T of straight cuts: front and back panels cut from lengths of cloth, joined at the shoulders (or cut in one), straight sleeves set at the armhole with an underarm gusset, a gusset or godet at the side seam for width. The hood is a rectangle folded to a point (or two panels joined along the top and back seam) and sewn to the round neck; the front opening is a vertical slit from the neck to mid-chest, finished with braid and buttons. Source: general knowledge; Tilke "Costume Patterns and Designs" is expected to include Maghreb garments and should be checked. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length to ankle. 2. Width at the hem wide enough to walk, widened by gusset or gore. 3. Hood: rectangle about head circumference + ease wide and 1.5-2 times the head height tall (typical, unverified), folded and sewn on the back edge. 4. Front slit 20-30 cm (typical).
- **Sewing order**: 1. shoulders. 2. neck facing. 3. hood to neck. 4. sleeves with gusset. 5. side seams to the slit. 6. braid, buttons and loops. 7. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: sfifa braid, toggle buttons, loop closures; hand-finished edges.
- **Fabrics**: wool or wool blend for winter, cotton or linen for summer; heavy wool needs less flare.
- **Fit faults**: hood falls off the head -> shorten and narrow it at the neck join; side seams pull -> widen the gusset.
- **Variants**: #thobe-kandura, #kaftan, #gandoura.
- **CLO3D**: hood as a separate piece sewn at the neck; wool preset; check the hem sweep.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
