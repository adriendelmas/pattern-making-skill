# Kurta {#kurta}

- **Aliases**: Indian long shirt, kurta pyjama (with pyjama); fr: kurta
- **Family**: South Asian tunics
- **Recognize**: hip- to knee-length collarless (or band-collar) shirt, side slits, short front placket (patti) of a few buttons, straight sleeves, underarm gusset.
- **Pieces**: front (1, with slit placket), back (1), sleeve (2), underarm gusset (2), neck band or facing (1), placket strip (1)
- **Shape**: The straight kurta is a T of rectangles: front and back cut in one with the shoulder, sleeves as rectangles sewn to the straight armhole, a square gusset set into the underarm, side slits starting at the hem. The kalidar kurta replaces the straight side seam with side panels (kali) that flare from hip to hem. Dimensions documented for a child's kalidar kurta in an Indian school textbook (Brainkart reproduction of 12th-grade Textiles and Dress Designing: Practical, size 4-5 years): gusset 8 x 8 cm; side panel 9 cm wide at the top and 8 cm at the bottom, 35 cm high; scale for adults. Source: that textbook page; the rest is general practice and unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure chest, shoulder, length, sleeve, neck. 2. Half-width = (chest + ease)/4. 3. Neck width about one sixth of the neck circumference (typical drafting convention). 4. For flare, one instruction sheet narrows the side panels 1-2 inches at the top and widens them at the hem. 5. Gusset: square.
- **Sewing order**: 1. placket. 2. shoulders. 3. neckband. 4. sleeves. 5. gusset and side seams. 6. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: covered or plain buttons, collar band, topstitching along the placket.
- **Fabrics**: cotton, linen, silk, khadi, chikankari embroidery.
- **Fit faults**: shoulders sag -> reduce shoulder width; rides up when sitting -> lengthen the slits.
- **Variants**: #kameez (women's), #sherwani (formal coat), #nehru-jacket.
- **CLO3D**: rectangles with shaped armhole; gusset set diagonally; placket as an overlap.
- **Confidence**: documented for the child-size kali and gusset; adult drafting unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
