# Dirndl {#dirndl}

- **Aliases**: Dirndlkleid, Tracht dress; fr: dirndl
- **Family**: Alpine folk dress (Bavaria, Austria, South Tyrol)
- **Recognize**: close-fitting bodice (Mieder) with a square or sweetheart neckline, laced or hook-and-eye front closure, a short-sleeved white blouse worn under it, a gathered skirt, and a separate apron tied at the waist with a bow.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (2, with princess seams or darts), bodice back (2-4, with side back), bodice lining (matching), skirt (1, rectangles gathered), apron (1), waistband (1), blouse (separate: front, back, sleeve)
- **Shape**: The bodice is a fitted, boned or lightly interlined princess-seam bodice with a low square neckline; it closes at the front with lacing over hooks, hooks and eyes, or a zip. The skirt is one or two straight rectangles gathered or knife-pleated to the waist; the apron is a straight rectangle with a waistband and ties. Source: general knowledge; no dimensioned pattern was read. Unverified. In tradition the position of the apron bow carries a social code; do not assume it without knowing the audience.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft a close-fitting bodice block with princess seams (see #close-fitting-bodice-block). 2. Cut the neckline square or sweetheart, low. 3. Skirt width = 1.5-2 times the waist for gathers (typical convention). 4. Skirt length: knee, midi or long, by style. 5. Apron slightly shorter than the skirt.
- **Sewing order**: 1. bodice seams; 2. line and finish the bodice; 3. lacing or closure; 4. gather skirt to the bodice; 5. hem; 6. apron separate; 7. blouse separately.
- **Finishes and notions**: lacing cord and hooks, boning (optional), contrast binding, buttons, lace trim.
- **Fabrics**: cotton, linen, brocade for the bodice; jacquard for the apron; stiffer cloth for the bodice.
- **Fit faults**: bodice gapes -> add a dart at the neck edge; bust is compressed -> shape the bust with a princess seam and a bust dart.
- **Variants**: #cotehardie (earlier fitted gown), #folk-shirt.
- **CLO3D**: bodice as a fitted bodice, skirt panels gathered with shirring or pleats; apron as a separate rectangle tied at the waist (placed by hand).
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
