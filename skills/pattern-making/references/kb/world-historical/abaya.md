# Abaya {#abaya}

- **Aliases**: aba, open abaya (kimono abaya), closed abaya, butterfly abaya; fr: abaya
- **Family**: Arabian Peninsula and Gulf robes
- **Recognize**: floor-length loose robe, usually black, T-shaped, long sleeves, open at the front (worn over clothes) or closed with a front zip or snaps; sleeves cut in one with the body or set in, often with a cuff; hem wide enough to walk.
- **Pieces**: front (2 if open, or 1 cut on the fold if closed), back (1, on the fold or with a centre-back seam), sleeve (2, or cut in one with the body), neck or front facing (1), cuff (2, optional)
- **Shape**: In modern commercial forms the body is a long rectangle folded over the shoulder line, with the sleeve cut in one with the body as a slanted T (the underarm angle sets the sleeve fullness), or with a separate straight sleeve sewn to a low armhole. The side seam runs straight or flares slightly from the underarm to the hem. Open versions have no front closure, so the front edges are finished with a binding or facing. Source: general modern abaya cutting practice; no museum drawn pattern or published dimensions were read. Unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length shoulder to floor. 2. Width at the chest = (chest + generous ease)/2 per layer, set loose (not known). 3. Sleeve slope: draw the sleeve as a line from the neck point at about 10-25 degrees below horizontal for a T-sleeve (typical range; unverified). 4. Flare 5-15 cm at the hem per side (typical, unverified). 5. Front neckline either a slit or round neck 8-9 cm drop (typical).
- **Sewing order**: 1. shoulder seams (or fold). 2. sleeve under-arm and side seam in one pass. 3. facing or binding on the front edges. 4. cuffs. 5. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps or a long zip for closed styles, piping or embroidery on the cuffs and fronts, bias binding.
- **Fabrics**: crepe, nida, georgette, chiffon, linen; a fluid fabric drapes from the shoulders, so reduce flare for heavier fabrics.
- **Fit faults**: sleeve restricts the arm -> deepen the underarm curve and widen the sleeve; hem swings forward -> cut the front hem slightly longer.
- **Variants**: #kaftan, #thobe-kandura, #kimono (compare the T-shape).
- **CLO3D**: fold-based T-shape; sleeve in one with the body; large bending length; if the abaya is open, do not sew the front edges.
- **Confidence**: from memory (modern practice); shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
