# Folk shirt (Trachtenhemd / Alpine shirt) {#folk-shirt}

- **Aliases**: Trachtenhemd, Alpine shirt, Bavarian shirt; fr: chemise folklorique
- **Family**: European folk dress
- **Recognize**: loose shirt with a stand collar, a short front placket with a few buttons, sometimes a stitched yoke, gathered sleeves into cuffs, often in checked or striped cotton, buttons of horn or antler.
- **Pieces**: front (1 or 2), back (1), yoke (2), sleeve (2), cuff (2), collar band (2), placket (1), underarm gusset (2, in traditional versions)
- **Shape**: In its older form the folk shirt follows the rectangular shirt construction (see #historical-shirt-rectangular) with body rectangles and square gussets; modern commercial versions are drafted on shirt blocks (see #shirt-block and #casual-shirt-mens). Source: general knowledge; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Use a casual shirt block with a stand collar. 2. Optional yoke of two layers. 3. Gather the sleeve cap or the upper arm into a cuff of 6-8 cm (typical). 4. Placket length 12-15 cm (typical).
- **Sewing order**: 1. yoke and shoulders; 2. collar; 3. placket; 4. sleeves; 5. cuffs; 6. side seams; 7. hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: horn buttons, topstitching, contrast piping.
- **Fabrics**: checked cotton, linen, flannel.
- **Fit faults**: collar tight -> lengthen the stand; sleeves restrict -> add gusset or deepen the armhole.
- **Variants**: #casual-shirt-mens, #mens-dress-shirt, #historical-shirt-rectangular.
- **CLO3D**: block shirt with a collar stand; pattern checks as a texture.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
