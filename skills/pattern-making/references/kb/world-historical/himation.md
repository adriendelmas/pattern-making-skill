# Himation {#himation}

- **Aliases**: Greek mantle, Greek cloak, palla (Roman equivalent for women, different cut), chlamys (short rectangle); fr: himation
- **Family**: Ancient Greek draped garments
- **Recognize**: large rectangular woollen cloth wrapped around the body, over the left shoulder and under the right arm, worn over a chiton or alone.
- **Pieces**: rectangle (1)
- **Shape**: A large rectangle with no sewing. Dimensions reported by a reenactment site (Tastes of History, low-reliability): about 4-5 m long and 1.2-1.5 m wide. Encyclopedia sources say generally large rectangular pieces in various dimensions; an 1867 handbook calls it square. Unverified. Weights were placed in the corners to keep the folds hanging (reported in general sources).
- **Drafting**: 1. Start from the reported size range (about 4-5 m x 1.2-1.5 m) and adjust to the wearer by a trial wrap. 2. Hem all four edges.
- **Sewing order**: hem the edges only.
- **Finishes and notions**: hand-rolled hem, corner weights or tassels.
- **Fabrics**: wool, fine linen.
- **Fit faults**: slips -> place the weighted corner on the shoulder fold; too short for the wrap -> use a longer cut.
- **Variants**: #chiton, #toga-roman, #sarong-pareo (unsewn rectangle).
- **CLO3D**: rectangle placed with pins on the avatar; arrange by hand.
- **Confidence**: from memory / low-reliability reenactment sources; dimensions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
