# Skirt Lengths {#skirt-lengths}

- **Aliases**: mini, above-knee, knee-length, midi, maxi, floor-length; fr: longueurs de jupe
- **Family**: skirt, reference
- **Recognize**: named by where the hem meets the leg: mini (mid-thigh), above-knee, knee, midi (mid-calf), maxi (ankle), floor-length.
- **Pieces**: not applicable (a reference entry for all skirt entries).
- **Shape**: Not a garment shape. Typical hem positions measured from the waist along CB or the side seam on an adult woman of 165 to 170 cm (figures vary by stature, to be replaced by measured values): mini about 40 to 50 cm, knee about 55 to 65 cm, midi about 70 to 80 cm, maxi about 90 to 100 cm, floor-length about 100 to 110 cm. Waist-to-floor measured per client. Unverified; typical values.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure waist to the desired hem on the body, front, side and back. 2. Subtract the waistband height if the band is added above. 3. Add the hem allowance. 4. For circle and flared skirts add 1 to 3 cm at the bias zones for drop.
- **Sewing order**: not applicable.
- **Finishes and notions**: not applicable.
- **Fabrics**: not applicable.
- **Fit faults**: hem appears uneven when worn -> check the posture and level on a dress form; skirt too short when sitting -> add 2 to 5 cm.
- **Variants**: #straight-skirt, #pencil-skirt, #full-circle-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: position the avatar's height to match the measured values.
- **Confidence**: from memory; typical.
