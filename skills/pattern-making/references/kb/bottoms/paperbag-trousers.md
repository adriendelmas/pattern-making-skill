# Paperbag-Waist Trousers {#paperbag-trousers}

- **Aliases**: paper bag waist pants, cinched-waist trousers; fr: pantalon paperbag, pantalon taille froncée
- **Family**: trousers, gathered waist
- **Recognize**: waist raised and gathered with elastic or a belt a few cm below the top edge, the extra fabric puffing out above.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2 (extended at the top), casing or waistband, elastic, optional belt and loops.
- **Shape**: Raise the waist on the block; extra height above the elastic casing forms the ruffle. Published tutorials: extend CF/CB and the side seams up by a fixed amount (one source uses 7 cm as a starting point) or by the distance from the natural waist to the new top, then square across; casing position drawn at elastic width + a small ease (about 0.9 cm) below the top, leaving the bag above it. One source notes the construction suits skirts best and that for trousers volume is added by slash and spread. Outline: front and back as the block with the top edge a straight line wider than the waist to allow gathering. Source: tutorial pages (Sew Over It, Simply Sewing, Ellie and Mac); shape unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Extend upward. 3. Slash and spread the top for volume. 4. Mark the casing line(s). 5. Casing height = elastic width + 0.6 to 1 cm.
- **Sewing order**: darts or pleats, pockets, side seams, inseams, crotch, casing, elastic, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 2 to 3 cm, self-fabric belt optional, belt loops.
- **Fabrics**: soft wovens (viscose, linen, crepe); stiff fabrics bulk at the waist.
- **Fit faults**: bag collapses -> too little extra above the elastic; bulk -> less ease, soft fabric.
- **Variants**: #high-waisted-trousers, #dirndl-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: gather ratio at the casing seam; elastic via shrinkage.
- **Confidence**: tutorials checked online; draft values typical.
- **Status**: unverified
