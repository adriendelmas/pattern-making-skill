# Palazzo Trousers {#palazzo-trousers}

- **Aliases**: palazzo pants, extra-wide pants; fr: pantalon palazzo
- **Family**: trousers, extra wide
- **Recognize**: very wide, flowing legs from the hip, skirt-like swing, usually high-waisted with elastic or a flat front, floor-skimming hem.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, waistband or elastic casing; pleats optional.
- **Shape**: Hem flat width 40 to 55 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2), obtained by slash and spread from the crease line in 2 to 3 slashes: front and back each slashed from the hem to the waist and spread by equal amounts; hem circumference > 1.2 m. The side seam and inseam flare out from the hip and crotch. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with +1 cm rise. 2. Slash from the hem to the waist at the crease and halves; spread to widen. 3. Redraw the hem as a smooth curve square to the seams. 4. Add waist pleats or gathers for the extra width at the waist, or keep the added width low by spreading from the knee.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser; elastic option.
- **Finishes and notions**: narrow or weighted hem, elastic waist 3 to 4 cm.
- **Fabrics**: viscose, silk crepe, georgette, jersey; drapey fabrics hang as skirt folds.
- **Fit faults**: bulk at the hip -> do not spread above the hip; hem trips -> shorten or measure with heels.
- **Variants**: #wide-leg-trousers, #culottes, #gauchos.
- **CLO3D**: lightweight drapey fabric; 5 mm particles; simulate walking pose if possible.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
