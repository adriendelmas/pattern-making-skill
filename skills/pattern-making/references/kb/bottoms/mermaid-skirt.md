# Mermaid Skirt {#mermaid-skirt}

- **Aliases**: fishtail skirt, fish-tail skirt; fr: jupe sirène
- **Family**: skirt, fitted-and-flared
- **Recognize**: very close fit from waist to the knee or below, then a sudden flare, often a flounce, godets or a long train; usually floor-length.
- **Pieces**: 6 to 8 gores (or front and back with godets), optional flounce x1 (circle section), waistband or facing.
- **Shape**: As #trumpet-skirt, with the flare start lower (at or below the knee, mid-calf) and a hem sweep of 2 to 3 x H or more. The flare may be a circle-section flounce sewn to the straight upper section at the flare line (flounce inner edge = fitted lower edge), or godets in slits. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Fitted upper skirt to the flare line; knee width = knee circumference + ease so the wearer can walk. 2. Flounce: a circle section with inner radius so that the arc equals the width at the flare line (r = arc/angle), outer radius = r + flounce height. 3. For godets see #godet.
- **Sewing order**: as #trumpet-skirt; join flounce or godets last; hang before the hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip, stay tape at the flare seam, boning or lining optional, horsehair braid at the hem.
- **Fabrics**: satin, crepe, stretch lace; heavy fabrics pull the flare down.
- **Fit faults**: cannot walk -> add a vent or slit in the fitted section; hem drags -> shorten or lift the train.
- **Variants**: #trumpet-skirt, #godet, #gored-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: separate fitted and flounce pieces sewn at the flare seam; fine mesh at the flare line.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet A11; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
