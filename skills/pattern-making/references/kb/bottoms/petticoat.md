# Petticoat {#petticoat}

- **Aliases**: underskirt, crinoline (stiffened), slip (smooth), tulle underskirt; fr: jupon, fond de jupe, petticoat
- **Family**: skirt, underlayer
- **Recognize**: gathered or tiered underskirt in tulle, net or stiff cotton worn under skirts and dresses to add volume; elastic waist; hem often scalloped or edged with lace or horsehair.
- **Pieces**: yoke or waistband x1 (or elastic casing), tiers x2 to x4 (rectangles or circle sections), lining skirt x1 optional (smooth base).
- **Shape**: Gathered tiers like #tiered-skirt, each tier sewn to the tier above at a gathered seam, ratio 2 to 3 for tulle; the top tier or yoke is smooth and fits the waist and hip. A circle-section variant uses annulus pieces (see #full-circle-skirt). Each tier's gathered edge sews to the lower edge of the tier above. Unverified as a drawn pattern; ratios from the techniques sheet.
- **Drafting**: 1. Length shorter than the overskirt hem by 2 to 5 cm. 2. Tier heights equal or increasing. 3. Width ratio 2 to 3 for tulle, 1.5 to 2 for cotton. 4. Waist elastic casing; hip opening large enough to pass over the hips.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Close tiers into rings. 2. Gather each tier. 3. Join tier to tier, top to bottom. 4. Waistband or casing. 5. Edge the hem with lace or finish.
- **Finishes and notions**: horsehair braid at the hem, lace edge, elastic waist, optional lining.
- **Fabrics**: tulle, net, stiff cotton, organza, taffeta.
- **Fit faults**: shows through -> lengthen or add a smooth layer; scratchy on thighs -> add a lining layer.
- **Variants**: #tiered-skirt, #full-circle-skirt, #double-circle-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: lightweight tulle preset, gather ratio per tier; heavy simulation, 5 mm particles.
- **Confidence**: construction typical; not read from a drawn pattern.
- **Status**: unverified
