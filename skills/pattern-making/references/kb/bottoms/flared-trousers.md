# Flared Trousers {#flared-trousers}

- **Aliases**: flares, bell-bottoms (extreme), flare pants; fr: pantalon évasé, pantalon patte d'éléphant
- **Family**: trousers, flared
- **Recognize**: fitted thigh and knee, leg widening toward the hem into a bell shape.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Shape**: Narrow to the knee, wide hem: hem flat 28 to 36 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2). Slash from the knee to the hem and spread by 3 to 6 cm per slash, or move the hem point outward in a straight line from the knee. Knee width as the slim leg. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft the slim leg. 2. Move the hem outward symmetrically about the crease to the target width (flat). 3. Redraw side and inseam from the knee straight to the new hem point. 4. Lengthen the hem so it nearly touches the floor with heels.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: hem 4 to 5 cm, optional hem weight.
- **Fabrics**: stretch twill, crepe, denim, velvet; stiff fabrics hold the bell.
- **Fit faults**: hem pulls forward -> balance the flare front/back; knee too tight -> add 1 cm.
- **Variants**: #bootcut-trousers, #wide-leg-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: 5 mm particles; fabric weight and bending stiffness matter for hem flare.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2.
- **Status**: unverified
