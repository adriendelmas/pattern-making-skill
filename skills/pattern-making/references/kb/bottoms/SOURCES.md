# Sources: bottoms

- Techniques sheet `references/techniques/skirts-trousers.md` (parts A1 to A14 and B1 to B10): main source of all dimensions, ratios and sewing orders; its numbers marked "checked" were cross-checked against published drafts by its author.
- dresspatternmaking.com, Circle Skirts, Skirt with Knife Pleats, Skirt with Inverted Box Pleat, Flared Skirt 2, A-line skirt pages (text read, diagrams not read): circle radius factors, knife pleat example (5 pleats, 1.5 in depth, 3 in underlay), inverted pleat cut-out/backing, flare spread figures. https://dresspatternmaking.com/patternmaking-basics/garment-elements/skirts-e/skirt-with-knife-pleats and .../skirt-with-inverted-box-pleat
- Textbook pleat example (box pleat: depth 12 cm, finished width 60 cm, pattern width 108 cm), CITS Dress Making Trade Practical Vol. 2, p. 204: https://nimi.co.in/cits_ebooks/Dress_Making/Dress_Making_Trade_Practical_(Volume_2)/files/basic-html/page204.html
- Sewing with Numbers, How to draft pleats; Minerva Patterns, Drafting pleats (knife, box, inverted): https://sewingwithnumbers.substack.com/p/project-013-how-to-draft-pleats ; https://minervapatterns.com/blog/drafting-pleats-knife-box-and-inverted (search snippets only).
- Threads magazine and thecreativecurator.com godet descriptions (sector geometry), as used in the godet entry.
- Harem trousers: Simply Sewing free harem pants pattern (hip + 30 cm / 4 per panel; template method, waist extended 15 to 20 cm): https://simplysewingmag.com/sewing-patterns/get-jazzy-with-it-free-harem-pants-pattern
- Paperbag waist: Sew Over It, Ultimate Trousers Hack (raising the waistline) https://sewoverit.com/ultimate-trousers-hack-raising-the-waistline-and-adding-a-waistband ; Simply Sewing, how to sew pants ; Ellie and Mac, paper bag mash up (casing position) https://www.ellieandmac.com/blogs/blog/fell-pretty-paper-bag-mash-up
- Jodhpurs and breeches: US patents US7533423B2 and US8146176B2 (panel construction, inseam and knee/seat patches); Sidney Museum and Archives jodhpurs record (full hip, tight below knee, inner-leg patch to within 8 cm of hem); Don McCunn / Cutters' Practical Guide jodhpur drafting method (historic).
- Sailor trousers: Royal Museums Greenwich, Royal Naval uniform pattern 1831 (flap front, buttons): https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-71390 ; University of Westminster archive item 2020.24.1 (white linen fall-front trousers); Folkwear Sailor Pant sew-along (button counts 12 to 16): https://www.folkwear.com/blogs/news/229-sailor-pant-sew-along-day-11 ; Sew Daily, sailor pants pattern hack; surplus listings for US Navy sailor pants (13-button broadfall).
- Stirrup pants: Seamwork Block Paper Scissors 34 (stirrup 2 to 3 inches wide, cut four), WeAllSew DIY Stirrup Leggings, Patterns for Pirates peg-leg stirrup hack, Wikipedia Stirrup pants.
- Dungarees: SQA children's dungarees instructions (bib, back bib, straps, facings, 1.5 cm facing turn): https://www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/h-fashion-textile-technology-childrens-dungarees-instructions-and-pattern.pdf ; Lyon kids overalls pattern listing (ikatee); Stitch Sisters dungarees (bib pattern).
- Not consulted as drawn patterns: Aldrich, Armstrong, Müller & Sohn, Esmod, Bunka books (no drawn outline for these garments was read by the author). Every outline in this file is therefore marked "unverified" unless a specific source is named in the Shape field.
