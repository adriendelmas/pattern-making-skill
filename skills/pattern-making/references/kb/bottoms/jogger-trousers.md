# Jogger Trousers {#jogger-trousers}

- **Aliases**: joggers, sweatpants, track trousers, tracksuit bottoms; fr: pantalon jogging, jogging
- **Family**: trousers, knit, sport
- **Recognize**: tapered leg with elastic cuffs at the ankle, elastic or drawcord waist, side pockets, knit fabric (fleece, French terry, jersey).
- **Pieces**: leg x2 (front and back combined or separate), waistband x1, ankle cuffs x2, pocket bags x2 pairs, drawcord.
- **Shape**: Based on the elastic-waist trouser block (techniques sheet B7): top width = hip + 2 to 6 cm so the garment passes over the hips; legs taper from the thigh to an ankle opening sized by the cuff (cuff circumference about ankle + 1 to 2 cm stretched, cut about 0.8 x); side pockets as an on-seam opening of 15 to 18 cm. The cuff is a rib band tube sewn to the leg hem, the leg hem circumference = cuff length x 1.2 to 1.5 (gathered) or sized to the band. Outline unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block, remove darts. 2. Waist top = hip circumference + 2 to 6. 3. Taper from the knee to the ankle (leg ankle width = 18 to 24 cm flat). 4. Elastic casing: height = 2 x (elastic width + 0.3) + SA; elastic length waist x 0.85 to 0.92. 5. Cuff: length = ankle x 0.85 to 0.9, width 2 x finished + SA.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pockets. 2. Crotch seams. 3. Side seams. 4. Inseams. 5. Cuffs to legs (quarter pin and stretch). 6. Waist casing, elastic, drawcord.
- **Finishes and notions**: 3-thread overlock, twin-needle hems, elastic 3 to 4 cm, drawcord and eyelets, rib cuff.
- **Fabrics**: French terry, fleece, cotton-poly jersey; stretch and recovery matter for the cuffs.
- **Fit faults**: waist rolls -> elastic too narrow; cuffs bag -> cut shorter; crotch hangs low -> shorten the rise.
- **Variants**: #tapered-trousers, #harem-trousers, #cargo-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; elastic waist and cuffs by shrinkage on the ribbing pieces; 5 mm mesh.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B7; ease and cuff factors typical.
- **Status**: unverified
