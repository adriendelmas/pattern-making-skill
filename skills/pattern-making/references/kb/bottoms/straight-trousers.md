# Straight Trousers {#straight-trousers}

- **Aliases**: straight-leg trousers, regular-fit trousers; fr: pantalon droit
- **Family**: trousers, from the block
- **Recognize**: parallel legs from knee to hem, hem width roughly equal to the knee.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Shape**: As the block of #classic-trouser: hem flat 20 to 24 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2), knee width approximately equal to the hem; side and inseam parallel to the crease line below the knee. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Hem width = 0.125 W + 13 (Method A; 21.75 for W 70). 3. Knee = hem + 1.3 each side.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Fabrics**: wool, twill, cotton, linen.
- **Fit faults**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Variants**: #slim-trousers, #wide-leg-trousers, #bootcut-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B1/B2.
- **Status**: unverified
