# Culottes {#culottes}

- **Aliases**: divided skirt trousers, wide shorts (short version), culotte pants; fr: jupe-culotte, culotte
- **Family**: trousers, wide, shortened (see also #culotte-skirt)
- **Recognize**: wide legs ending between knee and mid-calf, A-line outline, often high-waisted with pleats or a side zip.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, waistband x1, zip or fly facing, pocket bags.
- **Shape**: Trouser block shortened to mid-calf; hem flat width 30 to 50 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2); rise +1 cm; side seam and inseam both flare from the hip and crotch to the hem; hem must exceed half the thigh circumference. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block rise +1 cm. 2. Cut at the chosen length. 3. Widen with slash and spread or by moving the side seam and inseam out. 4. Add front pleats if needed.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip, waistband, narrow hem.
- **Fabrics**: crepe, linen, wool, twill.
- **Fit faults**: legs swing -> check grain; crotch bunches -> reduce the extension.
- **Variants**: #culotte-skirt, #gauchos, #palazzo-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: build as trousers.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
