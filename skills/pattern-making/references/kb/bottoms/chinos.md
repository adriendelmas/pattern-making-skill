# Chinos {#chinos}

- **Aliases**: chino trousers, khakis; fr: chino
- **Family**: trousers, casual, from the trouser block
- **Recognize**: cotton twill trousers with slanted front pockets, back welt or jetted pockets (often one with a button), flat or single-pleat front, belt loops, zip fly, slim-straight leg, cuffed or plain hem.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, pocket bags x4 (two facing, two back), pocket facings x2, back pocket welts/jets x2 per pocket and flap optional, waistband x1, belt loops x5, fly facing x1 and shield x1.
- **Shape**: As #classic-trouser. Front slanted pocket opening starts at the waist 5 to 7 cm from the side seam and ends on the side seam 15 to 19 cm below the waist (techniques sheet B8); opening line straight or slightly curved. Back welt pocket opening 12 to 15 cm (women), 14 to 16 cm (men), 8 to 10 cm below the waist, centred on the dart, jet width 0.5 cm each. Leg hem flat width 19 to 22 cm. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with hip ease 6 to 8 cm total for men's cotton twill, 4 to 6 women. 2. Add slant pockets and welt pockets. 3. Slim hem to 19 to 22 cm flat. 4. Waistband with extension 3 to 4 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets, fly, front crotch, back pockets, back crotch, side seams, inseams, waistband, belt loops, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: topstitching 6 mm, bar tacks at pocket corners, button/zip, hem 3 to 4 cm.
- **Fabrics**: cotton twill 200 to 300 g/m2, gabardine; stretch twill allows slimmer fit.
- **Fit faults**: as #classic-trouser; slanted pocket gapes -> stay tape on the opening.
- **Variants**: #classic-trouser, #slim-trousers, #cargo-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: twill preset, topstitch tool for pocket and fly edges.
- **Confidence**: trade practice and techniques sheet B8; dimensions typical.
- **Status**: unverified
