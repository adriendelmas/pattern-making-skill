# High-Waisted Trousers {#high-waisted-trousers}

- **Aliases**: high-rise trousers, high-rise pants; fr: pantalon taille haute
- **Family**: trousers, raised waist
- **Recognize**: waist 5 to 8 cm above the natural waist, wide waistband, often pleats and buttons or a side zip.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser; waistband wide (4 to 7 cm).
- **Shape**: Raise the waist line at the side and CF by 4 to 8 cm (techniques sheet B2). Measure the body at the high-waist level (rib-waist measurement); the pattern width at the new waist = measured high waist + ease. Continue the side seam up following the waist-to-hip shape and add darts or pleats to remove the width at the top. Contour band recommended. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Extend CF and side seam up. 3. Square the waist line, add a contour. 4. Take excess width into darts or pleats. 5. Check the high-waist measure.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser; sew the band last.
- **Finishes and notions**: interfaced waistband, button or hooks, optional boning at the CB.
- **Fabrics**: wool, crepe, twill; stretch wovens allow comfort.
- **Fit faults**: waist gapes at CB -> raise less or take 1 cm out at CB; band digs in -> add 1 cm or ease.
- **Variants**: #paperbag-trousers, #pleated-trousers, #sailor-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: as #classic-trouser; position the waist at the rib line on the avatar.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2.
- **Status**: unverified
