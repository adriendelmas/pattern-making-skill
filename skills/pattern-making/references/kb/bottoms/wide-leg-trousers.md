# Wide-Leg Trousers {#wide-leg-trousers}

- **Aliases**: wide trousers, loose trousers; fr: pantalon large
- **Family**: trousers, wide
- **Recognize**: wide from hip to hem, legs falling straight and parallel, usually high-waisted, with pleats or darts; fluid or crisp.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser; pleats optional.
- **Shape**: Widen the thigh to the hem parallel to the crease: hem flat 28 to 36 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2). The side seam and the inseam leave the hip and crotch and run parallel to the crease line to the hem; the crotch extension is kept or increased 1 cm; ease at the hip 6 to 10 cm total. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Move side and inseam lines out equally from the crease line at the knee and hem to the chosen widths. 3. Check the hem sweep > 1.2 m for stride comfort. 4. Add pleats at the front waist to take the extra width, or darts.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: weighted hem tape optional, blind hem 4 cm.
- **Fabrics**: wool crepe, viscose, linen, drapey crepe; heavy hand holds a wider silhouette.
- **Fit faults**: hem catches under the shoe -> shorten 1 to 2 cm or measure with the shoe; legs swing -> check grain on the crease.
- **Variants**: #palazzo-trousers, #flared-trousers, #culottes.
- **CLO3D**: lightweight fabric preset, 5 mm particles; hem touches the floor.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
