# Jeans (Five-Pocket) {#jeans}

- **Aliases**: denim jeans, blue jeans, five-pocket trousers, dungaree jeans; fr: jean, jean cinq poches
- **Family**: trousers, denim workwear
- **Recognize**: curved back yoke, two back patch pockets, two front scoop pockets and a coin pocket, J-shaped topstitching at the fly, rivets, belt loops, flat-felled seams.
- **Pieces**: front leg x2, back leg x2, back yoke x2, back patch pocket x2, front pocket bag x2, front pocket facing x2, coin pocket x1, fly facing x1, fly shield x1, waistband x1 (or x2 with CB seam), belt loops x5 to x7.
- **Shape**: Trouser block adapted with hip ease 3 to 5 cm for rigid denim and 0 to -3 cm for stretch denim (stretch 15 to 30 percent); rise low (front 20 to 23 cm), mid (24 to 26), high (27 to 31). Back yoke: the back waist darts are pivoted into the yoke seam, which runs straight or curved from the side seam to CB with a CB depth 6 to 8 cm and a side-seam depth 3.5 to 5 cm; the intake becomes a small concave/convex shape (1.5 to 3 cm); the lower back has no dart. Back patch pockets 13 to 15 cm wide, 14 to 16 cm tall, top edge 5 to 8 cm below the yoke seam, tilted to the seat (CB side edge 5 to 6 cm from CB at the top). Front scoop: opening starts at the waist 6 to 8 cm from the side seam and ends on the side seam 14 to 17 cm below the waist, scoop depth 4 to 6 cm into the front, bag 20 to 25 cm deep; facing on the opening. Coin pocket on the right front 6 to 8 cm wide, 6 to 9 deep, 1.5 to 2 cm below the waistband. Fly: J stitch line 3.2 to 3.5 cm from CF, fly length 14 to 16 (women) or 16 to 18 cm (men), extension 3.5 to 4 cm, shield 6 to 8 cm wide. Source: techniques sheet B3; not a drawn pattern read by the author.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with the chosen rise and ease. 2. Yoke: pivot back darts into the yoke seam. 3. Draft pockets, coin pocket, facing. 4. Fly extension and shield. 5. Belt loops: finished width 1.0 to 1.3 cm, cut 4 x finished width. 6. Waistband W + 1 + extension 3 to 4 + 2 SA.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pocket bags, coin pocket and facings. 2. Front pockets attached, topstitch opening. 3. Fly (zip, shield, J stitch). 4. Front rise to the fly. 5. Yoke to back legs (flat-felled), back pockets. 6. Back rise (flat-felled). 7. Side seams (flat-felled), inseams (flat-felled). 8. Belt loops, waistband. 9. Hem, rivets, bar tacks, button.
- **Finishes and notions**: flat-felled seams (SA 1.5 cm wide side, 1 to 1.5 narrow side, trim to 3 to 4 mm), twin topstitching 5 to 7 mm, rivets at pocket corners, bar tacks, jeans button, YKK zip 14 to 18 cm.
- **Fabrics**: denim 150 to 400 g/m2 (8 to 14 oz); stretch denim with 1 to 3 percent elastane; selvedge denim for outer seams.
- **Fit faults**: back yoke gapes at CB -> curve the yoke seam less, bring CB down 1 cm; pocket gape at the hip -> loosen the hip or widen the pocket bag; honeycomb creases are wash effects, not pattern faults.
- **Variants**: #classic-trouser, #denim-skirt, #chinos.
- **CLO3D**: denim preset, topstitch tool for the J and pockets, pocket bag on a lower layer.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B3 (typical); dimensions from trade practice.
- **Status**: practice
