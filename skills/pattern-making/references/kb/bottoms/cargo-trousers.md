# Cargo Trousers {#cargo-trousers}

- **Aliases**: combat trousers, utility trousers, cargo pants; fr: pantalon cargo, treillis
- **Family**: trousers, utility
- **Recognize**: large patch pockets with flaps and bellows on the thigh (one or two per leg), sometimes more at the calf; straight to wide leg, drawcord or elastic ankle; belt loops.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, waistband, patch pockets with bellows x2 (or x4), pocket gusset strips x2 (or x4), pocket flaps x4 (x8 cut with facing), front slant pockets bags optional, fly facing and shield.
- **Shape**: Base trouser block (#classic-trouser or #straight-trousers). Cargo pocket: rectangular body 14 to 18 cm wide and 16 to 20 cm deep (typical), a gusset strip (bellows) 3 to 4 cm wide along both sides and bottom (the strip is cut as one L- or U-shaped band, or three pieces), flap 1 cm wider than the pocket, depth 5 to 7 cm, straight or pointed bottom edge; placed on the outer thigh with the top edge 10 to 14 cm below the hip line, centred about 3 to 5 cm in from the side seam. Pocket bottom edge pleat folds inward at the bellows; the bellows band sews to the pocket body and to the leg. Dimensions typical, shape unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with ease +4 to 6 cm at hip. 2. Draw the cargo pocket pieces (body, bellows band, flap). 3. Position on the leg with the bellows seam allowance planned. 4. Pocket body width = flat width + 2 x gusset depth when the sides are pleated.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Make pockets and flaps flat before assembly. 2. Sew pockets to the legs. 3. Fly, crotch, seams, waistband. 4. Hem or cuff with drawcord.
- **Finishes and notions**: topstitching, snaps, hook-and-loop or buttons at the flaps, bar tacks at the pocket corners, drawcord at the hem optional.
- **Fabrics**: cotton twill, canvas, ripstop; heavy fabric needs a lower pocket bulk (taper the bellows).
- **Fit faults**: pockets sag -> position higher or reduce depth; flap does not close over the bulge -> widen the flap.
- **Variants**: #cargo-skirt, #cargo-shorts, #chinos, #jogger-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: pockets as separate pieces on higher layers with bellows folded via internal lines; 3 to 5 mm particles at the pocket.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; pocket sizes typical; not read from a drawn pattern.
- **Status**: unverified
