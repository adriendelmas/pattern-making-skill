# Tapered (Carrot) Trousers {#tapered-trousers}

- **Aliases**: carrot-fit trousers, carrot pants, peg-leg trousers, tapered pants; fr: pantalon carotte, pantalon fuselé
- **Family**: trousers, tapered
- **Recognize**: full at the hip and thigh, tapering to a narrow ankle; often pleated at the front waist.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser (usually with pleats).
- **Shape**: Add 2 to 4 cm to the hip and thigh, taper from the knee to a hem of 17 to 20 cm flat per leg (techniques sheet B2). Crotch depth often increased 1 to 2 cm; side seam and inseam draw straight lines from the thigh to the narrow hem. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with +2 to +4 cm ease at the hip. 2. Add pleats at the waist. 3. Draw straight lines from the thigh to the narrow hem. 4. Check the hem for the foot.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: hem 2 cm or an ankle band.
- **Fabrics**: cotton twill, linen, wool; drapey fabrics create a soft drape at the hip.
- **Fit faults**: legs balloon at the knee -> reduce knee; seat pulls -> add rise.
- **Variants**: #pleated-trousers, #slim-trousers, #jogger-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: 5 mm particles, check pleats opening.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
