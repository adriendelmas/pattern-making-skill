# Capri Pants {#capri-pants}

- **Aliases**: pedal pushers, cropped pants, clam diggers (calf-length, looser); fr: pantacourt, corsaire
- **Family**: trousers, cropped
- **Recognize**: fitted trousers ending at mid-calf; slit at the outer hem optional.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Shape**: Trouser block (slim) cut at the capri inseam length of 50 to 60 cm (techniques sheet B4) below the crotch; hem width slim (flat 16 to 18 cm) or straight; hem slit 5 to 8 cm at the outer seam optional. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Slim trouser draft. 2. Cut at calf. 3. Hem allowance 2 to 3 cm or hem facing.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: hem slit with bar tack, button at the hem optional.
- **Fabrics**: stretch cotton, linen, twill.
- **Fit faults**: hem cuts the calf -> lengthen or change the hem line to meet the calf narrowest.
- **Variants**: #cigarette-trousers, #bermuda-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B4.
- **Status**: unverified
