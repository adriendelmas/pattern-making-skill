# Slim Trousers {#slim-trousers}

- **Aliases**: skinny trousers (narrower), slim-fit trousers, slim chinos; fr: pantalon slim, pantalon fuselé
- **Family**: trousers, narrow
- **Recognize**: narrow thigh and narrow hem, close to the leg from hip to ankle; often stretch fabric.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Shape**: As #classic-trouser but with the thigh narrowed 1 to 2 cm per leg and the knee 2 to 3 cm; hem flat 16 to 19 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2). For non-stretch fabrics the hem must still pass the foot (>= 17 cm flat for a 38 EU foot, check with a shoe). Crotch extensions unchanged. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Reduce the knee and hem widths symmetrically about the crease line. 3. Reduce the thigh by taking from the inseam. 4. Check the hem opening with the foot.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser; for stretch fabric use a stretch stitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip fly, back slit hem or a zip at the ankle if no stretch, hem 2 cm.
- **Fabrics**: stretch twill, stretch wool, denim; non-stretch needs ankle zips or a split hem.
- **Fit faults**: thigh pulls -> add 1 cm at the thigh; hem too tight over the foot -> widen the hem 1 to 2 cm or add a vent.
- **Variants**: #cigarette-trousers, #tapered-trousers, #straight-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: stretch preset; shrink horizontally to simulate negative ease if the fabric stretches.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
