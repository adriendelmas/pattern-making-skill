# Gauchos {#gauchos}

- **Aliases**: gaucho pants, gaucho trousers; fr: pantalon gaucho
- **Family**: trousers, calf-length wide
- **Recognize**: wide flared legs ending at mid-calf, wider than culottes at the hem, high waist; derived from South American gaucho riding wear.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, waistband, zip/fly.
- **Shape**: Trouser block shortened to mid-calf (about 40 to 55 cm from the crotch line, typical), side seam and inseam flaring; hem flat 30 to 40 cm per leg (typical, between #culottes and #palazzo-trousers); waist raised. Not found in a drawn pattern; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. High-waisted block. 2. Cut to mid-calf. 3. Add flare by slash and spread. 4. Hem curve square to the seams.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: topstitched hem, zip or buttons.
- **Fabrics**: suede-look cottons, twill, wool, crepe.
- **Fit faults**: hem flares unevenly -> relevel after hanging and check the grain on the crease; legs swing apart -> reduce the flare or add hem weight; crotch binds -> add rise.
- **Variants**: #culottes, #flared-trousers, #palazzo-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: as #culottes.
- **Confidence**: from memory; no source.
- **Status**: unverified
