# Harem Trousers {#harem-trousers}

- **Aliases**: harem pants, drop-crotch trousers, sarouel pants, hammer pants; fr: sarouel, pantalon harem
- **Family**: trousers, drop crotch
- **Recognize**: very low crotch (a deep gusset or pouch), baggy thigh, narrowing to gathered cuffs at the ankle; elastic waist or waistband; no fly.
- **Pieces**: leg panels x2 (front and back combined), or front x2 + back x2, gusset/crotch panel x1 (diamond) optional, waistband or casing x1, ankle cuffs x2.
- **Shape**: Tutorials show a harem made by tracing a jeans or trouser block, omitting the gusset, and extending the waist upward by 15 to 20 cm (and the leg length 2.5 cm hem). The crotch drops a couple of cm to 15 cm below the normal level when the crotch curve is lowered or when a rectangular or diamond gusset (width 15 to 20 cm) is inserted between the two legs. Width per panel from a published free pattern: (hip + 30 cm)/4 per panel (Simply Sewing). Front and back crotch curves are joined to a gusset, or one leg is slid inside the other and the crotch seam closes all round. Gusset geometry and rise-extension maths not found; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block widened at hip (+ 20 to 40 cm total). 2. Lower the crotch point 8 to 20 cm and extend the inseam correspondingly; round the crotch curve. 3. Taper from the thigh to a narrow ankle (cuff 18 to 24 cm, gathered). 4. Waist raised for an elastic casing. All values typical; make a toile.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Inseams (leave the gusset area open). 2. Insert the gusset or nest one leg inside the other and sew the crotch closed. 3. Side seams. 4. Waist casing and elastic. 5. Cuffs.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic waist 3 to 4 cm, cuffs with elastic or bands, topstitched seams.
- **Fabrics**: viscose, cotton, jersey, linen; drape determines the pouch shape.
- **Fit faults**: crotch too low to walk -> raise 3 to 5 cm; baggy knees -> shorten; cuffs balloon -> shrink cuff.
- **Variants**: #jogger-trousers, #tapered-trousers, #culottes.
- **CLO3D**: gathers at the cuffs via gather ratio; drop crotch can pinch (fine mesh at the crotch).
- **Confidence**: tutorials checked online (template method and width formula); gusset shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
