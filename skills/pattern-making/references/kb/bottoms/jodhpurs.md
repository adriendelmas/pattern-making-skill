# Jodhpurs and Riding Breeches {#jodhpurs}

- **Aliases**: breeches, jods, riding pants; fr: culotte de cheval, jodhpur
- **Family**: trousers, equestrian
- **Recognize**: full over the hip and thigh (historic) or fitted stretch; fitted from the knee to the ankle (jodhpurs end at the ankle, breeches below the knee); inner knee or full-seat patches; front pockets; back seat seam.
- **Pieces**: front x2, back x2, knee patches x2 (or inner-leg patches), seat patch x1 (optional), waistband x1, pocket bags, fly facing and shield, calf closure (buttons or elastic) for breeches.
- **Shape**: A patent for equestrian breeches describes four panels joined at a crotch seam, an outseam and an inseam; the inseam rubs the saddle so reinforcement is placed there; patches are separate pieces (knee patches on the inner sides of the knees, or a full-seat patch). A museum record of 1925 to 1940 breeches describes legs rounded out very full at the hips and fitted tightly below the knee, with a buckskin patch hand-stitched to the inside of each leg from the knee to within 8 cm of the hem. A historic drafting text (Cutters' Practical Guide, reproduced by Don McCunn) builds jodhpurs from waist, seat, knee, calf, full length and side seam measurements and a bottom width at taste. Outline: thigh flare drawn out from the hip to the knee level, then a straight narrow lower leg to the ankle. Thigh flare amount not found; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block, rise as measured plus 1 cm. 2. Widen the thigh (historic) by 4 to 8 cm per leg between the hip and knee, tapering to the knee. 3. Fit the lower leg to the knee and calf measurements (stretch fabrics reduce this). 4. Knee patch: trace between two lines on the front inner knee, add 1 cm top and bottom, cut a mirrored pair. 5. Calf closure for breeches: 10 to 15 cm placket with buttons, or elastic. All values typical.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Patches on the legs. 2. Pockets and fly. 3. Crotch and seat seams. 4. Side seams, inseams. 5. Calf closure and cuff. 6. Waistband.
- **Finishes and notions**: suede or silicone patches, topstitching, zip fly, belt loops, ankle strap or stirrup for jodhpurs.
- **Fabrics**: stretch twill or cotton drill (modern), wool twill (historic), suede or leather patches.
- **Fit faults**: knee patches bunch -> match the knee curve; bag at the thigh -> reduce the flare; crotch pulls on the saddle -> lengthen the rise.
- **Variants**: #classic-trouser, #leggings, #stirrup-pants.
- **CLO3D**: patches as separate pieces on the leg; stretch fabric preset.
- **Confidence**: the patent and museum descriptions verified online; flare dimensions unknown; no drawn pattern read.
- **Status**: unverified
