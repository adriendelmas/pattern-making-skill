# Bootcut Trousers {#bootcut-trousers}

- **Aliases**: boot-cut, boot-leg trousers; fr: pantalon bootcut
- **Family**: trousers, slight flare
- **Recognize**: slim thigh and knee with a mild flare from the knee to a hem wide enough to pass over a boot.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Shape**: Hem flat 23 to 26 cm per leg (techniques sheet B2); knee kept as slim; flare starts at the knee, side seam and inseam straight lines from the knee to the wider hem. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Slim trouser draft. 2. Hem width +3 to +6 cm total from the slim hem. 3. Straight line knee to hem.
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: hem 3 to 4 cm; long hem to cover the boot heel.
- **Fabrics**: stretch denim, twill, ponte.
- **Fit faults**: hem drags -> shorten; flare looks wide -> reduce the hem.
- **Variants**: #flared-trousers, #slim-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2.
- **Status**: unverified
