# Cigarette Trousers {#cigarette-trousers}

- **Aliases**: ankle pants, cropped slim pants, 7/8 trousers; fr: pantalon cigarette
- **Family**: trousers, narrow, ankle-length
- **Recognize**: narrow straight or slightly tapered leg ending above the ankle bone.
- **Pieces**: as #classic-trouser; hem facing or slit optional.
- **Shape**: Hem flat 16 to 18 cm per leg, hem 5 to 8 cm above the ankle bone (7/8 length), per the techniques sheet B2; legs straight and narrow from knee to hem. Unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Slim trousers. 2. Shorten the hem to 7/8. 3. Check the hem opening for the heel (cut a small slit at the hem outer seam if needed).
- **Sewing order**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Finishes and notions**: 2 cm hem, ankle slit optional.
- **Fabrics**: stretch wool, crepe, twill.
- **Fit faults**: hem too short when sitting -> lengthen 1 cm; thighs pull -> widen.
- **Variants**: #slim-trousers, #tapered-trousers, #capri-pants.
- **CLO3D**: as #classic-trouser.
- **Confidence**: techniques sheet B2.
- **Status**: unverified
