# Turban / Headwrap {#turban-headwrap}

- **Aliases**: head wrap, hair wrap, twist turban, wrap scarf; fr: turban, foulard de tete
- **Family**: hat, soft, wrapped
- **Recognize**: fabric wrapped round the head, with twists, folds or a knot; pre-made versions have a sewn band and knot.
- **Pieces**: for a tied wrap: one rectangle (x1). For a pre-formed turban: band (x1 head circumference), twist section (x1), knot section (x1), optional inner liner (x1).
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. A tied wrap is a rectangle of about 50 to 80 wide by 150 to 200 long [T, unverified]. A pre-formed twist turban is a band of H x 0.9 around and 100 to 140 wide (technique sheet part 7, "turban / wide band") with a centre knot piece 60 x 80, the band sewn into a ring and twisted.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure H. 2. Band length H x 0.9 (stretch). 3. Width 100 to 140. 4. Knot piece 60 x 80. 5. SA 8.
- **Sewing order**: seam the band into a ring, twist, sew the knot piece.
- **Finishes and notions**: stretch seams, interlining optional.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, cotton, silk, viscose, linen.
- **Fit faults**: slips (add elastic), twist uneven.
- **Variants**: #headband, #scrunchie, #snood.
- **CLO3D**: pre-formed version only; model the twist by a draped rectangle.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T]; tied-wrap dimensions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
