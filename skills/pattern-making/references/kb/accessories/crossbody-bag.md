# Crossbody Bag {#crossbody-bag}

- **Aliases**: cross-body, sling bag, small shoulder bag, camera bag; fr: sac bandouliere
- **Family**: bag, small, long strap
- **Recognize**: small bag on a long thin strap, worn across the body; usually a flat envelope with a zipper or flap.
- **Pieces**: front and back (x2), gusset (optional, x1), flap or zipper, strap (x1), lining (x2).
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Technique sheet 3.3 [T]: crossbody strap total length 1100 to 1400, adjustable by 250; drop 500 to 600 from the strap top to the bag top. Bag body: flat rectangle or small gusset (D 30 to 60), flap one third to a half of the front.
- **Drafting**: 1. Rectangles W + 20 x Ht + 20. 2. Strap length = drop x 2 + bag width ± adjuster. 3. Strap width 15 to 30 for a small bag. 4. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: as #tote-bag with the strap in the side seams or on the gusset.
- **Finishes and notions**: swivel clips, D-rings, zipper, magnetic snap.
- **Fabrics**: leather, nylon, canvas, quilted fabric.
- **Fit faults**: strap too short or too long (drop), bag flips (strap not at the bag's side seams).
- **Variants**: #messenger-bag, #clutch, #bum-bag.
- **CLO3D**: strap as a separate piece, place on the avatar.
- **Confidence**: strap lengths technique sheet [T]; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
