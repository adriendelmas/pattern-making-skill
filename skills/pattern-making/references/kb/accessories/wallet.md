# Wallet {#wallet}

- **Aliases**: bifold wallet, card holder, purse (women's), billfold; fr: portefeuille, porte-cartes
- **Family**: small leather goods
- **Recognize**: flat folding piece with card slots, a bill compartment, and (optional) a zip coin pocket.
- **Pieces**: outer (x1), inner card slot panels (x2 to x6 strips), bill panel (x1), lining optional, coin pocket (x1).
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Typical bifold (unverified): closed 110 x 90, open 220 x 90; card slots are strips about 90 wide x 55 to 70 high stacked with each lower strip showing 10 to 12 of the one beneath; strips sewn to the inner panel on the sides and bottom.
- **Drafting**: 1. Open outer rectangle with rounded corners. 2. Card slots stacked at 10 to 12 reveal. 3. Bill compartment. 4. SA 3 to 6 (leather, edges finished).
- **Sewing order**: slots to the inner panel, join the panels, edge finish.
- **Finishes and notions**: edge paint or burnish, snap, zip.
- **Fabrics**: thin leather (0.8 to 1.2 mm), PU, canvas with interfacing.
- **Fit faults**: too thick at the fold (skive the leather), cards too tight.
- **Variants**: #clutch, #boxed-corner-pouch.
- **CLO3D**: hardly relevant.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
