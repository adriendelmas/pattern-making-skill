# Ski Glove {#ski-glove}

- **Aliases**: snow glove, insulated glove, gauntlet ski glove; fr: gant de ski
- **Family**: glove, sport, insulated, waterproof
- **Recognize**: bulky, pre-curved fingers, a long cuff that goes over the jacket sleeve, wrist strap, nose wipe on the thumb, reinforced palm and fingers.
- **Pieces**: shell back and palm (x2 each per pair), shell thumb, fourchette arms (often present for finger depth), waterproof insert (membrane glove, same patterns reduced), insulation layer, lining, gauntlet cuff piece, strap, pull-on loop, palm reinforcement.
- **Shape**: unverified. Construction logic: a layered glove, each layer a #two-piece-trank or #trank-glove with pre-curved fingers (the palm piece finger axes are drawn with a gentle arc, typical total bend 20 to 30 degrees, confirm on a toile) instead of straight ones; cuff is a #gauntlet. The insert is a separate waterproof glove, slightly smaller, bonded or loose; shell and insert are joined at the cuff and fingertips.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft the trank per #trank-glove with ease G + 15 to 25 for the insulated layers. 2. Reduce the lining and the insert by 1.5 to 2.5 mm at the sides and 3 mm at the tips. 3. Gauntlet cuff per #gauntlet with a closure (elastic + strap). 4. Seams sealed or taped for waterproofness.
- **Sewing order**: shell, insert, lining, assembly at the cuff, tip tacking.
- **Finishes and notions**: seam tape, gauntlet cinch, wrist leash, nose wipe.
- **Fabrics**: leather palm, nylon shell, breathable membrane, fleece or synthetic insulation.
- **Fit faults**: fingers cold -> too tight; shell and insert mismatch -> insert too big; cuff gaps -> strap.
- **Variants**: #gauntlet, #mitten-separate-thumb (ski mitten), #lobster-mitt.
- **CLO3D**: multi-layer simulation is slow; model one layer.
- **Confidence**: from memory; no verified outline.
- **Status**: unverified
