# Lobster Mitt {#lobster-mitt}

- **Aliases**: lobster claw glove, three-finger glove, split mitten; fr: gant lobster, moufle a deux doigts
- **Family**: glove, hybrid mitten
- **Recognize**: three compartments: a thumb, one for the index and middle fingers, one for the ring and little fingers.
- **Pieces**: palm and back (as a #trank-glove with two finger groups), thumb, one gusset or fourchette arms between the two finger groups, lining.
- **Shape**: unverified. Construction logic: a #mitten-gusset-thumb draft with a single central slit between two finger groups: two parallel straight "fingers", each about twice a finger width, rounded tips, a short slit between them ending at a crotch point. Thumb as a separate piece set into a hole, or a gusset thumb.
- **Drafting**: 1. Widths: each group = the sum of its two finger girths/2 + ease. 2. One slit between the groups at the web height of the middle-ring crease. 3. Thumb per #mitten-separate-thumb.
- **Sewing order**: close the slit with a gusset or fourchette, sew the thumb, close the sides and tips.
- **Finishes and notions**: as mittens; seam allowance 6 mm [T].
- **Fabrics**: fleece, wool, insulated nylon.
- **Fit faults**: finger groups pinched -> widen; slit too deep -> web stress.
- **Variants**: #mitten-separate-thumb, #ski-glove.
- **CLO3D**: as a mitten with one slit.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
