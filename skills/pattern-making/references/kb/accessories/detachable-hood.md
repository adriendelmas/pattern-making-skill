# Detachable Hood {#detachable-hood}

- **Aliases**: removable hood, zip-in hood, button-on hood; fr: capuche amovible
- **Family**: hat, accessory for outerwear
- **Recognize**: a hood with its own neck edge fitted with a zipper, snaps or buttons that attaches to the neckline of a coat or jacket.
- **Pieces**: hood side (x2 mirror, with centre back and top seam, or x1 on the fold at CB), centre gusset (x1 strip, optional), facing at the front edge (x2) or a front band, attachment tape (x1 zipper or snap tape), drawcord (x1) in a front casing, lining (x2 sides) optional.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic (generic hood): each side is a roughly rectangular piece with a curved neck edge (the neckline of the coat plus the attachment overlap), a vertical centre back line, a curved top, and a front edge that frames the face; the two sides sew along the centre back and over the top. Typical size: depth (front to back) 30 to 34, height from neck to crown 36 to 40 [T, unverified]; neck edge length = the length of the garment neckline (collar stand edge) minus 0 to 5 mm. The neck edge sews to the attachment tape, whose mating tape is on the garment.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure the neck edge of the garment (CB to CF, doubled). 2. Draw the hood side with that neck edge length. 3. Height and depth from the head. 4. Casing at the front edge 12 to 15 wide for the drawcord. 5. Zipper or snap tape at the neck edge. 6. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: sides at CB and top, facing and drawcord casing, tape at the neck edge, lining.
- **Finishes and notions**: zipper (continuous) or snaps every 60 to 80, drawcord with stoppers.
- **Fabrics**: same shell as the garment, lining in a lighter fabric.
- **Fit faults**: hood pulls back the coat (neck edge too short), hood gapes (neck edge too long).
- **Variants**: #balaclava, #snood.
- **CLO3D**: sew the neck edge to the garment neckline with segment sewing; lining optional.

- **Status**: unverified
