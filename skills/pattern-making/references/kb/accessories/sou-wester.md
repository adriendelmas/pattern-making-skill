# Sou'wester {#sou-wester}

- **Aliases**: souwester, rain hat, oilskin hat, fisherman's rain hat; fr: suroit
- **Family**: hat, weatherproof, brimmed
- **Recognize**: rounded crown, a brim that is much deeper at the back (neck flap) than at the front, often with ear flaps or a chin strap, waterproof coated cotton or oilskin.
- **Pieces**: crown (x4 to x6 panels or a pull-shaped piece), brim (x1 or x2, ring with a wider back), lining optional, chin tie (x2), optional ear flaps.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic: the brim is a ring around the head opening whose width varies round the head, from a narrow front (about 40 to 60, unverified) to a wide nape flap (about 100 to 150, unverified); the crown follows the head. The brim sews to the crown at the head line, and the nape edge tilts down to shed water. Seams sewn, then taped or sealed.
- **Drafting**: 1. Head opening oval. 2. Brim: ring with variable width, symmetrical about the centre back. 3. Crown panels as a dome (#baseball-cap-6-panel). 4. Chin tie 400 x 15 [T, unverified]. 5. Seal seams.
- **Sewing order**: crown seams, brim to crown, hem or bind the brim edge, chin ties.
- **Finishes and notions**: seam tape or wax, binding, grommets, tie.
- **Fabrics**: waxed cotton, PVC-coated cotton, PU-coated nylon.
- **Fit faults**: water runs down the neck (brim too short at the back), hat blows off (chin tie).
- **Variants**: #bucket-hat, #wide-brim-sun-hat.
- **CLO3D**: a rigid brim fabric, coated cotton preset.
- **Confidence**: from memory; no verified outline.
- **Status**: unverified
