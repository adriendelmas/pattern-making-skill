# Backpack {#backpack}

- **Aliases**: rucksack, daypack, knapsack; fr: sac a dos
- **Family**: bag, structured, shoulder straps
- **Recognize**: two shoulder straps, a back panel, a top flap or zipper, side walls forming the depth, a top handle.
- **Pieces**: front panel (x1), back panel (x1 with padding), gusset (x1 U-strip, or side x2 + bottom x1), bottom panel (x1), top flap or zip (x1), shoulder straps (x2 padded), top handle (x1), pockets (x1 to x3), lining, foam.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern; derived geometry for the rectangles. Typical daypack (unverified): front/back 300 to 340 wide x 420 to 450 high, depth D 120 to 160 [T]. The gusset (side wall + bottom) follows the same path rule as #gusseted-tote (length = 2 x Ht + W for a U with square corners). Shoulder strap: width 50 to 60, length 700 to 900 including adjuster, padded zone 300 to 350 (taper at the top where it joins the back panel at the shoulder line, curved to follow the shoulder), attached at the top of the back panel and at the bottom corners (or at the bottom panel). Back panel is padded with foam 10 to 15 and mesh. Top handle 150 x 40.
- **Drafting**: 1. Front/back rectangles with rounded lower corners. 2. Gusset strip D wide. 3. Bottom panel (if separate) W x D. 4. Strap shaped with a curve of about 60 mm at the top. 5. Pockets sewn on the front before assembly. 6. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pockets and panels. 2. Straps (padding, topstitch) and attachment stitched with box-X on the back panel. 3. Gusset to front and back. 4. Lining. 5. Turn, closure.
- **Finishes and notions**: webbing, buckles, tri-glides, zippers, foam, mesh, bar tacks.
- **Fabrics**: nylon, Cordura, canvas, leather; seam sealing for waterproofness.
- **Fit faults**: straps dig into the shoulders (too narrow or no curve), bag sags (no stiffener), too wide at the back.
- **Variants**: #drawstring-bag, #messenger-bag, #bum-bag.
- **CLO3D**: panels and gusset with stiff fabric; straps as separate strap pieces; place on the avatar with the straps over the shoulders; unverified.
- **Confidence**: geometry exact; sizes and strap shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
