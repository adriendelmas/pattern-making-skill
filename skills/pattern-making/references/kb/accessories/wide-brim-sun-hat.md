# Wide-Brim Sun Hat {#wide-brim-sun-hat}

- **Aliases**: floppy hat, garden hat, beach hat, Panama (straw); fr: capeline, chapeau de soleil
- **Family**: hat, brimmed, soft or wired
- **Recognize**: low rounded or flat crown with a brim much wider than the crown, often drooping or wired, sometimes with a chin cord.
- **Pieces**: crown top (x1 or crown side + top), crown side (x1), brim (x2 top/under, ring or sectors), interfacing (x1 for the brim), wire (x1), sweatband, chin cord.
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Crown as in #bucket-hat. Brim: flat ring, inner radius Cb/(2 pi) and outer radius inner + B, with B = 80 to 120 (wide) [T, unverified]. A drooping brim at angle delta below horizontal is a ring sector of angle 360 x cos(delta) with inner radius Cb/(2 pi cos(delta)); delta 15 degrees gives 347.7 degrees, 25 degrees gives 326.3 degrees. Wired edge: a pocket at the outer edge for millinery wire; the two brim layers sew right sides together along the outer edge. A brim cut as 2 to 4 sectors avoids a long seam but sectors must be sewn edge to edge.
- **Drafting**: 1. Crown from H + 10. 2. Brim width and droop angle. 3. Ring or sector, interfaced. 4. Wire pocket 10 from the edge. 5. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: crown, brim layers right sides together, turn, topstitch rows or wire, attach to crown, band or chin cord.
- **Finishes and notions**: wire, interfacing, chin cord or ties, ribbon band.
- **Fabrics**: straw, cotton, linen, canvas with interfacing; wool for winter.
- **Fit faults**: brim flops (add interfacing or wire), crown sits high (too tall).
- **Variants**: #bucket-hat, #cloche, #sou-wester.
- **CLO3D**: brim fabric with high bending stiffness; wire effect by locally stiff fabric.
- **Confidence**: geometry from technique sheet 2.1; brim widths unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
