# Cycling and Fingerless Glove {#cycling-fingerless-glove}

- **Aliases**: bike glove, half-finger glove, fingerless glove, mitts; fr: gant de cyclisme, mitaine
- **Family**: glove, sport, cut-off fingers
- **Recognize**: fingers end at the first knuckle (half-finger) or at the base crease (fingerless), padded palm, mesh or Lycra back, hook-and-loop wrist closure, a terry thumb patch on the back of the thumb.
- **Pieces**: palm (x1 per hand), back (x1), palm pad (gel or foam, 1 to 3 pieces), thumb (terry) patch, finger tabs or stubs, wrist strap with hook-and-loop, pull tabs, binding at the finger ends.
- **Shape**: unverified. Construction logic: the #trank-glove draft cut off at the finger base crease minus 5 mm (fingerless) or at the proximal joint (half-finger), with a hem of 10 mm (technique sheet 1.12). Seams are kept off the pressure zones: palm panel in one piece, seams moved to the sides, thumb hole an oval 20 x 35 at the thumb web height. The back panel is stretch fabric across the knuckles. Padding sits under the heel of the palm; its outline is brand-specific (unverified).
- **Drafting**: 1. Tube width = G + 10, length = wrist crease to finger base crease - 5 + hem. 2. Thumb hole oval 20 x 35. 3. Hem top 10 mm. 4. Pad pocket stitched on the palm piece. 5. Wrist strap 25 x 100 with hook-and-loop.
- **Sewing order**: back and palm assembly, pads, thumb patch, side seams, finger-end binding, wrist closure.
- **Finishes and notions**: bound edges, flatlock or overlocked seams, hook-and-loop.
- **Fabrics**: synthetic leather or goatskin palm; mesh and stretch back; terry for the thumb.
- **Fit faults**: bunching in the palm -> reduce the palm width, pad too thick; finger-end gap -> shorten.
- **Variants**: #driving-glove, #mitten-separate-thumb.
- **CLO3D**: palm and back as two fabrics, stretch preset for the back, pad as an extra layer piece.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet 1.12 [T]; pad geometry unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
