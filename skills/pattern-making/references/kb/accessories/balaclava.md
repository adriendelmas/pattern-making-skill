# Balaclava {#balaclava}

- **Aliases**: ski mask, head sock, helmet liner, face tube; fr: cagoule, passe-montagne
- **Family**: hat, knit or fleece, face covering
- **Recognize**: covers head, neck and most of the face, with an opening for the eyes or the whole face; hood shape with a neck tube.
- **Pieces**: head-and-face pieces (x2 mirror halves joined at the centre back and over the top, or x1 folded), neck tube (x1 rectangle), optional face-opening band (x1), optional mouth vent.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic: a close-fitting hood (centre-back seam and top seam, like a #detachable-hood with negative ease) with the front edge cut back to form the face opening; below it a neck tube whose circumference is neck girth plus ease, sewn to the hood's lower edge. The face opening is finished by a band (ribbing or elastic) 20 to 30 wide, length about 0.85 to 0.9 x the opening length.
- **Drafting**: 1. Head girth H x 0.9 to 0.95 (stretch). 2. Hood height = crown to chin + 20. 3. Face opening: oval cut at the front, 160 to 180 long x 90 to 110 wide for a full face opening [T, unverified]. 4. Neck tube = neck girth + 30 around, 100 to 150 long. 5. SA 8 stretch.
- **Sewing order**: centre back seam, top seam, neck tube, bind the face opening.
- **Finishes and notions**: stretch seams, rib band, flat seams.
- **Fabrics**: fleece, merino, polyester knit, 40 to 60 % stretch.
- **Fit faults**: the opening gapes (band too long), neck rides up (tube too short).
- **Variants**: #beanie, #detachable-hood, #snood.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; mesh 5 mm.
- **Confidence**: from memory; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
