# Scrunchie {#scrunchie}

- **Aliases**: hair tie, hair scrunchy, gathered hair elastic; fr: chouchou
- **Family**: hat, accessory, gathered tube
- **Recognize**: a gathered fabric tube around an elastic ring.
- **Pieces**: fabric strip (x1), elastic (x1, ring).
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Strip length L, width Wd; folded lengthwise right sides together, sewn into a tube along the long edge, turned, then the elastic is threaded and the ends of the tube joined. Gather ratio = L / (relaxed elastic length). Typical (unverified): strip 10 to 12 x 40 to 55, elastic 15 to 22 relaxed, flat tube width 4 to 6 after seaming.
- **Drafting**: 1. Cut strip. 2. Sew the long edge with 1 cm SA. 3. Turn. 4. Thread the elastic, tie it. 5. Sew the two tube ends right sides together and close the gap by hand.
- **Sewing order**: as above.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 6 mm or 10 mm, safety pin to thread.
- **Fabrics**: velvet, satin, cotton, jersey.
- **Fit faults**: too tight or loose -> elastic length; too small -> longer strip.
- **Variants**: #headband.
- **CLO3D**: not worth simulating.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical sizes unverified.
