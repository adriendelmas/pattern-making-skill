# Fedora and Trilby {#fedora-trilby}

- **Aliases**: felt hat, snap-brim hat, Homburg (stiffer, turned-up brim, related); fr: feutre, chapeau borsalino (brand name)
- **Family**: hat, structured, brimmed, blocked
- **Recognize**: crown with a lengthwise centre crease and two side pinches (fedora) or a shorter crown and narrow brim snapped down in front and up at the back (trilby); ribbon band with a bow at the side; brim edge with a bound or raw rolled edge.
- **Pieces**: crown tip (x1 oval, for the sewn version), crown side (x1 band, for the sewn version), brim (x1 ring), ribbon (x1 grosgrain), sweatband (x1), lining (x1), brim wire (x1) or stiffener.
- **Shape**: derived geometry for the sewn fabric version; felt hats are formed by blocking and steaming over a wooden block, not by pattern (the felt shaping is not reproducible by cutting or by simulation). Head opening: an oval, long axis 2a, short axis 2b = 0.82 x 2a; with H = 580, a = 101 and b = 83 (Ramanujan perimeter). Crown tip: the same oval; crown side height 105 to 120 (fedora), shorter for a trilby [T]. The crown side is a band of length H + 10 SA, closed at the back seam, sewn to the tip along its oval; the pinch is formed by creasing (blocking) or by darts. Brim: a flat oval ring whose inner oval equals the crown base, width 55 to 65 (fedora), 40 to 50 for a trilby [T], wired at the edge; front 60, back 55. Ribbon grosgrain 25 to 35 wide x (H + 30). Source: technique sheet 2.5; trilby proportions from common usage, unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Oval opening with a = H/5.73, b = 0.82 a. 2. Crown tip = same oval + 10 ease at the base. 3. Brim ring, inner oval = crown base, width as above, with front/back difference. 4. Ribbon and sweatband strips. 5. SA 10 (6 on the brim edge).
- **Sewing order**: 1. Crown side seam. 2. Side to tip. 3. Brim ring seam, wire in the edge. 4. Brim to crown. 5. Sweatband, lining, ribbon with bow.
- **Finishes and notions**: grosgrain ribbon, leather sweatband, brim wire (millinery wire), edge binding.
- **Fabrics**: fur felt, wool felt, straw, cotton twill for a sewn version; felt needs steam and block.
- **Fit faults**: brim sags (wire or stiffener missing), oval too round (head is an oval), crown too tall.
- **Variants**: #top-hat, #wide-brim-sun-hat.
- **CLO3D**: model the crown as a single deformed cylinder; the felt shaping is not reproducible by simulation; use a stiff fabric with high bending.
- **Confidence**: oval geometry verified by calculation; proportions technique sheet [T]; shape of felt hats unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
