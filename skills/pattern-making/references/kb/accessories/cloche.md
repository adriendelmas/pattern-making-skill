# Cloche {#cloche}

- **Aliases**: bell hat, 1920s hat, helmet hat; fr: cloche
- **Family**: hat, close-fitting, brimmed
- **Recognize**: bell-shaped crown that comes down to the eyebrows and the nape, small brim or none, close to the head.
- **Pieces**: crown panels (x4 to x6 gores) or a pull-shaped felt body, brim (x1 to x2 pieces), lining, band.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic: crown gores that follow the head sphere (as #baseball-cap-6-panel) but extend to the lower brow line, cut from a shallow dome to a bell, with a narrow brim of 30 to 50 mm that points down (ring sector, see #bucket-hat). Felt cloches are blocked and steamed, not stitched.
- **Drafting**: 1. Gore base width (H + 10)/n. 2. Length from the crown top to the brow line along the surface about 0.5 x H + 20 (arc F-B). 3. Brim as a drooping ring sector. 4. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: sew gores, attach brim, line.
- **Finishes and notions**: petersham band, hat wire at the brim edge.
- **Fabrics**: felt, wool, straw braid, velvet.
- **Fit faults**: too deep at the nape -> shorten the back; brim too wide -> shorten.
- **Variants**: #wide-brim-sun-hat, #bucket-hat.
- **CLO3D**: dome plus drooping ring.
- **Confidence**: from memory; outline unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
