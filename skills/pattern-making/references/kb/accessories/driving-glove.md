# Driving Glove {#driving-glove}

- **Aliases**: motoring glove, keystone-thumb driving glove, open-back driving glove; fr: gant de conduite
- **Family**: glove, leather, close fit
- **Recognize**: wrist-length leather glove, snug, thin, outseam stitching visible on the outside, often a snap at the wrist and perforations or an open back with a strap over the knuckles; the thumb is a wing or keystone-shaped insert.
- **Pieces**: palm (x1 per hand), back (x1), thumb piece(s), fourchette arms (often 0 to 6), wrist tab with snap, knuckle strap if open back.
- **Shape**: unverified. Sources disagree about the keystone thumb: some describe it as a wing-like piece cut with the palm that opens the thumb, others as a reinforced inset thumb that is double sewn, so a drawn pattern must be read before building the thumb. Fingers are straight, parallel, cut at the palm-side length without extra back ease because the wearer's fingers are curved. The cuff ends at the wrist, straight.
- **Drafting**: 1. #trank-glove draft with small negative ease (G + 0 to 4). 2. Cuff straight, width = wrist girth + 25 (opening) or elastic or tab. 3. Open back: cut a window in the back panel across the knuckles, bind with leather, add a strap with a snap. 4. Snap tab on the palm side.
- **Sewing order**: back and palm panels, thumb, fingers with outseam, cuff, tab and snap, knuckle strap.
- **Finishes and notions**: outseam or prixseam; snap; perforation.
- **Fabrics**: lambskin, cabretta, peccary, goatskin 0.6 to 0.9 mm; thin leather.
- **Fit faults**: fingers too long when gripping a wheel -> palm-side lengths minus 3 mm; back wrinkles -> negative ease.
- **Variants**: #trank-glove, #cycling-fingerless-glove.
- **CLO3D**: leather preset, thickness 0.6 mm, 4 mm mesh.
- **Confidence**: from memory; construction of the thumb unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
