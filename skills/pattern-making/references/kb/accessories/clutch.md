# Clutch {#clutch}

- **Aliases**: envelope clutch, pouch clutch, zip clutch, evening bag; fr: pochette
- **Family**: bag, small, handheld
- **Recognize**: flat bag with no handles, held in the hand: an envelope with a triangular flap, or a rectangle with a top zipper, wristlet optional.
- **Pieces**: envelope: body (x1 outer, x1 lining) with a flap; zip pouch: front and back (x2 outer, x2 lining), zip (x1), wristlet strap (x1), card slots optional.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Envelope clutch: one long rectangle (about 3 times the finished height, unverified), folded: bottom third up to form the front, top third forming the flap with a pointed or rounded end; the sides sew. Zip pouch: two rectangles with the zip between the top edges, sides and bottom sewn; typical 250 x 150 closed [T].
- **Drafting**: 1. Finished size. 2. Add 10 SA. 3. Flap shaped by a curve or point. 4. Interfacing on the outer.
- **Sewing order**: interface, fold, sew sides, line, closure (snap, magnet, zipper).
- **Finishes and notions**: zipper, snap, magnet, wrist strap.
- **Fabrics**: satin, leather, brocade, canvas.
- **Fit faults**: flap does not close (width mismatch), bag too thick (reduce interfacing).
- **Variants**: #crossbody-bag, #wallet, #boxed-corner-pouch.
- **CLO3D**: flat shapes with an interfaced fabric.
- **Confidence**: geometry exact; proportions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
