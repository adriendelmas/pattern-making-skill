# Scarf {#scarf}

- **Aliases**: neckerchief, square scarf, long scarf, stole, pashmina; fr: echarpe, foulard, carre
- **Family**: accessory, rectangle
- **Recognize**: rectangular or square piece with a finished edge or fringe.
- **Pieces**: scarf (x1; or 2 layers), fringe (tied or knotted), optional lining.
- **Shape**: technique sheet part 6 [T]: neckerchief 600 x 600 folded on the diagonal; square scarf 900 x 900 or 1000 x 1000 (silk twill 14 to 16 momme, rolled hem 2 to 3); long scarf 300 x 1800 (wool, fringe 80); pashmina or stole 700 x 2000; triangle = a square cut on the diagonal. Edge finishes: rolled hem 2 mm, narrow hem 6 mm, fringe (tied or knotted), serger with wooly nylon.
- **Drafting**: cut the rectangle on the straight grain; add a hem of 6 or fringe length.
- **Sewing order**: finish the edges, join layers if lined.
- **Finishes and notions**: fringe, rolled hem, serger.
- **Fabrics**: silk, wool, cashmere, cotton, viscose.
- **Fit faults**: curl (stabiliser, 5 mm hem).
- **Variants**: #snood, #pocket-square, #cravat-ascot.
- **CLO3D**: rectangle with a low-weight fabric.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T].
- **Status**: practice
