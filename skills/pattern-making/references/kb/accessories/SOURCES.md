# Sources: accessories

Gloves:

1. The Shapes of Fabric, "Exploring how to make gloves from scratch", https://www.theshapesoffabric.com/2022/02/15/exploring-how-to-make-gloves-from-scratch/ : widths, back +4 / palm -4, finger widths, fourchettes 15 to 5, thumb hole, Bolton thumb drawn pattern, sewing order (drawn pattern for #trank-glove, #bolton-thumb, #fourchette, #knit-glove).
2. John Koch, "Anyone who sews can make gloves", Threads, Oct/Nov 2009 : terms (trank, gouch, tongue, fourchette, Bolton thumb), pair counts, thumb set-in, fourchette tips.
3. Royal Canadian Mounted Police, G.S. 1045-044, Gloves, Brown, Leather, Unlined, Male and Female (2016) : trank widths by size, table cut, stitching, hem, snap, finishing.
4. The Dreamstress, "Terminology: Fourchette, quirks and other glove terms", https://thedreamstress.com/2015/05/terminology-fourchette-quirks-and-other-glove-terms/
5. Yeovil's Virtual Museum, "Gloving glossary", https://www.yeovilhistory.info/gloving-glossary.htm : seam names, French thumb, Bolton variant definition.
6. Cornelia James, "Glove glossary" and "Glove length: button length" : button convention.
7. Vintage Fashion Guild, "Glove construction terms", https://forums.vintagefashionguild.org/threads/glove-construction-terms.63082/ : trank, fourchette, quirk, prixseam, pique, inseam definitions.
8. Kinco, "Work Glove Patterns" (could not be retrieved); trade summaries (Kinco, Pyramex, Palmflex, Northstar, MDS, Safeopedia) of clute cut, gunn cut, straight, wing and keystone thumbs, as relayed by a web search; sources conflict, no dimensions.
9. US patents on glove construction (for example US 2125673, US 2316575, US 10842208) : front and back thumb portions, English and French fourchettes; no dimensions.
10. Eunice Close, "How to Make Gloves" (1950); Isabel Ellis, "Glove Making": historical texts, not consulted directly.
