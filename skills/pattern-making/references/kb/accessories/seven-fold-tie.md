# Seven-Fold Tie {#seven-fold-tie}

- **Aliases**: sette pieghe, self-lined tie, unlined tie; fr: cravate sept plis
- **Family**: neckwear, handmade
- **Recognize**: no separate lining or interlining; silk folded in on itself so that several layers show in a cross-section; fuller, softer knot; hand made.
- **Pieces**: sources say one large piece of silk (a square of 90 cm or about one yard, folded seven times) plus tipping; hand-rolled fold. Cutting layout unverified. Published specs: finished length 146 to 152, blade width 8 to 10 cm, a proportion proposal of blade 7 front and 4 rear with a neck width of about 3 to 3.2 and a total of about 148 cm; Turnbull & Asser quote 0.77 m of silk per tie and a blade width of 9.5 cm.
- **Shape**: unverified. Sources agree: no interlining (the folded layers provide body), a very large silk area (about three times a conventional tie, or twice according to one maker), folds shaped by hand. The usual construction cuts a bias-oriented piece (or pieces) with the finished width as seven layers across; the exact cutting layout, fold sequence and joins vary by maker and are not given in the sources consulted.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take the profile of #necktie (length 146 to 152, blade 8 to 10 cm). 2. The cut piece must be much wider than the finished width, because seven layers have to fit in the blade. 3. Rather than guess, make the cutting width from a toile of the finished folds. 4. Seam allowance for any join 6 to 10 mm.
- **Sewing order**: unverified; typically sew the pieces on the bias, fold in on themselves, press, close the fold with a hand slip stitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: self-tipping, hand slip stitch, keeper.
- **Fabrics**: silk of about 30 to 36 momme (one source), mid-weight; fabric that holds a fold.
- **Fit faults**: bulky knot (too many layers in the neck section), twist.
- **Variants**: #necktie.
- **CLO3D**: not practical; model as a #necktie.
- **Confidence**: from marketing and retail sources only (Turnbull & Asser, Robert Talbott, pattern sellers); layout unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
