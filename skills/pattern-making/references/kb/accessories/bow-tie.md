# Bow Tie {#bow-tie}

- **Aliases**: butterfly, batwing, diamond point, self-tie bow tie, pre-tied bow tie; fr: noeud papillon
- **Family**: neckwear
- **Recognize**: a bow-shaped knot at the neck; pre-tied versions have a fixed shaped bow and a neckband with a hook and slider, self-tie versions are a strip that the wearer ties.
- **Pieces**: pre-tied: bow (x1 folded rectangle), knot cover (x1 strip), neckband (x1), interlining (x1). Self-tie: one strip with flared ends.
- **Shape**: technique sheet 5.2 [T]: pre-tied butterfly: finished bow 120 x 60, cut as 2 x (130 + 130), centre band 20 x 40; self-tie: a strip with ends 60 to 70 wide narrowing to 25 at the neck, length 760 to 880; adjustable neckband 380 to 430 long for an adult. Retail listings for self-tie bow ties show total length about 985 (one example) and a neck range of 36 to 48 cm, so the sheet's length is at the low end: confirm on a toile. Shapes (butterfly, batwing, diamond point) differ in the end silhouette: unverified as drawn patterns.
- **Drafting**: 1. Choose bow width and the end shape. 2. Neck width 25 to 30. 3. Length from the neck range. 4. Interlining. 5. Neckband with a hook and slider.
- **Sewing order**: sew the strip right sides together, turn, press; for pre-tied, pinch the middle and wrap it with the knot cover, tack the folds, attach the band.
- **Finishes and notions**: hook and slider, interlining, knot cover.
- **Fabrics**: silk, cotton, wool, velvet.
- **Fit faults**: bow too stiff (lighter interlining), too small (increase the length 10 to 20).
- **Variants**: #necktie, #cravat-ascot.
- **CLO3D**: model the bow as a flat strip folded; low value.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T]; shapes unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
