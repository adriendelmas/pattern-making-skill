# Mitten with Gusset Thumb {#mitten-gusset-thumb}

- **Aliases**: gusseted mitten, thumb-gusset mitten; fr: moufle a soufflet de pouce
- **Family**: glove, mitten
- **Recognize**: the thumb grows out of the side of the mitten with a diamond gusset at its base; the palm and back have a one-piece thumb extension.
- **Pieces**: back (x1), palm (x1) with thumb extension, gusset (x1 diamond per hand), cuff.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Technique sheet 1.12 [T]: add a gusset, a diamond of width 35 and height 70. Logic: the thumb is drafted into the side of the mitten piece (as a side extension), the slit between thumb and hand ends at the crotch, and the diamond is set into it so the thumb can swing away from the hand.
- **Drafting**: 1. Mitten per #mitten-separate-thumb. 2. Add the thumb as a side extension of width T_g/2 + 8. 3. Slit between thumb and hand. 4. Gusset diamond 35 x 70 [T] with notches.
- **Sewing order**: sew the gusset to one side of the slit, then the other; close the thumb and mitten.
- **Finishes and notions**: as #mitten-separate-thumb.
- **Fabrics**: leather, wool, fleece.
- **Fit faults**: gusset puckers -> the diamond too short; thumb pulls -> longer gusset.
- **Variants**: #mitten-separate-thumb, #lobster-mitt.
- **CLO3D**: gusset as a separate diamond piece, sewn on the slit edges.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T]; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
