# Work Glove, Clute Cut {#work-glove-clute-cut}

- **Aliases**: clute pattern, clute-cut work glove; fr: coupe clute
- **Family**: glove, work, knit or fabric
- **Recognize**: one-piece palm, seams on the back or on the inside of the fingers (sources differ); used mainly on knit, textile and cotton gloves.
- **Pieces**: palm piece with fingers (x1 per hand), back piece (x1) with finger strips, thumb, wrist knit.
- **Shape**: unverified. Sources conflict: Kinco and Pyramex say a seamless palm with three seams between the fingers down the back of the hand; Palmflex and Northstar put the seams on the inside of the fingers. This base uses neither without a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft the palm as a single piece including the finger sides (a wrap, not a trank with fourchettes). 2. Finger widths = full finger girth/2 + ease. 3. Seam allowance 5 to 6 mm; cuff knit wrist [T].
- **Sewing order**: wrap the palm, close the back, thumb, cuff.
- **Finishes and notions**: overlocked seams, knit wristband.
- **Fabrics**: knit cotton, jersey, canvas.
- **Fit faults**: finger seams chafe -> move them to the back; fingers stiff -> more stretch.
- **Variants**: #work-glove-gunn-cut.
- **CLO3D**: two to four panels; stretch preset.
- **Confidence**: trade glossaries, conflicting; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
