# Snood / Infinity Scarf {#snood}

- **Aliases**: infinity scarf, circle scarf, neck tube, cowl, neck warmer, loop scarf; fr: snood, tour de cou
- **Family**: accessory, loop
- **Recognize**: a closed loop of fabric worn round the neck once or twice, no ends.
- **Pieces**: tube (x1 rectangle joined at the short ends).
- **Shape**: technique sheet part 6 [T]: infinity loop 280 x 1500 (circumference 1400 to 1600); join the ends and fold the seam to the inside; a Moebius version is joined with a half twist. The seam sews the two short edges together, right sides together; for a tube-lined version the long edges are sewn first, turned, then the ends closed.
- **Drafting**: 1. Width 280 (one layer), length 1500. 2. SA 10. 3. Seam position inside.
- **Sewing order**: join the short ends, turn, press.
- **Finishes and notions**: hidden seam, optional lining.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, wool knit, fleece, silk.
- **Fit faults**: twist of the loop (half twist for Moebius), too bulky (single layer).
- **Variants**: #scarf, #balaclava.
- **CLO3D**: tube; knit preset.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T].
- **Status**: practice
