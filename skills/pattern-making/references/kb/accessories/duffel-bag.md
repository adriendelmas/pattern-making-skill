# Duffel / Barrel Bag {#duffel-bag}

- **Aliases**: duffle bag, gym bag, barrel bag, sports bag, sea bag; fr: sac polochon, sac marin
- **Family**: bag, cylindrical
- **Recognize**: a cylindrical body with a top zipper or drawstring, flat circular ends (barrel) or oval ends, handles on the top.
- **Pieces**: body (x1 rectangle), ends (x2 discs), zip or opening (x1), handles (x2), shoulder strap (x1), lining.
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Barrel: body rectangle of width pi x D (+ SA) and length L; ends: discs of diameter D. The body rectangle sews to the discs around their circumference (disc circumference pi D = body edge). The zip sits in an opening cut lengthwise across the top (the body rectangle is cut in two panels with the zip between). The disc edge and the rectangle end sew edge to edge. For D = 250: pi x D = 785. Typical gym bag D 250 to 300, L 450 to 550 [T, unverified].
- **Drafting**: 1. Choose D and L. 2. Body rectangle pi x D x L, split in two with the zip between. 3. Discs D diameter. 4. Handles at the zip edges. 5. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: zip into the body, handles, ends, lining.
- **Finishes and notions**: bar-tacked handles, D-rings, shoulder strap.
- **Fabrics**: canvas, nylon, leather.
- **Fit faults**: puckers at the discs (notch every 40 mm), bag collapses (stiffener or foam).
- **Variants**: #gusseted-tote, #drawstring-bag.
- **CLO3D**: body with higher stiffness; the discs sewn with 1:1 segment sewing.
- **Confidence**: geometry exact; sizes unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
