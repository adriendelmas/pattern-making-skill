# Braces / Suspenders {#braces-suspenders}

- **Aliases**: suspenders, galluses, trouser braces, Y-back braces; fr: bretelles
- **Family**: accessory, strap, elastic
- **Recognize**: two straps over the shoulders attached to the trouser waistband at front and back, joined at the back in a Y or X, adjusted with sliders; ends with clips or button tabs.
- **Pieces**: straps (x2, elastic or webbing, 25 to 35 wide), back joint (x1 leather or fabric Y piece), button tabs or clips (x4 to x6), adjusters (x2 sliders), front end tabs.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic: two parallel straps run over the shoulders and each splits into two ends (front and back) by a Y joint at the back, or each strap is a separate X at the back. Typical strap width 25 to 35 [T, unverified]; total length of one strap (front clip to back clip) 1100 to 1300 adjustable [T, unverified]. Front ends: two clips or button tabs (leather) each; back ends joined at the back centre.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure from the waistband front over the shoulder to the waistband back (about 55 to 65 cm for each side) and add 15 to 20 per end for tabs and adjuster. 2. Back joint patch about 80 to 100 long. 3. Leather end tabs doubled.
- **Sewing order**: assemble straps and adjusters, attach clips or tabs, sew the Y joint, bar tack.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic webbing, metal clips, leather end tabs, sliders.
- **Fabrics**: elastic webbing, leather tabs, grosgrain.
- **Fit faults**: straps slide off the shoulder (wrong spacing at the back), clips tear (reinforce the tabs).
- **Variants**: #belt.
- **CLO3D**: elastic strap pieces; hang from the waistband with pins.
- **Confidence**: from memory; unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
