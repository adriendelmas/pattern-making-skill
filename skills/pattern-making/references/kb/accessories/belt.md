# Belt {#belt}

- **Aliases**: waist belt, leather belt, fabric belt, waistband belt; fr: ceinture
- **Family**: accessory, strap
- **Recognize**: strip with a buckle at one end, holes (or a slide) at the other, one or two keeper loops; pointed or rounded tip.
- **Pieces**: belt strap (x1 leather, or x1 folded fabric), keeper loop (x1 to x2), buckle (x1), interlining for a fabric belt.
- **Shape**: derived geometry (technique sheet part 4). A strip with constant width; length = waist (at the belt line) + 150 to 180; size = distance from the buckle fold to the middle hole. Finished width 25 to 40 (jeans 30 to 38, dress 20 to 25); buckle inner width = belt width + 1 to 2. Holes: 5, spacing 25, centre hole at the size, first hole 15 from the tip, diameter 4 to 5. Buckle end: fold over 40 to 50 and stitch with 2 rows. Tip: pointed (60 degrees) or round. Fabric belt: strip width = 2 x finished + 20, long edges folded to the centre, interlined with stiff interfacing. A curved waistband belt: the upper edge is shorter than the lower; for a width w and a curve angle phi (radians) the difference is w x phi; draw by arcs.
- **Drafting**: 1. Width and length from the buckle and the waist. 2. Holes at 25 spacing. 3. Fold at the buckle end 40 to 50. 4. Tip shape.
- **Sewing order**: leather: cut, burnish or paint edges, punch holes, fold and stitch the buckle end; fabric: fold the long edges, interline, topstitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: buckle, keeper, eyelets, edge finish.
- **Fabrics**: leather 3 to 4 mm thick, webbing, grosgrain, canvas.
- **Fit faults**: loose (too few holes), buckle bar too tight (strap too thick; reduce thickness on the fold).
- **Variants**: #braces-suspenders.
