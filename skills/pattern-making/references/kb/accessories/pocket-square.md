# Pocket Square {#pocket-square}

- **Aliases**: handkerchief, pochette; fr: pochette de costume, mouchoir
- **Family**: accessory, square
- **Recognize**: small square of fabric displayed in a jacket breast pocket, with hand-rolled or narrow-hemmed edges.
- **Pieces**: square (x1).
- **Shape**: derived geometry. A square of 300 to 400 per side [T, unverified] with a rolled hem 2 to 3 (silk) or a narrow hem 6; corners mitred; the diagonal grain is not used. Sources mention 16 to 20 momme silk (scarves: 14 to 16 momme).
- **Drafting**: 1. Cut the square. 2. Finish the edges. 3. Fold for the display (flat fold, puff, one-point or two-point fold).
- **Sewing order**: hem the four edges (rolled hem by hand or on a rolled-hem foot).
- **Finishes and notions**: rolled hem 2 to 3, hand-rolled edge.
- **Fabrics**: silk, linen, cotton lawn, wool.
- **Fit faults**: curls (use a 5 mm hem or a stabiliser), too bulky (thinner fabric).
- **Variants**: #scarf (square scarf is a bigger square).
- **CLO3D**: not worth.
- **Confidence**: sizes technique sheet part 6 [T] and trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
