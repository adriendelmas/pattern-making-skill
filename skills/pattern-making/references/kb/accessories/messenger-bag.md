# Messenger Bag {#messenger-bag}

- **Aliases**: courier bag, satchel, postman bag, bike bag; fr: sacoche, besace
- **Family**: bag, soft, flap, shoulder strap
- **Recognize**: a bag worn across the body on a long adjustable strap, large flap that covers the front, side gussets.
- **Pieces**: front (x1), back (x1, often continuous with the flap), gusset sides (x2) and bottom (x1) or a U-strip, flap (x1 outer, x1 lining), strap (x1, 40 to 50 wide), pocket (x1), buckles, lining.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. Construction logic: back panel and flap are cut as one long rectangle (W x (Ht + flap length)), the flap folding over the front; flap length covers about 0.5 to 0.7 x Ht [T, unverified]; gusset D 80 to 120 typical; strap 40 to 50 wide x 1000 to 1400 total with an adjuster, joined to the gusset tops (box-X). Flap corners are rounded or angled.
- **Drafting**: 1. Back + flap rectangle. 2. Front rectangle, gusset strip D wide. 3. Strap with adjuster. 4. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pockets. 2. Gusset to front and back. 3. Flap lining. 4. Strap. 5. Buckles.
- **Finishes and notions**: webbing, adjusters, buckles, snap or buckle closure.
- **Fabrics**: canvas, waxed cotton, leather, Cordura.
- **Fit faults**: bag sags to the side (strap anchors too far apart), flap gapes.
- **Variants**: #crossbody-bag, #backpack, #bum-bag.
- **CLO3D**: strap plus flap fold line; high bend stiffness.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
