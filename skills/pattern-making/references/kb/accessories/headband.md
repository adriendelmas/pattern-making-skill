# Headband {#headband}

- **Aliases**: hairband, twisted headband, Alice band (rigid), sweatband; fr: bandeau, serre-tete
- **Family**: hat, accessory
- **Recognize**: strip worn round the head; twisted knot at the front; wide or narrow; or a rigid covered band.
- **Pieces**: plain woven band (x1 strip), knit band (x1 tube), twisted headband (x1 rectangle), Alice band (x1 bias strip over a rigid band).
- **Shape**: technique sheet part 7 [T]. Plain woven: strip width 2 x finished + 20, length 0.9 x H (elastic at the back 100). Knit: tube, flat width = finished width x 2 + 12, length = 0.88 x H + 10. Twisted: rectangle, length 0.88 x H + 20, width 2 x finished + 12; seam the short ends into a ring, twist the ring one half turn, fold the two layers together and sew the join. Alice band: cover a 10 mm band with a bias strip, length 0.55 x H. Finished width 25 to 100.
- **Drafting**: as listed; knit stretch across the length.
- **Sewing order**: sew the long seam (tube), turn, join the ends, twist.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic at the back, topstitch, ball point needle for knit.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, knit, cotton; stretch across the length.
- **Fit faults**: slips (add 10 % negative ease); uneven twist.
- **Variants**: #turban-headwrap, #scrunchie.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset, elastic through the pattern.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T].
- **Status**: practice
