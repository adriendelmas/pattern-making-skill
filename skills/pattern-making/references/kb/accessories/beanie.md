# Beanie (Cut-and-Sew Knit) {#beanie}

- **Aliases**: knit cap, toque, watch cap, skull cap; fr: bonnet
- **Family**: hat, soft, knit
- **Recognize**: close-fitting knit crown, folded cuff, seams radiating from the top (gores) or one seam across the top.
- **Pieces**: crown gores (x6 or x4) or a flat tube (x1), cuff (x1 band), optional pompom.
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Gore: pointed arch (the top is a point), base width = Cb/6 (6 gores), length about 0.24 x H (crown height 120 to 140). Cuff: a straight band, cut height 90 to 110 (folded), length Cb. Alternative: flat tube, width Cb/2, height 220 to 250, closed at the top by a seam or gathered on a cord. Gores sew edge to edge; the cuff sews to the gore base. Source: technique sheet 2.4 [T]; outline unverified as a drawn pattern.
- **Drafting**: 1. Knit stretch 40 to 60 %; Cb = H x 0.85 to 0.9. 2. Gore base Cb/6, length 0.24 H, arch top. 3. Cuff band as above. 4. SA 8 (stretch seam).
- **Sewing order**: sew the gores, attach the cuff, fold, topstitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: ball point needle, stretch stitch; optional lining.
- **Fabrics**: rib, jersey, fleece, 40 to 60 % stretch.
- **Fit faults**: too loose (increase the negative ease), wavy cuff (stretch not matched).
- **Variants**: #balaclava, #beret.
- **CLO3D**: knit fabric preset with stretch; mesh 5 mm.
