# Drawstring Bag {#drawstring-bag}

- **Aliases**: sack, cinch bag, gym sack, pouch bag, dust bag; fr: sac a cordon, sac a dos cordon
- **Family**: bag, soft, closure by cord
- **Recognize**: a gathered top closed by a cord running in a casing, often two cords for backpack straps, flat or rounded bottom.
- **Pieces**: body (x2, or x1 cut as a tube), casing (self, folded at the top), cord (x1 or x2), bottom (x1 circle, optional), lining optional.
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Body = rectangle, width W + 20, height Ht + casing (about 2 x 15 + SA, depending on cord width) + 10. For a round base: side = rectangle of width pi D + SA, bottom = disc of diameter D. Casing: fold the top edge to the inside, stitch twice (cord channel width = cord width + 6), leaving an opening in each side seam above the casing stitch line, or buttonholes. Cord length ≈ 2 x (bag width) + 300 [T, unverified]; backpack version: two cords, each about 2 x height + a loop.
- **Drafting**: 1. Panel size. 2. Casing fold 2 x (cord channel width) + 10. 3. Cord. 4. SA 10.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Hem the casing edge. 2. Side seams stopping at the casing opening. 3. Bottom seam. 4. Casing. 5. Thread the cord.
- **Finishes and notions**: cord stops, grommets, French seams for lining.
- **Fabrics**: canvas, nylon, cotton, linen, satin.
- **Fit faults**: casing too tight (cord does not slide), cord twists (hem edge).
- **Variants**: #tote-bag, #backpack.
- **CLO3D**: tube with a gathered top via elastic or pin; low value.
- **Confidence**: geometry exact; cord length unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
