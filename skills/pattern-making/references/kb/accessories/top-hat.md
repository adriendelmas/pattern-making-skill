# Top Hat {#top-hat}

- **Aliases**: silk hat, topper, formal hat, stovepipe (tall); fr: haut-de-forme
- **Family**: hat, structured, formal
- **Recognize**: tall cylindrical crown with a flat top, narrow brim with sharply curled sides (flat at front and back), grosgrain band.
- **Pieces**: crown tip (x1 oval), crown side (x1, tapered or straight band), brim (x1 ring), band, sweatband, lining, stiffener (buckram or shellac) for crown and brim, wire.
- **Shape**: derived geometry; proportions unverified. Head opening oval as in #fedora-trilby (a = H/5.73, b = 0.82 a). Crown: a straight or slightly flaring cylinder: tip oval equal to the base oval at the head line, side height about 120 to 140 [T, unverified]. Side band length = oval perimeter + SA, closed with a seam at the back, sewn to the tip along the oval. Brim: ring of width 35 to 50 with sides curled up (curl formed by wire and blocking, not by cut). The pattern for the crown side is a rectangle (cylinder) or a ring sector (cone) with sector formulas as in #bucket-hat.
- **Drafting**: 1. Oval opening. 2. Side height. 3. Side band = oval perimeter, rectangle or sector. 4. Brim ring width 35 to 50 wire. 5. Stiffener in all pieces. 6. SA 8.
- **Sewing order**: stiffen, seam the side, tip, brim, band, lining.
- **Finishes and notions**: silk plush or felt, grosgrain ribbon, leather sweatband.
- **Fabrics**: silk plush, fur felt, wool.
- **Fit faults**: tilts (oval not matching the head), crown flops (stiffener missing).
- **Variants**: #fedora-trilby, #pillbox-hat.
- **CLO3D**: cylinder with very high bending stiffness.
- **Confidence**: geometry exact; proportions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
