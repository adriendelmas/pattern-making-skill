# Pillbox Hat {#pillbox-hat}

- **Aliases**: pill box, Jackie hat; fr: toque pill-box
- **Family**: hat, structured, brimless
- **Recognize**: low flat-topped cylinder with straight vertical sides, worn at the back of the head, often with a veil.
- **Pieces**: top (x1 oval or circle), side band (x1), stiffener (x2), lining (x2), optional veil, hair comb or band.
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Top = a circle or oval of circumference Ctop; side = a rectangle of length Ctop and height 60 to 80 [T, unverified]. The band sews to the top, closes at one seam. A pillbox sits on the crown or back of the head and does not enclose the full head circumference: typical top diameter 150 to 200 (unverified), held by a comb, band or elastic. Interface both pieces.
- **Drafting**: 1. Top oval or circle. 2. Side = Ctop + 20 SA x height. 3. Interface; line.
- **Sewing order**: seam the side, attach to the top, line, add the band or comb.
- **Finishes and notions**: buckram, wire, comb or elastic.
- **Fabrics**: wool, silk, felt, buckram base.
- **Fit faults**: slides off -> comb or elastic; top bulges -> stiffener.
- **Variants**: #beret, #top-hat.
- **CLO3D**: cylinder with a top.
