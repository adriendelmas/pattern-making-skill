# Flat Cap / Newsboy Cap (Eight Panels) {#flat-cap-newsboy}

- **Aliases**: newsboy cap, baker boy cap, eight-panel cap, ivy cap (one-piece crown); fr: casquette gavroche, casquette plate
- **Family**: hat, soft crown, short peak
- **Recognize**: a rounded crown that overhangs the band, eight triangular panels radiating from a button at the top, a short stiffened peak at the front attached to the crown.
- **Pieces**: crown panels (x8 outer, x8 lining), peak (x2 fabric, x1 stiffener), button (x1), headband or stay (x1), optional elastic back band. Lining crown is an identical eight panel crown. Some patterns use a one-piece crown (flat cap) instead.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern. From sources: eight triangular panels sewn together form the rounded crown; the peak sews to the front; the button hides the top; a typical vintage pattern uses 5/8 inch (16 mm) SA, which is too wide for small curved panels (trim to about 1/3 inch with notches on the curves). Panel proportions (the crown is wider than the headband, with extra width over the band) are not given in the sources consulted; do not invent them.
- **Drafting**: 1. Base circumference of the crown larger than H + 10 by the pouf amount (typical, unverified). 2. Eight panels, each with a curved side. 3. Peak: a short rounded rectangle, curved to the front. 4. Elastic back band optional. 5. Band stay: narrow ribbon.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Join the eight panels one at a time to form a circle. 2. Assemble the lining crown. 3. Assemble the peak. 4. Attach the peak to the front. 5. Lining, band, button.
- **Finishes and notions**: stay ribbon, interfacing on peak and crown, button.
- **Fabrics**: wool tweed, herringbone, corduroy, leather.
- **Fit faults**: crown flops (panels too wide at the top), band tight (no elastic).
- **Variants**: #baseball-cap-6-panel, #beret.
- **CLO3D**: eight panels simulated as a dome; stiff peak.
- **Confidence**: from web sources describing the typical construction; panel outline unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
