# Tote Bag (Flat) {#tote-bag}

- **Aliases**: shopping bag, flat tote, canvas tote; fr: sac cabas plat, tote bag
- **Family**: bag, soft, unstructured
- **Recognize**: two rectangles joined along the sides and bottom, two handles, no gusset or boxed corners, open top.
- **Pieces**: body (x2, or x1 folded at the bottom), handles (x2), lining (x2, 10 shorter), interfacing (x2, fusible fleece or stabiliser), optional inner pocket (x1).
- **Shape**: derived geometry. Body panel = rectangle W + 20 wide x Ht + 20 high (10 SA each side). Handle strap: finished width 25 to 40, cut width = 4 x finished (both long edges folded to the centre, then folded again); hand handle length 300 to 350 (drop 100 to 140), shoulder handle 560 to 650 (drop 230 to 280). Handles are placed 70 to 120 from the side seams on the right side of the front and back panels and stitched with a box-X. Side seam and bottom seam sew front to back edge to edge; lining the same, 10 shorter. Source: technique sheet 3.1 [T].
- **Drafting**: 1. Panels W + 20 x Ht + 20. 2. Handle length from the drop. 3. Handle placement: centre of each handle 70 to 120 from the side edge. 4. SA 10, top hem 40 (fold twice 20).
- **Sewing order**: 1. Interface. 2. Make and attach the handles to the front and back. 3. Sew the body side and bottom seams. 4. Sew the lining (leave a 120 turning gap). 5. Insert the lining into the bag right sides together, sew the top, turn, topstitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: box-X handle attachment, topstitched rim, optional magnetic snap or zipper.
- **Fabrics**: canvas, denim, cotton duck, jute, linen; interfacing for a flimsy fabric.
- **Fit faults**: handles tear off (box-X and interface), bag sags (add fleece), the bag twists (handles not symmetric).
- **Variants**: #gusseted-tote, #boxed-corner-pouch, #drawstring-bag.
- **CLO3D**: two rectangles joined at sides and bottom; handle as a strap; use a canvas preset; mesh 5 mm.
- **Confidence**: geometry verified; handle ranges technique sheet [T].
- **Status**: practice
