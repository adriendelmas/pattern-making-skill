# Mitten with Separate Thumb {#mitten-separate-thumb}

- **Aliases**: sewn mitten, standard mitten, cut-and-sew mitten; fr: moufle
- **Family**: glove, mitten
- **Recognize**: no separate fingers, a rounded hand part, a thumb as a separate tube set into a hole on the palm side.
- **Pieces**: back (x1 per hand), palm (x1 per hand) with thumb hole, thumb (x2 per hand if cut as front and back, or x1 folded), cuff (x1 or ribbing), lining and insulation if lined.
- **Shape**: unverified as a drawn pattern; technique sheet 1.12 [T]: two identical pieces back and palm, width G/2 + 12 (stretch) or G/2 + 20 (no stretch), length HL + 20, top a semi-ellipse of depth 0.4 x width; thumb a separate piece of width T_g/2 + 8 and length T_o + 10 with the same rounded top; thumb hole on the palm side at 35 mm from the cuff and 25 mm from the side. The thumb sews round the hole; the back and palm sew along the sides and over the top.
- **Drafting**: 1. Width and length as above. 2. Round the top. 3. Mark the thumb hole on the palm piece only (the back is plain). 4. Cut the thumb pair, sew the long seam, set into the hole. 5. Cuff: ribbing or turned hem.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Thumb unit into the hole. 2. Close the sides and top. 3. Cuff. 4. Lining.
- **Finishes and notions**: overlocked or flat seams, knit cuff, hook strap.
- **Fabrics**: fleece, wool, felt, leather, quilted nylon.
- **Fit faults**: thumb binds -> hole too small or too far from the side; tight across the palm -> width.
- **Variants**: #mitten-gusset-thumb, #lobster-mitt.
- **CLO3D**: two flat pieces plus a thumb tube; place the thumb and sew round the hole.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet [T]; shape unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
