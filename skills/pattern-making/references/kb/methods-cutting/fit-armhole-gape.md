# Fit fault: armhole gape {#fit-armhole-gape}

- **Aliases**: gaping armhole, loose front armhole; fr: emmanchure baillante
- **Family**: fitting (armhole, bodice front)
- **Recognize**: the front armhole edge stands away from the body above the bust and shows a gap, often with
  a small bust or a hollow upper chest.
- **Pieces**: bodice front, front armhole facing or sleeve.
- **Shape**: the front armhole curve is longer than the body needs above the bust. Excess is pinched out and
  transferred into the bust dart, or the armhole edge is trimmed. Source: trade practice; amounts depend
  on the toile and are not known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Pin out the gap on the toile and measure it. 2) On the pattern, draw a line from the
  armhole (about midway between shoulder and underarm) to the apex. 3) Cut and close at the armhole by the
  measured gap, which enlarges the dart elsewhere. 4) Redraw the armhole smoothly. 5) Re-check the
  sleeve cap length against the new armhole.
- **Sewing order**: stay-tape the armhole
- **Finishes and notions**: stay tape
- **Fabrics**: any, bias portions worse
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-gaping-neckline, #fit-small-bust-adjustment
- **CLO3D**: check the tension map.
- **Confidence**: method: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
