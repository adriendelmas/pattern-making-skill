# Fit fault: broad back {#fit-broad-back}

- **Aliases**: wide back, tight across the back; fr: dos large
- **Family**: fitting (back bodice)
- **Recognize**: horizontal pulling across the back above the blades, centre back opens at the zip, sleeves
  restrict the arm reach forward.
- **Pieces**: back bodice, sleeve cap.
- **Shape**: back width at the across-back line is too small. The back is spread along a line parallel to CB,
  from the shoulder to the waist, by the missing amount. The shoulder seam gets longer unless the
  shoulder tip is reduced. Source: trade practice; amounts unknown.
- **Drafting**: 1) Measure the missing width on the toile. 2) On the pattern, draw a line from mid-shoulder
  to the waist parallel to CB, cut and spread by the amount at the across-back line. 3) Redraw the
  shoulder and the waist. 4) If the shoulder is lengthened, shorten the tip, and re-check the armhole and
  the cap.
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: none
- **Fabrics**: stable wovens show the fault the most
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-rounded-back, #fit-forward-shoulder
- **CLO3D**: use a size grade to see the fault.
- **Confidence**: method: trade practice.
- **Status**: practice
