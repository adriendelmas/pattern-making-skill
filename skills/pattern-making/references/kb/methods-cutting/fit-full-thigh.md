# Fit fault: full thigh {#fit-full-thigh}

- **Aliases**: thick thighs, tight thigh, drag lines across the thigh; fr: cuisses fortes
- **Family**: fitting (trouser)
- **Recognize**: horizontal drag lines across the top of the thigh, a tight leg, the crease pulled to the
  side.
- **Pieces**: trouser front and trouser back.
- **Shape**: the thigh circumference is too small. A leg circumference is the width of the front and
  back pieces together, so the extra needed per leg is shared between the front and the back and
  between the side seam and the inseam. Source: trade practice; amounts measured on the toile and not
  known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Measure the extra thigh circumference. 2) Draw a vertical line along the crease on
  the front and the back. 3) Cut, spread by half the total extra for each of the front and back, at
  thigh level, tapering to zero at the knee. 4) Redraw the side seam and inseam, and extend the crotch
  curve if the inseam is moved. 5) Re-check the inseam lengths.
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: none
- **Fabrics**: stretch needs less

- **Variants**: #trouser-block, #fit-crotch-adjustments
