# Skirt drape {#skirt-drape}

- **Aliases**: draped skirt, skirt sloper drape; fr: moulage de la jupe
- **Family**: draping
- **Recognize**: muslin on the hips with darts at the waist and side seams marked from the form.
- **Pieces**: skirt front (1 half), skirt back (1 half).
- **Shape**: CF/CB lengthwise grain on the form's CF/CB, crosswise grain at the hip line; the muslin is
  smoothed down from the hip line to the hem; the waist excess is pinned into darts (front 1 or 2,
  back 2 typical). The side seam is placed at the form's side. The hem is levelled from the floor.
  Source: general draping practice; unverified.
- **Drafting**: 1) Pin the hip line level to the form's hip line. 2) Smooth up to the waist and pin the
  darts. 3) Smooth down to the hem. 4) Mark waist, side seam and dart legs. 5) Level the hem by measuring
  from the floor.
- **Sewing order**: not applicable
- **Finishes and notions**: pins, pencil, hem gauge
- **Fabrics**: muslin; bias or fluid fabrics move, so level after hanging
- **Fit faults**: hip line not level: re-pin; side seam leans: re-set the muslin grain.
- **Variants**: #skirt-block, #adding-flare

- **Confidence**: practice: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
