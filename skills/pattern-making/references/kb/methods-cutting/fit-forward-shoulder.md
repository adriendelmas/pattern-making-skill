# Fit fault: forward shoulder {#fit-forward-shoulder}

- **Aliases**: rounded shoulders, forward-set shoulder, shoulder seam too far back; fr: epaules enroulees, epaule en avant
- **Family**: fitting (shoulder, bodice)
- **Recognize**: the shoulder seam sits behind the top of the shoulder, drag lines run from the back
  neck towards the armhole and the front armhole looks tight.
- **Pieces**: front bodice, back bodice, sleeve (cap alignment).
- **Shape**: the shoulder line should lie further to the front. The shoulder seam is shifted forward: the back
  shoulder edge gains length at the tip end and the front shoulder edge loses the same. Source: trade practice;
  amounts measured on the toile and not known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Mark on the toile where the shoulder seam should lie. 2) Add the measured amount at the
  back shoulder tip and remove it at the front shoulder tip, keeping the neck point. 3) Recut the
  armholes and re-check the cap length. 4) Re-check the sleeve alignment (#fit-sleeve-twist).
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: shoulder pad position may shift
- **Fabrics**: all
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-rounded-back, #fit-broad-back
- **CLO3D**: check shoulder seam position on an avatar with the matching posture.
- **Confidence**: method: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
