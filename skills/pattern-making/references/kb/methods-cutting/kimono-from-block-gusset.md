# Kimono with gusset {#kimono-from-block-gusset}

- **Aliases**: kimono sleeve with underarm gusset, diamond gusset; fr: manche kimono avec carre d'aisance
- **Family**: manipulation (sleeve)
- **Recognize**: body and sleeve in one piece, with a diamond or triangle inserted in the underarm slit to
  allow the arm to lift.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), gusset (1 square, or 2 triangles) per underarm.
- **Shape**: the underarm seam is slit from the body edge into the sleeve. A square gusset is set on the
  bias (as a diamond); its four sides are sewn to the two slit edges of the front and the two of the
  back. Slit length equals the side of the square. Gusset side length: not known, varies with the design.
  Source: traditional gusset construction; unverified for dimensions.
- **Drafting**: 1) Cut the underarm slit. 2) Draw the gusset as a square whose side equals the slit
  length. 3) Mark the corner points. 4) Add seam allowance and notches.
- **Sewing order**: reinforce the slit end with stay stitching, gusset to slit with a pivot at each corner,
  then close the underarm seam up to the gusset.
- **Finishes and notions**: reinforcement of the slit end
- **Fabrics**: wovens; a bias gusset moves with the arm
- **Fit faults**: pulling at the gusset corner: deepen the slit; wrinkles: lower the underarm.
- **Variants**: #kimono-block, #raglan-from-set-in
- **CLO3D**: set the gusset on the bias grain and sew in four segments.
- **Confidence**: structure: trade practice; dimensions: not known.
- **Status**: unverified
