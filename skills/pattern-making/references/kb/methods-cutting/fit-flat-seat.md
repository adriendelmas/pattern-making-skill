# Fit fault: flat seat {#fit-flat-seat}

- **Aliases**: flat buttocks, low flat seat; fr: fesses plates
- **Family**: fitting (trouser and skirt back)
- **Recognize**: vertical wrinkles and bagging below the seat, a back crotch that hangs loose.
- **Pieces**: back trouser or back skirt.
- **Shape**: the back has too much length and width over the seat. Remove width at the CB and length by
  lowering the back crotch curve or reducing the CB at the waist. Source: trade practice; amounts
  measured on the toile and not known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Measure the excess on the toile. 2) Remove a wedge at CB from the waist, tapering to
  the crotch; narrow the dart. 3) Straighten the back hip curve slightly. 4) Redraw the back crotch
  curve and compare its length with the front. 5) Re-check the waistband length.

- **Finishes and notions**: none
- **Fabrics**: all
