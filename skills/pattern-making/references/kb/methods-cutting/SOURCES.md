# Sources: methods-cutting

- Winifred Aldrich, Metric Pattern Cutting (women's wear, menswear, children's wear, lingerie, jersey
  fashion): identity of the system and half-bust-plus-5 rule from secondary instructions. The constants
  were not seen.
- Helen Joseph Armstrong, Patternmaking for Fashion Design; Draping for Apparel Design: identity only.
- Comparison study of Bunka, ESMOD, Armstrong and Aldrich fitted bodices (journal abstract found by search):
  silhouette and ease differences.
- Tomoko Nakamichi, Pattern Magic (Bunka size M data reported on a blog; formulas from memory).
- Rundschau-Verlag Koeniger / M. Muller & Sohn books and company history page: publishing facts.
- Skill references in this repository: references/blocks.md, ease.md, finishes.md, transformations.md
  (ease values, block rules, notches, seam allowances, transformations, grading note).
- Fashion Incubator, "What are grade specs, grade rules and grade rule libraries?"
  https://fashion-incubator.com/what-are-grade-specs-grade-rules-and-grade-rule-libraries/ (cardinal
  points and grade rules); Minerva Patterns blog on grading.
- ASTM D5585-21, Standard Tables of Body Measurements for Adult Female Misses Figure Type, Size Range 00-20
  (store listing https://store.astm.org/d5585-21.html); ASTM D5586 (listing seen in a grading textbook
  bibliography). Table values were not seen.
- Wikipedia, "Joint European standard for size labelling of clothes" (EN 13402); BS 3666 history as
  summarised on the same page.
- ASTM D6673, Standard Practice for Pattern Data Interchange (DXF-based) (ANSI/ASTM store listings and
  archived copies found by search); Minerva Patterns blog posts on DXF-AAMA vs DXF-ASTM; a Rhino forum
  thread and a Scribd DXF-AAMA document (layer numbers).
- apparelresources.com, "Performance measurement tools: cutting production planning" and "Fabric
  utilization: cut order planning" (marker efficiency, lay consumption); onlineclothingstudy.com articles on
  spreading modes (face-to-face, one-way, two-way, nap modes).
- Patent and vendor pages on leather nesting (US 10338564 and Lectra leather-yield page): defects and
  yield figures, 2 to 4 % and up to 7 % nesting gain.
- Timo Rissanen and Holly McQuillan, Zero Waste Fashion Design (Bloomsbury); Rissanen chapter on zero-waste
  design (UTS repository); study on jigsaw, tessellation and subtraction methods (Nigde Omer Halisdemir
  University); seamwork.com zero-waste article.
- Not verified and marked as such in the entries: dart point set-backs beyond the skill's figures, specific
  fit-fault correction amounts (always measured on the toile), Bunka constants, Kunick, Dresdner as a
  documented system, die-cutting and laser-cutting details, AAMA layer numbers.
