# Fit fault: small bust adjustment {#fit-small-bust-adjustment}

- **Aliases**: SBA, small bust, hollow chest; fr: poitrine faible, ajustement pour petite poitrine
- **Family**: fitting (bust)
- **Recognize**: baggy fabric over the bust, dart too long and pointing past the body, gaping armhole or
  neckline.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (half).
- **Shape**: the reverse of the full bust adjustment: the cut pieces are overlapped instead of spread; the
  reduction per half front = the circumference to remove / 4. The apex moves up and in; the dart gets smaller
  and shorter. Source: trade practice; amounts depend on the body.
- **Drafting**: 1) Mark the new apex. 2) Cut the same lines as for #fit-full-bust-adjustment. 3) Overlap
  the vertical cut by the reduction and raise the side piece by the same value. 4) Redraw the dart to the
  new apex. 5) Redraw side seam, shoulder and hem.
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: none
- **Fabrics**: all
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-full-bust-adjustment, #fit-armhole-gape
- **CLO3D**: edit points or re-import the DXF.
- **Confidence**: method: trade practice.
- **Status**: practice
