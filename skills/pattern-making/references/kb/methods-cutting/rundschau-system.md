- **Aliases**: Rundschau fuer Internationale Herrenmode (Herren-Rundschau), Rundschau fuer die deutsche
  Damenschneiderei; Rundschau-Verlag Koeniger
- **Family**: publisher and trade journal with a published cutting tradition
- **Recognize**: German trade magazines and books. The company publishes the Muller & Sohn system
  books (dress and blouse, jackets and coats, trousers, shirts, menswear). A review reports historical
  Rundschau menswear constructions going back to Muller & Sohn.
