# Fit fault: rounded back {#fit-rounded-back}

- **Aliases**: dowager's hump, kyphosis, high round back; fr: dos rond, dos voute
- **Family**: fitting (upper back)
- **Recognize**: the back neckline sits away from the neck, horizontal folds at the upper back, the centre back
  seam rises and the back hem lifts.
- **Pieces**: back bodice (half), collar or facing at the back neck.
- **Shape**: the upper back needs extra length at CB. Cut a horizontal line across the back at the level
  of the shoulder blades, from CB to the armhole, open at CB by the amount and hinge at the armhole;
  the shoulder dart increases. Source: trade practice; amounts measured on the toile and not known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Measure the lift needed at CB on the toile. 2) Draw the horizontal line from CB to a
  point near the armhole and cut up to the hinge. 3) Open the cut at CB by the amount and rotate
  the upper part, tapering to zero at the armhole. 4) Redraw the neckline and shoulder, add or widen the
  shoulder dart. 5) Re-measure the neckline for the collar.
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: collar neck length adjusted
- **Fabrics**: all
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-swayback, #fit-forward-shoulder, #fit-broad-back
