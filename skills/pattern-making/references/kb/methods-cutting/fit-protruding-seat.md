# Fit fault: protruding seat {#fit-protruding-seat}

- **Aliases**: full seat, prominent buttocks, high round seat; fr: fesses fortes, cambrure forte
- **Family**: fitting (trouser and skirt back)
- **Recognize**: horizontal drag lines and smile folds below the seat, the back waist dips, side seams twist,
  the back crotch pulls up.
- **Pieces**: back trouser or back skirt.
- **Shape**: the back needs more length from the waist over the seat and more width at the hip. Extra width
  and length are added by slashing from the waist through the dart to the hem and spreading at the
  seat level. Source: trade practice; amounts measured on the toile and not known.
- **Drafting**: 1) Measure the missing width and length on the toile. 2) Draw a vertical line from
  the waist through the back dart to the hem (trouser: along the crease), and a horizontal line at seat
  level to the side seam. 3) Cut both lines, spread the vertical cut by the width and lower the side
  piece by the same value. 4) Add a little to CB and raise the CB waist by the length. 5) Re-check the
  crotch curve length (#fit-crotch-adjustments).
- **Sewing order**: unchanged
- **Finishes and notions**: none
- **Fabrics**: all
- **Fit faults**: this is the fault entry
- **Variants**: #fit-flat-seat, #fit-crotch-adjustments, #trouser-block
