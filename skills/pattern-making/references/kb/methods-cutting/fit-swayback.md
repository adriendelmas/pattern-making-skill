# Fit fault: swayback {#fit-swayback}

- **Aliases**: sway back, hollow back, excess length at the back waist; fr: dos creux, ensellure
- **Family**: fitting (back waist)
- **Recognize**: horizontal folds across the back waist or the upper hip, the waist seam rides up at the centre
  back and the side seams swing back.
- **Pieces**: back bodice, back skirt or back trouser, waistband.
- **Shape**: the centre back is too long between the waist and the hip. A wedge is removed at the waist: depth
  at CB, tapering to zero at the side seam. The back waist dart is adjusted. Source: trade practice;
  the block already lowers the CB waist by 1 cm in skirts (blocks.md); wedge depth is measured on the
  toile and is not known here.
- **Drafting**: 1) Pinch out the fold on the toile at CB and measure. 2) On the pattern, draw a line from
  CB at the waist to the side seam. 3) Overlap the line by the measured depth at CB, tapering to zero at
  the side seam (hinge). 4) Lower the CB waist by the amount and re-curve the waist line. 5) Redraw
  the CB seam straight.

- **Fabrics**: all

- **Variants**: #fit-rounded-back, #fit-flat-seat
