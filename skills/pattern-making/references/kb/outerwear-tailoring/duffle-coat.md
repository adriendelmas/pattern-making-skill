# Duffle coat {#duffle-coat}

- **Aliases**: duffel coat, toggle coat, Montgomery duffle; fr: duffle-coat, duffle coat
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: three-quarter length heavy wool coat, a big hood that lies on the shoulders, 3 to 4 toggles (horn or wood) with loops of rope or leather, a short shoulder yoke (shoulder strap on the right), large patch pockets (TO 12.3)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1), front yoke (2) and back yoke (1) (TO 12.3), hood (2) + lining (2), sleeve (2 or 2+2), patch pockets (2), toggle straps (3 to 4, leather) and loops, lining optional
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 12.3 gives the piece set; the exact yoke shape is not documented).
  Body: straight three-quarter length (85 to 100), a yoke across the shoulders (front pair and a back yoke), large patch pockets with flaps (18 to 22 by 20 to 24, TO 14.3 for a parka pocket), front closed by toggles on loops; the right front has a shoulder strap/yoke strap (TO 12.3). Hood: large, per #hood-two-piece with depth 29 to 33 and height 36 to 40 (men), no drawcord; the neck edge is sewn to the neckline without a collar, the front ends at the front edge so the hood is open at CF.
- **Drafting**: coat ease 26 to 36, hood per TO 13.2; toggle spacing 9 to 12 apart (coats), bottom toggle 12 to 15 above the hem (TO 9.3); toggle loops 60 to 90 long (TO 9.1 toggle length)
- **Sewing order**: yokes, fronts and backs, patch pockets, shoulders and sides, hood, sleeves, toggles at the end
- **Finishes and notions**: horn or wooden toggles 60 to 90 long, rope or leather loops, patch pockets stitched and bar-tacked, raw or bound edges on melton
- **Fabrics**: heavy wool melton, duffle cloth
- **Fit faults**: hood falls back -> lengthen the CB neck seam; toggle loops pull -> reinforce the front edge with tape
- **Variants**: #pea-coat, #hood-two-piece, #parka-fishtail-m51, #anorak
- **CLO3D**: hood sewn to the neckline, mesh 8 to 10 mm; toggles decorative
- **Confidence**: TO 12.3 (trade practice, pieces); yoke outline and toggle loop detail unverified
- **Status**: unverified
