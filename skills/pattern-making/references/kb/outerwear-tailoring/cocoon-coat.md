# Cocoon coat {#cocoon-coat}

- **Aliases**: egg coat, oversized coat, bubble coat; fr: manteau cocon, manteau oversize
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: rounded volume, narrow at the hem, dropped shoulders, curved side seams, funnel or standing collar, sleeves that taper (TO 12.4)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), collar (1), facing (2), pocket bags (TO 12.4)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 12.4, 12.5).
  Egg-shaped body: the side seams bulge to the widest point at the hip (about 18 to 25 below the waist), then return towards the hem, which is narrower by 4 to 8. Dropped shoulder: the shoulder end is moved 3 to 10 outward; the armhole is a short, deep curve; the underarm is lowered 6 to 10; the sleeve is joined as a straight seam to the dropped shoulder, cap height 3 to 8, no cap ease; sleeve top width = armhole depth + 4 to 6; the sleeve is narrow at the cuff (13 to 15 flat). The sleeve joins the dropped armhole at 45 degrees. No darts.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Coat block with ease 35 to 55 (TO 1.2).
  2. Slash and spread the side seams to open the hip by 8 to 12 total per piece at the widest point.
  3. Bring the hem back in by 4 to 8.
  4. Drop the shoulder 5 to 10 and lower the underarm 6 to 10.
  5. Draw the sleeve from the lowered point as a straight sleeve (cap height 3 to 8).
  6. Reinforce the curved side seams with stay tape.
- **Sewing order**: shoulder seams, sleeves set flat before the side seams (easier on a dropped armhole), side seams with stay tape, collar, facing, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: stay tape on curved side seams, interfaced collar, hem 4 to 6
- **Fabrics**: wool, double-faced wool, boiled wool; heavy drape fabrics avoid seam stretch
- **Fit faults**: shoulder collapses -> stabilise with tape; hem gapes -> reduce the hem width less than 4; sleeve pulls at the armpit -> deepen the underarm
- **Variants**: #swing-coat, #shrug, #kimono-jacket, #raglan-coat-sleeve
- **CLO3D**: mesh 10 to 15 mm; pieces seamed with ease; drape fabric property
- **Confidence**: TO 12.4 and 12.5 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
