# Biker / Perfecto leather jacket {#biker-perfecto}

- **Aliases**: Perfecto, motorcycle jacket, moto jacket, double-rider jacket; fr: perfecto, blouson de motard, blouson en cuir motard
- **Family**: leather outerwear
- **Recognize**: heavy leather jacket with an asymmetric front zip, wide snap-down lapels and collar, snap-down epaulettes, a belt with a square buckle, zippered pockets (three outside plus a coin flap), zippered cuffs, short length (about 25 inch at the back in the Schott 613, reviewers: 618 sits just above the waist)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back panels (centre back and wide side back, or a large back panel), sleeve (2, curved, one or two pieces), collar (2 outer + 2 under) with snaps, lapels (integral), epaulettes (2), belt (1) and loops, pocket bags and welts (zip pockets 3 + coin pocket 1), lining (quilted nylon, fixed)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline; features from Schott NYC product pages (asymmetric zipper, snap-down lapels and epaulettes, "bi-swing back panels", underarm "footballs" with grommet vents, zippered cuffs, quilted lining) and Wikipedia.
  The front closure is asymmetric: the zip runs on a diagonal line offset from CF, with the right panel overlapping the left (double-rider design: one part of the front zipper sits a couple of inches in, giving a wind seal). The lapels snap down to the chest. The back is a large panel (Schott: "bi-swing" panels permitting freedom of movement) with vented underarm gussets (footballs); the Schott 613 lower back panel and sleeve underside are one piece (early construction description). Belt with a nickel square buckle sewn into the side seams. Exact piece outlines and measurements not known.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Short jacket block, ease 14 to 20 (leather has little ease), shoulder drop none, sleeve with a curve (elbow pitch 2 to 3).
  2. Asymmetric front: draw the zip line 5 to 8 off CF on the left (estimate, unverified).
  3. Snap-down collar and lapel: the lapel width 8 to 10, collar stand 3.
  4. Add leather seam allowance 0.6 to 1.0, no easing at the cap; gusset (football) panels under the arm.
  5. Zip pockets 14 to 18 long (TO 8.7).
- **Sewing order**: glue and topstitch (no pins), assemble back panels, attach fronts, pockets and zips, sleeves, collar and lapel with snaps, belt, lining
- **Finishes and notions**: heavy zips, snaps, belt buckle, leather edge finish, topstitching 3.5 to 4 mm stitch length
- **Fabrics**: steerhide or horsehide leather; leather has no stretch, so cap ease 0 to 1 and ease concentrated in the back and underarm
- **Fit faults**: sleeve restricts movement -> add the underarm gusset and a pitch; zip bulges -> relieve the facing; collar stands away -> stitch the lapel snaps closer
- **Variants**: #denim-trucker-jacket, #bomber-ma1, #field-jacket-m65
- **CLO3D**: leather fabric property, thick, low drape; asymmetric zip as a topstitched line and a zipper trim; mesh 5 to 8 mm

- **Status**: unverified
