# Cloak {#cloak}

- **Aliases**: mantle, hooded cloak, Inverness (with cape), pelerine cloak; fr: cape à capuche, mantelet, manteau-cape
- **Family**: capes and cloaks
- **Recognize**: long full cape that reaches to the knee or ankle, often with a hood, tied or clasped at the neck, with or without arm slits
- **Pieces**: body (1 half-circle or 2 pieces), hood (2), lining, ties
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (no sheet; the circle geometry is as #cape).
  A long cape with the same geometry as the circle cape, with length 100 to 140 (typical); the hood is per #hood-two-piece or #hood-three-piece and sewn to the neck curve; the front edges can be straight with a tie, a clasp or buttons.
- **Drafting**: as #cape with the hood added; neck radius from the neck circumference; hood height 36 to 40 and depth 29 to 33 (TO 13.1)
- **Sewing order**: as #cape, hood last, lining bagged
- **Finishes and notions**: clasp or ties, optional pockets in the front slits
- **Fabrics**: wool, velvet, heavy cotton
- **Fit faults**: cloak slides from the shoulders -> reduce the neck radius, add shoulder shaping; hood heavy -> interface
- **Variants**: #cape, #capelet, #hood-two-piece, #duffle-coat
- **CLO3D**: as #cape; hood sewn to the neck
