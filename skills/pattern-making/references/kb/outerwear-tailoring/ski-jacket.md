# Ski jacket {#ski-jacket}

- **Aliases**: snow jacket, shell jacket (ski), insulated ski jacket; fr: veste de ski, blouson de ski
- **Family**: technical outerwear
- **Recognize**: waterproof taped shell with insulation, a powder skirt at the hem, wrist gaiters, a helmet-compatible hood, pit zips, a lift-pass pocket on the sleeve, a snow-proof storm flap
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2, articulated), hood (3-piece), collar (2), storm flap (2), pocket bags (4+), powder skirt (1), cuff gaiter (2), lining (3), insulation (pieces as the lining)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 15 and 17 generalise; no sheet).
  Longer back, an inner powder skirt of knit or fabric 3 to 4 deep sewn into the hem (estimate), hood sized for a helmet (extra 3 to 4 on height and 2 on width over TO 13.1), taped seams (TO 17.3), pit zips 25 to 30 long (estimate).
- **Drafting**: ease 28 to 40 (over layers), underarm lowered 4 to 6, hood #hood-three-piece; seam allowance 1.0 to 1.2 for taped seams
- **Sewing order**: tape-able sequence per TO 17.3: shoulders before sleeves, side before hem; zips covered with flaps
- **Finishes and notions**: PU-coated zips, seam tape 16 to 22, Velcro, snaps, powder skirt snaps
- **Fabrics**: 2- or 3-layer waterproof breathable membrane, wadding 60 to 100 g/m2
- **Fit faults**: sleeves ride up when reaching -> add length and cap height; hood obscures view -> reduce width
- **Variants**: #puffer-jacket, #softshell-jacket, #anorak, #parka-fishtail-m51
- **CLO3D**: thick fabric property, padding setting; mesh 6 to 8 mm
- **Confidence**: from memory plus TO 13, 15, 17; most dimensions estimates; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
