# Chesterfield coat {#chesterfield-coat}

- **Aliases**: Chesterfield overcoat, velvet-collar overcoat, fly-front overcoat; fr: manteau Chesterfield, pardessus à col de velours
- **Family**: coats, overcoats
- **Recognize**: knee-length single-breasted (or double-breasted) coat with a velvet undercollar, a fly front hiding the buttons, covered buttons, ticket pocket, plain or slightly fitted body (a descriptive source: "single-breasted, semi-long, velvet collar, ticket pocket, fly front, sack or slightly fitted back")
- **Pieces**: front (2), side front (2, optional), back (2, CB seam), side back (2), top sleeve (2), under sleeve (2), top collar (1, velvet or cloth), undercollar (2, felt or velvet), fly strip (1 or cut on the front), front facing (2), back neck facing (1), pocket flaps (2 to 4), ticket pocket flap (1), welts (2), chest welt (1), bags (4), canvas and chest pieces (2), lining pieces, interlining (TO 10.2)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 10.2, 10.3; descriptive Wikipedia and The London Lounge threads for the features).
  Body: straight with little waist shaping. Front edge CF + wrap 3 (large buttons); fly front: a facing strip folded behind the button edge, buttonholes work through the front, covering the buttons. Lapel width 8 to 10, gorge 8 to 10 (TO 2.5) or higher for the overcoat; collar stand 3 to 4, fall 4.5 to 6; velvet collar: the top collar is cut in velvet (straight cut) or the undercollar in velvet and the top collar in self cloth, depending on the version (unverified). Hem sweep 130 to 160. Back vent or CB box pleat 28 to 40. Slit pockets angled 2 to 3; ticket pocket flap on the right.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Coat block (TO 1.3, coat column): ease 20 to 26 (men), underarm lowered 3.0 to 4.0, shoulder widened 1.0 to 1.5.
  2. Length 100 to 110 (knee).
  3. Fly: extend the front edge CF + wrap 3 plus a 3.5 to 4.5 wide fly strip.
  4. Collar and lapel per #notched-lapel with coat values; velvet collar cut on straight grain.
  5. Sleeve #two-piece-sleeve with coat values, length arm + 2 to 3.
  6. Facing 10 to 12 at the chest, 8 to 10 at the hem.
- **Sewing order**: TO 2.8 with a wide hand-hemmed hem 5 to 7, lining hung with CB pleat 2.0 and a jump pleat 3, velvet collar applied last
- **Finishes and notions**: covered buttons 28 to 35 mm, fly strip, hair canvas, shoulder pad 12 to 20 mm, hem weight, velvet 
- **Fabrics**: wool melton, cashmere, herringbone tweed; velvet in cotton or silk; use a velvet nap direction downward at CB
- **Fit faults**: collar and lapel curl -> shrink the undercollar, tape the roll line; hem swings -> add a hem weight; sleeves too tight over a jacket -> check bicep girth including the jacket sleeve (TO 1.1)
- **Variants**: #overcoat, #crombie-coat, #ulster-coat, #polo-coat, #tailored-coat
- **CLO3D**: fly as a separate piece sewn behind the front edge; velvet collar with a velvet fabric property; buttons by hand
- **Confidence**: construction trade practice (TO 10); feature list from secondary descriptions (Wikipedia, The London Lounge forum); fly width and collar piece layout unverified
- **Status**: unverified
