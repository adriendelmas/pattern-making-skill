# Trench coat {#trench-coat}

- **Aliases**: Burberry-type trench, Mackintosh trench; fr: trench-coat, trench
- **Family**: coats, raincoats
- **Recognize**: double-breasted, belted, gabardine; storm flap on the right chest, gun flap at the right shoulder, epaulettes, back yoke (rain cape), cuff straps, deep back vent or box pleat, D-rings (TO 11.1)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 on fold or 2), back yoke (1), storm flap (1), gun flap (1), epaulettes (2 pairs, 5 x 13 to 15), sleeve (top 2, under 2, or raglan 2), sleeve strap (2, 4 wide), belt (1 or 2, 5 to 6 wide, length waist + 50 to 80), belt loops (6), top collar (1), undercollar (2), facing (2), pocket flaps (2) + bags, lining (front 2, back 1, sleeve 4, optional), D-rings (2 to 4), buttons (10, 6 working), see TO 11.2
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline; piece list from TO 11.2, Minerva trench anatomy and The Shapes of Fabric articles.
  Front: DB overlap 8 to 10 from CF, two button columns 10 to 12 apart; right front carries the storm flap (8 to 10 wide at the chest, attached at the shoulder seam side and lying under the buttoned front) and, above it, the gun flap (14 to 18 wide at the shoulder, straight lower edge 12 to 14 below the armhole level, sewn in at the shoulder and the armhole only). Back: back yoke cape hanging from the shoulder, 18 to 25 deep at CB, free from the armhole with a gap of 1 to 2 for drainage; deep back vent 30 to 45 or a box pleat at CB 5 to 6 wide. Sleeve: set-in two-piece or raglan; cuff strap 4 by 22 fixed at the hind seam. Collar: stand 3, fall 5 to 6, raisable with a throat latch 4 wide. Belt with buckle and D-rings; belt loops at the side seams and the back.
- **Drafting**: TO 11.3 steps 1 to 12: block with darts or princess seams, ease 20 to 28 (women) or 24 to 32 (men), underarm lowered 2.5 to 3.5, length men 105 to 115, women 95 to 110, DB wrap 8 to 10, flaps, yoke, epaulettes, belt, collar, cuff strap, vent
- **Sewing order**: TO 11.4: make yoke, flaps and belt first; flat-felled or bound seams; front darts, flaps, back, shoulders with the yoke, side seams, sleeves, collar, facing and lining, hem, then buttons, D-rings, epaulettes, belt, cuff straps; double topstitching 0.6 to 0.8 from the edge
- **Finishes and notions**: flat-felled seams, 10 buttons 22 to 28 mm, buckles and D-rings, snaps
- **Fabrics**: cotton gabardine (water resistant by weave), poplin, waterproof treated cotton; keep the pieces on the same grain for a uniform sheen
- **Fit faults**: flap lifts -> tape stay and button; yoke hangs crooked -> check the grain and equal shoulder seam length; belt skewed -> straight grain, even edge stitching; rain enters the sleeve -> adjust the cuff strap
- **Variants**: #raincoat-mac, #storm-flap, #gun-flap, #epaulette, #raglan-coat-sleeve, #wrap-coat
- **CLO3D**: yoke as a separate piece sewn at the shoulder with a free hem; flaps as pieces; belt with loops; mesh 8 to 10 mm
- **Confidence**: piece list from TO 11.2 and Minerva anatomy page (descriptions); dimensions trade practice; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
