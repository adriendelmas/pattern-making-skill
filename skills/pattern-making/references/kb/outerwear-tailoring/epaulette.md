# Epaulette {#epaulette}

- **Aliases**: shoulder strap, shoulder tab, shoulder loop, shoulder board; fr: épaulette, patte d'épaule, passant d'épaule
- **Family**: outerwear components
- **Recognize**: a strap on top of the shoulder, fixed at the armhole end and buttoned at the neck end; on trench coats, field jackets, shirts
- **Pieces**: strap (2 per epaulette, upper and under, or 1 folded), interfacing, button
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline (TO 11.2 and 11.3 step 7).
  Strap 5 wide by 13 to 15 long (finished), with a pointed or squared free end, a bar tack at the fixed end and a button about 2 from the free end (TO 11.3: "fixed at the shoulder seam by a bar tack, button 2 from the armhole"). Which end is fixed is ambiguous in TO 11.3; the usual trade practice (unverified) is: fixed end sewn into the armhole/shoulder seam at the sleeve, free end buttoned towards the neck. The strap lies on the shoulder line, centred on the shoulder seam.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Cut upper and under strap 5 x 14 plus allowances; shape the free end (point).
  2. Interface the upper, sew, turn, topstitch.
  3. Place the free end at the neck side with a button and buttonhole.
- **Sewing order**: make the strap, sew into the shoulder/armhole seam before setting the sleeve, bar tack the position, sew the button
- **Finishes and notions**: button 15 to 20 mm, topstitching 0.6 from the edge
- **Fabrics**: as the coat; two layers with interfacing
- **Fit faults**: strap curls -> interface; strap is crooked -> mark positions on the toile
- **Variants**: #gun-flap, #trench-coat, #field-jacket-m65, #safari-jacket
- **CLO3D**: a separate small piece fixed at the shoulder; stiff fabric property
- **Confidence**: TO 11.2, 11.3 (trade practice); strap dimensions for the field jacket not known; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
