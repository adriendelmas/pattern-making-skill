# Pea coat {#pea-coat}

- **Aliases**: peacoat, pea jacket, reefer (US usage), naval bridge coat; fr: caban, caban de marin
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: short (75 to 82) double-breasted wool melton coat, wide lapels with a high collar that can be turned up, 6 to 8 large buttons (25 to 35 mm, often anchor-stamped), vertical or slanted slash pockets (hand warmers), warm quilted lining (TO 12.1)
- **Pieces**: front (2), side front (2, optional), back (2), side back (2), top sleeve (2), under sleeve (2), top collar (1), undercollar (2), facing (2), slash pocket welts (2), bags (4), lining pieces (TO 12.1)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 12.1).
  Body: DB, wrap 8 from CF, two button columns each about 5 to 5.5 from CF; lapel width 8 to 10, collar stand 3.5 to 4; armhole lowered 2.5 to 3.5; hem straight. Pockets: slash pockets cut at the front/side-front seam at an angle 20 to 30 degrees, opening 16 to 18; some versions have vertical slits in the front edge. Back with a CB seam and a vent or none (unverified).
- **Drafting**: ease 20 to 26 (men), 16 to 22 (women); length 75 to 82 from nape; wrap 8; lapel #peak-lapel or notch; pockets angled 20 to 30; sleeve #two-piece-sleeve with coat values; facing 10 to 12
- **Sewing order**: as #overcoat; slash pockets before side seams; buttons sewn through a stay button on the inside
- **Finishes and notions**: large anchor buttons, stay buttons, interfacing, lining quilted
- **Fabrics**: wool melton (navy), Kersey wool; thick cloth needs turn of cloth 0.5 to 0.8
- **Fit faults**: shoulders tight over a sweater -> raise the underarm, add 1 across the back; lapel stands away -> shorten the neck edge, shrink the undercollar
- **Variants**: #reefer-jacket, #double-breasted-jacket, #duffle-coat, #overcoat
- **CLO3D**: DB front with decorative buttons; mesh 8 to 10 mm; stiff melton property
- **Confidence**: TO 12.1 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
