# Hood, three-piece {#hood-three-piece}

- **Aliases**: gusset hood, hood with centre panel, visor hood; fr: capuche trois pièces, capuche à pièce centrale
- **Family**: hood components
- **Recognize**: two side panels and a strip over the crown from the front edge to the nape, giving a hood that stands off the head; common on technical shells with a peaked brim
- **Pieces**: side panel (2), centre panel (gusset, 1), lining (3)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline (TO 13.3, SC 2.10).
  Take the two-piece hood; remove a strip of 4 to 6 from the top of each side piece along the head curve (SC 2.10), the centre panel is 8 to 12 wide at the top (6 to 10 at the front, 8 to 12 at the back in TO 13.3) tapering to 5 to 8 at the nape (SC 2.10); its length equals the length of the top curve of the side pieces from forehead to nape measured on the seam line (side seams about 45 each, TO 13.3). The panel is sewn to both side pieces, eased evenly; the CB seam of the side pieces runs below the panel at the nape. The seam over the head is taped on waterproof garments.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Start from #hood-two-piece.
  2. Remove 4 to 6 along the head curve from each side piece.
  3. Draw the centre panel with the widths above.
  4. Check the seam lengths of the sides and the panel equal.
- **Sewing order**: sew the panel to the two sides, then the CB seam below the panel, then lining, face edge, neckline
- **Finishes and notions**: wired brim, drawcord, seam tape
- **Fabrics**: nylon, shell fabrics; insert a stiffener in a peaked brim
- **Fit faults**: panel puckers at the curve -> ease evenly, clip allowances; hood falls back -> reduce the depth or add a nape dart
- **Variants**: #hood-two-piece, #ski-jacket, #softshell-jacket
- **CLO3D**: three pieces sewn into a bowl; mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: TO 13.3 and SC 2.10 (trade practice); the two sheets differ slightly; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
