# Anorak {#anorak}

- **Aliases**: pullover jacket, parka (pullover), cagoule (long), smock; fr: anorak, kway (brand name, short), blouson à capuche pull-over
- **Family**: casual and technical outerwear
- **Recognize**: pullover jacket with a half-length front zip or placket (a kangaroo pocket on the chest in some versions), hood, drawcord at the hem, patch pockets, waist cord
- **Pieces**: front (1 or 2 with a front slit), back (1), sleeve (2), hood (2) + lining, placket (1), kangaroo pocket (1 or 2), cuffs, hem channel, zip (short, 20 to 35 cm)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (no sheet; derived from TO 14 and hood sheets).
  Body: pullover, the front cut with a slit for the zip/placket from the neck down to the chest (25 to 35 long, typical), closed by a half zip or buttons; hood sewn to the neckline; the kangaroo pocket (if present) a rectangle at the hips with slanted openings; hem and cuff with elastic or cords.
- **Drafting**: ease 20 to 30 (nylon), drop shoulder 1 to 3, hood per #hood-two-piece, placket 3.5 to 4.5 wide, kangaroo pocket 28 to 34 wide by 20 to 24 high with slanted openings 14 to 16 (TO 16.3)
- **Sewing order**: front placket and zip, shoulders, hood, sleeves, side seams, channels
- **Finishes and notions**: short zip, drawcords, elastic cuffs, seam taping for waterproofs
- **Fabrics**: nylon, polyester, coated cotton
- **Fit faults**: hood pulls the placket -> lengthen the neck seam; head does not pass -> lengthen the zip opening
- **Variants**: #windbreaker, #parka-fishtail-m51, #raincoat-mac
- **CLO3D**: front slit with zip trim; hood sewn to the neckline
- **Confidence**: from memory; kangaroo dimensions from TO 16.3; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
