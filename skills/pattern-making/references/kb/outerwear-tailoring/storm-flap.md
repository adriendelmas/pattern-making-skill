# Storm flap {#storm-flap}

- **Aliases**: wind flap, zip placket, storm placket, front guard; fr: patte de recouvrement, pattes de protection, rabat pare-vent
- **Family**: outerwear components
- **Recognize**: a strip of fabric behind or over the zip along the front edge, closed with snaps or hook-and-loop; on parkas, field jackets, shells; on a trench coat a flap on the right chest
- **Pieces**: outer storm flap (1, on the fold or 2 mirrored), inner wind flap (1), interfacing
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline (TO 17.2 gives dimensions; the trench-coat chest storm flap is described in TO 11.3 step 5).
  Zip-front type: outer flap 3 to 4.5 finished (parka storm flap 5 to 6 in TO 14.3), as long as the zip edge from the collar base to above the hem, sewn on one front edge only, closing over the zip with snaps or Velcro 2 squares; the free edge is at the other front. Inner flap: 4 to 5 wide, cut on the left front behind the zip, filling the gap between teeth and body. Trench coat chest flap: shaped flap on the right front, 8 to 10 wide at the chest, attached at the shoulder and fixed on the shoulder side only, lying under the buttoned front (TO 11.3).
- **Drafting**:
  1. Cut the flap double on the fold (width finished x 2 + 2 x allowance), interface, round or square the bottom end.
  2. Sew snaps or Velcro at 8 to 12 spacing (typical, parkas).
- **Sewing order**: sew flap with the front facing seam on one side only, topstitch 0.6 from the edge, add the closures
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps, Velcro, drain/stitch details; seam-tape the zip on waterproof garments (TO 17.3)
- **Fabrics**: coated nylon, gabardine, waxed cotton
- **Fit faults**: flap lifts -> add a snap at the bottom; water enters at the zip -> widen the flap and add a chin guard
- **Variants**: #gun-flap, #parka-fishtail-m51, #trench-coat, #field-jacket-m65
- **CLO3D**: flap as a separate piece sewn to the front edge on one side only; snap points as decoration
- **Confidence**: TO 17.2 and 11.3 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
