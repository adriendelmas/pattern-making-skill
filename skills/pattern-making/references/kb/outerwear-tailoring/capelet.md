# Capelet {#capelet}

- **Aliases**: short cape, shoulder capelet, cape collar, pelerine; fr: capeline, pèlerine, petite cape
- **Family**: capes and cloaks
- **Recognize**: a short cape ending at the bust, upper arm or waist; may be part of a coat (cape-back)
- **Pieces**: capelet body (1 or 2 with CB seam), neck facing (1), collar optional, lining (1)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline; geometry as #cape: neck radius r = neck circumference / 6.28, hem radius R = r + length (cape tutorials).
  A smaller circular or semi-circular cut, length 20 to 40 from the neck (typical); a shoulder dart or a shaped neck curve may be used instead of full circle flare. Closure at the front neck.
- **Drafting**: as #cape with L = 20 to 40 and the hem radius; neck curve trimmed 1 to 2 for a better fit on the shoulder
- **Sewing order**: facing, lining, hem, closure
- **Finishes and notions**: hook and eye or a button, optional stand collar
- **Fabrics**: wool, velvet, lace, silk
- **Fit faults**: slides back -> shorten the neck curve; flares too much -> reduce the arc to a half circle
- **Variants**: #cape, #bolero, #shrug
- **CLO3D**: draw as a circle section; pin on the shoulders
- **Confidence**: geometry from the circle-cape formula; numbers estimates; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
