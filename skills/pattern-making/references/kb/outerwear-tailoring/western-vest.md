# Western vest {#western-vest}

- **Aliases**: western waistcoat, cowboy vest, suede vest; fr: gilet western
- **Family**: sleeveless outerwear
- **Recognize**: V neck, usually shaped yoke or pointed front hem, flap or welt chest pockets, snap or concho buttons, back yoke, leather or suede, optional fringe
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), yoke (2 front + 1 back optional), facing (2), pocket flaps (2), lining
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (trade construction).
  Similar to #waistcoat-suit but looser: armholes lower, fronts longer, hem point at CF; front yoke is a curved seam from the shoulder across the chest at 8 to 12 below the shoulder (typical), back yoke a straight or curved seam.
- **Drafting**: chest ease 12 to 20; yoke lines drawn as in a western shirt; front edge straight with a snap or button stance; hem 2 to 4 below the waist at the sides, point at CF
- **Sewing order**: yokes, fronts, shoulders, sides, lining, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps, concho buttons, topstitching, no hems on leather (edge finish), or binding
- **Fabrics**: suede, leather (glue and topstitch, no pins), wool, denim; leather has no ease, use the seam allowance minimal 0.6 to 1.0
- **Fit faults**: yoke pulls -> release the front yoke line 1 to 2; neck stands off -> shorten the neck edge
- **Variants**: #waistcoat-suit, #denim-trucker-jacket, #gilet-quilted
- **CLO3D**: use a leather property with low stretch
- **Confidence**: from memory; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
