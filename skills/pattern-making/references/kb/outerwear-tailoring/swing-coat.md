# Swing coat {#swing-coat}

- **Aliases**: A-line coat, trapeze coat, flared coat, tent coat; fr: manteau trapèze, manteau évasé, manteau « swing »
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: coat widening from a fitted or small shoulder to a wide hem; no waist; usually a yoke or a flared back; set-in or raglan sleeves; large buttons; swings when walking
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), collar (1), facing (2), pockets (2), lining
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (no sheet; common slash-and-spread flared coat).
  Front and back pieces widen from the armhole level to the hem by slashing the block at the bust and spreading: total hem sweep about 140 to 200 (estimate, unverified) with the side seams as straight diagonals; a back yoke is optional, the back may be cut with a CB seam and a flared panel from the shoulder blade level. The hem is straight.
- **Drafting**: coat block with ease 20 to 30 at the chest; side seam moved out at the hem by 15 to 25 each side (typical, to confirm on a toile); length 85 to 105; neck and sleeve as for the tailored coat
- **Sewing order**: back yoke if any, sides, shoulders, sleeves, collar, facing, hem (weighted)
- **Finishes and notions**: hem facing or deep hem 5 to 7, hem weight to avoid lift, buttons 25 to 35 mm
- **Fabrics**: wool, boucle, crepe; flared hems on the bias drop, hang the coat 24 hours before hemming
- **Fit faults**: hem lifts at the sides -> weight the hem; front sags -> stay the front edge with tape
- **Variants**: #cocoon-coat, #cape, #wrap-coat
- **CLO3D**: side seams at equal length; hem drape tested on an avatar in a walking pose
