# Raincoat, mac {#raincoat-mac}

- **Aliases**: mackintosh, mac, rain mac, waterproof coat, slicker; fr: imperméable, ciré (slicker), mac
- **Family**: coats, raincoats
- **Recognize**: single-breasted raincoat in coated or rubberised cotton, a fly front or concealed placket, raglan or set-in sleeves, a plain collar, bonded or taped seams, a hem a little below the knee
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), raglan or set-in sleeve (2), collar (1), undercollar (1), facing (2), fly (1), pocket flaps (2), bags (4), lining or none, optional belt
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (generic construction; Mackintosh originals use glued/taped seams, details not verified).
  Straight body, A-line from the underarm, length 100 to 115, raglan sleeves (#raglan-coat-sleeve), fly front 3.5 to 4.5, stand collar. Seams: glued, taped or double-needle with taping (TO 17.3 for the tape widths 16 to 22).
- **Drafting**: ease 24 to 32 over knit, underarm lowered 3 to 4, hem sweep 130 to 150; seam allowance 1.0 to 1.2 on taped seams; pockets in the seam (slash) or with a flap
- **Sewing order**: assemble flat seams, seal the seams with tape before crossing seams, collar, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: taped seams, snap or button fly, storm flap on the front, rubberised or PU zip covers
- **Fabrics**: coated cotton, rubberised cotton, PU-coated nylon; needles 70 to 80, stitch 2.5 to 3.0; avoid pins where holes show
- **Fit faults**: leaks at seams -> tape every seam through which water can pass; shoulder drags -> shift the raglan line
- **Variants**: #trench-coat, #anorak, #windbreaker, #waxed-jacket
- **CLO3D**: low-friction coated fabric property; taped seams are decorative
- **Confidence**: from memory and TO 17.3 for tape; unverified outline
- **Status**: unverified
