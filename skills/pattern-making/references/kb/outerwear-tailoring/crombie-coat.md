# Crombie coat {#crombie-coat}

- **Aliases**: covert coat, short topcoat, Crombie overcoat; fr: manteau Crombie, manteau « covert coat », pardessus court
- **Family**: coats, overcoats
- **Recognize**: shorter single-breasted topcoat with notch lapels, straight cut, stitched hem and cuffs (four lines of stitching on cuffs and hem, descriptive source), welted or flap pockets, a covert twill cloth; Crombie is also a brand name
- **Pieces**: as #overcoat (front, back, sleeves, collar, facings, flaps, lining), without a velvet collar
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (trade construction; features from The London Lounge and Wikipedia descriptions: short topcoat, notched lapels, single-breasted).
  Body: straight with little waist shaping. Length shorter than the Chesterfield (about mid-thigh to knee: 90 to 100 typical, unverified). Single-breasted with 3 or 4 buttons, front edge CF + 2.5 to 3, flap pockets, an optional ticket pocket. Four lines of topstitching at the cuffs and hem (descriptive). Collar and lapel as #notched-lapel with the coat stand 3 to 4.
- **Drafting**: as #overcoat with the length 90 to 100, straight cut, hem sweep 120 to 140 (typical), sleeve #two-piece-sleeve; topstitch the hem 0.6 from the edge in four lines for the traditional covert look
- **Sewing order**: as the overcoat; the lining half or fully, topstitching after the hem is pressed
- **Finishes and notions**: horn or leather buttons 25 to 30 mm, canvas, four-row stitching 
- **Fabrics**: covert twill (wool or cotton), melton; twill direction matched at the front and at the pockets
- **Fit faults**: as #overcoat; short length drops at the hem -> raise the front hem 0.5 to 1.0
- **Variants**: #overcoat, #chesterfield-coat, #polo-coat, #reefer-jacket
- **CLO3D**: as #overcoat; row stitching as topstitch lines
- **Confidence**: description-level (Wikipedia/forum); construction as the overcoat; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
