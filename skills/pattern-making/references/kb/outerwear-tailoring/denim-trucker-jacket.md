# Denim / trucker jacket {#denim-trucker-jacket}

- **Aliases**: jean jacket, Type III trucker, western denim jacket; fr: veste en jean, veste en denim, blouson type trucker
- **Family**: casual jackets
- **Recognize**: pointed front yokes, two chest flap pockets, two vertical seams down the back panel with a back yoke, waistband with adjuster tabs, button front, flat-felled seams with double topstitching, 2-piece sleeve with a button cuff (TO 16.2)
- **Pieces**: front (2), front yoke (2), back panel centre (1), back side panels (2), back yoke (1), waistband (2) with adjuster tabs, sleeve (2) + cuff (2) + placket (2), collar (2) + undercollar, chest pocket (2) + flap (2), button placket (cut on the front, folded double 3 to 4 wide), side pockets optional (2) (TO 16.2)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 16.2).
  Front yoke seam 8 to 10 below the shoulder, pointed or curved, the front body piece hangs from the yoke; back: centre back panel between two vertical seams (the seams pass from the back yoke to the waistband), back yoke 12 to 14 deep at CB, curved; waistband 4 to 5 high; sleeve two-piece, bicep ease 10 to 14, cuff 6 to 7 wide with 2 buttons. Patch chest pockets with flaps. Felled seams: first seam allowance 1.5, trim one side to 0.5, fold and topstitch; finished felled width 0.6.
- **Drafting**: block with ease 12 to 20 for rigid denim, yokes, back vertical seams, waistband, sleeve placket and cuff; seam allowance 1.0 for felled seams
- **Sewing order**: yokes, front, pockets and flaps, shoulders and sides, sleeves, collar, waistband, cuffs, buttonholes, buttons (rivet or jeans button)
- **Finishes and notions**: heavy topstitching thread, stitch length 3 to 3.5, rows 0.6 apart, bartacks at pocket corners, rivets or tack buttons
- **Fabrics**: 10 to 14 oz denim, stretch denim needs less ease and a stable woven interfacing at the placket; sanforised or raw: pre-shrink raw denim
- **Fit faults**: yoke pulls -> release the yoke line 1 to 2; waistband rides up -> increase length at the hem; sleeve restricts -> add a pitch
- **Variants**: #western-vest, #field-jacket-m65, #shacket, #biker-perfecto
- **CLO3D**: felled seams as topstitch lines; denim fabric property; mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: TO 16.2 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
