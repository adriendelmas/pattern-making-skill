# Field jacket (M-65) {#field-jacket-m65}

- **Aliases**: M65, M-65, military field coat, field coat; fr: veste de combat M65, veste militaire, M65
- **Family**: military and utility jackets
- **Recognize**: olive cotton-sateen (or cotton-nylon) hip-length jacket, four chest and hip flap pockets with snaps, epaulettes with buttons, front zip covered by a snap storm flap, upright collar with a hood concealed in it, internal drawstring at waist and hem, Velcro cuffs (retail descriptions)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1, with an action pleat in some models), sleeve (2, set-in), collar (outer + under, 2 to 3), hood (2, stowed in the collar), chest pockets (2) + hip pockets (2) with flaps (4), storm flap (outer 1 + inner 1), epaulettes (2), cuff tabs (2), waist channel and cord, hem channel, lining/liner
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (retail descriptions only: Rothco, The Real McCoy's, Standard & Strange; no military pattern drawing found; the M-65 is specified by MIL-C-11448 per one maker, spec not read).
  Boxy body with generous ease (designed oversized, carrying gear), length 78 to 82 (estimate); four bellows or plain patch pockets with flaps, snap closures, the chest pockets above the waist drawcord and the hip pockets below; front zip with an outer storm flap closed by snaps, an inner wind flap; collar upright with the concealed hood in a zip pocket at the collar; epaulettes buttoned at the shoulder; waist and hem drawcords in channels; sleeve with a cuff tab (Velcro).
- **Drafting**: ease 24 to 32; patch pockets per #bellows-pocket or flat patch 16 to 20 wide by 18 to 22 deep (estimate); waist channel 3.5 to 4 wide with eyelets 8 mm (TO 14.3); storm flap 3 to 4.5 finished (TO 17.2); epaulette 5 by 14 (TO 11.3)
- **Sewing order**: pockets on the flat fronts, front and back yokes/seams, zip with storm flap, collar with the hood pocket, channels, shoulders and sides, sleeves, cuff tabs, buttons/snaps
- **Finishes and notions**: coil or metal zip, snaps, drawcords, buttons for epaulettes, Velcro
- **Fabrics**: cotton sateen, cotton-nylon poplin, ripstop; liner button-in optional
- **Fit faults**: pocket flaps will not close -> flap depth +0.5 to 1; hood chokes the collar -> enlarge the collar pocket
