# Interfacing and canvas plan {#interfacing-canvas-plan}

- **Aliases**: canvas construction, chest piece, floating canvas, hair canvas plan, fusing plan; fr: entoilage, plan d'entoilage, toile de crin, thermocollage
- **Family**: tailoring components, interlinings
- **Recognize**: invisible from outside; a jacket that holds a roll at the lapel and a smooth chest has canvas or fusible stabilisation; half and full canvas differ by the length of the canvas
- **Pieces**: main canvas front (2), chest piece (2, 2 layers hair canvas), felt/domette wadding (2), back stay (1), hem canvas strips (bias), roll tape (2), edge tape (2), armhole tape (2), shoulder tape (2), undercollar canvas (2), pocket opening stays
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline (TO 5.1 to 5.4).
  Main canvas: shape of the front from the shoulder (and armhole) to the waist or hem; cut on the straight with a dart cut and overlapped. Chest piece: covers from the shoulder to the armhole bottom, about 18 to 20 wide and 24 to 28 high, with felt or wadding 3 to 5 mm on top. Layers outside to inside: shell front, main canvas, chest piece, shoulder pad and sleeve head, lining (TO 5.2). Half canvas: hair canvas from the shoulder to just below the chest, about to the pocket line; below fused. Full canvas: hair canvas floating from the shoulder to the hem with a hem canvas bias 4 to 6 deep. Fused: fusible interlining in 2 to 3 layers. Tapes: roll line tape 0.8 to 1.0 short over 20 to 24 cm (10 to 13 mm tape), front/lapel edge tape 1.0 to 1.5 short on the curve, armhole tape 0.5 to 1.0 short, shoulder stay straight, neck tape exact, back stay from the armhole to 12 below the nape on the straight grain, pocket stays 2 to 3 wide.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Trace the front without allowances for the canvas; mark the dart cut.
  2. Cut the chest piece and felt as a pair.
  3. Cut tape lengths from the seam lengths minus the shortening.
  4. Interface weights: fronts 120 to 180 g/m2 hair canvas or woven fusible; light shell 60 to 100 g/m2; hems bias woven fusible; knit fabrics knit fusible or tape at shoulder and front edge (TO 5.4).
- **Sewing order**: (1) pad stitch lapel and chest piece to the canvas, (2) tape the roll line and edges, (3) attach the canvas to the front by fell stitch, (4) join the seams, (5) hem canvas
- **Finishes and notions**: hair canvas (cotton or linen warp, hair weft), cotton or linen tape, wadding or felt, silk thread for pad stitching
- **Fabrics**: lightweight shells: fused or half canvas; firm wool: full canvas; sheers: no fusing
- **Fit faults**: lapel does not roll -> pad stitching too light, tape too long; bubbling -> poor fusing, switch to canvas; stiff chest -> too many layers
- **Variants**: #lining-patterns, #notched-lapel, #suit-jacket-single-breasted
- **CLO3D**: canvas as a stiffer piece beneath the shell or use the fusing option on the shell; collision thickness 0
- **Confidence**: TO 5 (trade practice with Minerva, Rampley, The Artefact pages); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
