# Polo coat {#polo-coat}

- **Aliases**: camel coat, camel hair polo coat, wrap-over polo coat; fr: manteau polo, manteau en poil de chameau
- **Family**: coats, overcoats
- **Recognize**: double-breasted camel-colour coat, often with a half belt or full belt, patch pockets, set-in or raglan sleeves, large lapels, buttoned across; originally worn by polo players
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), sleeve (2 or 2+2), collar (1), undercollar (2), facing (2), patch pockets (2), belt (1), belt loops (2 or 4), lining
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (secondary sources; TO 10.1 names the polo coat as belted camel hair with patch pockets).
  Body: straight with DB overlap 8 to 10, six buttons in two columns (typical), patch pockets large, set 8 to 10 below WL, belt half (back) or full. Raglan or set-in sleeves; collar notch or peak lapel, wide stance (8 to 10).
- **Drafting**: as #double-breasted-jacket scaled up to the coat: ease 20 to 28, length 100 to 110, patch pockets 18 to 22 by 20 to 24 (typical), belt 5 to 6 wide
- **Sewing order**: as #overcoat; patch pockets before the side seams; belt at the end
- **Finishes and notions**: horn buttons 28 to 32, hair canvas, lining half or full
- **Fabrics**: camel hair, wool cashmere blends; camel hair has a nap, cut all pieces in the same direction
- **Fit faults**: patch pockets pull -> interface; belt twists -> belt loops at the right level
- **Variants**: #overcoat, #chesterfield-coat, #ulster-coat, #pea-coat
- **CLO3D**: patch pockets as separate pieces; belt separate
- **Confidence**: from memory plus TO 10.1; unverified outline
- **Status**: unverified
