# Tailored coat {#tailored-coat}

- **Aliases**: fitted coat, women's tailored coat, princess coat, redingote-style coat; fr: manteau tailleur, manteau cintré, manteau princesse
- **Family**: coats, tailoring
- **Recognize**: a coat built like a jacket: notch or shawl collar, waist suppression by princess seams or darts, set-in two-piece sleeve, lining, mid-thigh to knee length, single- or double-breasted
- **Pieces**: centre front (2), side front (2), back (2, CB seam), side back (2), top sleeve (2), under sleeve (2), top collar (1), undercollar (2, CB seam), front facing (2), back neck facing (1), welt/flap pockets (2 flaps), pocket bags (4), canvas and chest piece (2), lining front (2), lining back (1 or 2), lining sleeve (4), interlining optional (FreeSewing Carlton: front, side, back, sleeves, collar, facing, flaps, welts, chest canvas; the full cutting list was not read)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (trade construction, TO 10 and 2.3.2).
  As #blazer-womens or #suit-jacket-single-breasted scaled up: princess seams from the shoulder (1/3 of the shoulder length from NP) or armhole through the bust point to the hem, side back seam from the back armhole about 7 to 9 below the shoulder line. The pieces are longer, with the hem sweep 130 to 160 full round (straight coat) by flaring the side seams 1.5 to 2.5 from the hip line. Facing 10 to 12 at the chest, 8 to 10 at the hem. Back vent or CB pleat 28 to 40.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Block per TO 1.3 (coat column): ease women 16 to 22 (fitted), 22 to 28 (easy); underarm lowered 2.5 to 4.0; shoulder raised pad + 0.5; neck widened 0.5 to 1.0.
  2. Length 90 to 110 from the nape (TO 1.5 for coat lengths).
  3. Princess seams and side back as the blazer; waist suppression 5 to 10.
  4. Lapel and collar #notched-lapel with the coat values (stand 3 to 4, fall 4.5 to 6).
  5. Sleeve #two-piece-sleeve with coat values (cap height 12 to 14, cap ease 3 to 5, cuff 16 to 18).
  6. Pockets: slit or flap 17 to 19 in the side seam or panel seam.
- **Sewing order**: TO 2.8 with a 5 to 7 hand-hemmed hem, hem weight optional, lining bagged with CB pleat 2.0
- **Finishes and notions**: canvas, shoulder pad 12 to 20 mm, sleeve head, hem chain, buttons 28 to 35 mm, lining 
- **Fabrics**: wool melton, wool-cashmere, double-faced wool; double-faced is unlined with hand-finished bound seams
- **Fit faults**: as TO 10.5 (drag across the back -> add 1.0 to 1.5 across the back width and a pleat; collar curl -> shrink the undercollar, tape the roll; hem swings -> add a hem weight)
- **Variants**: #overcoat, #blazer-womens, #wrap-coat, #pea-coat
- **CLO3D**: mesh 8 to 10 mm; princess seams at equal lengths; lining as inner layers; avatar shoulder pad placed manually
- **Confidence**: trade practice (TO 10); FreeSewing Carlton piece names partly checked; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
