# Cape {#cape}

- **Aliases**: circular cape, half-circle cape, shoulder cape, poncho cape, opera cape; fr: cape
- **Family**: capes and cloaks
- **Recognize**: sleeveless outer garment hanging from the shoulders, open at the front, with arm slits or openings; falls in folds from a shoulder curve; may be a circle or half-circle cut, or fitted at the shoulder with darts
- **Pieces**: cape body (1 half-circle on the fold, or 2 quarter-circles with a CB seam), neck facing (1), collar (optional), lining (1 equivalent), arm slits (2)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline; the circle-cape geometry is derived from the circle skirt (neck radius r = neck circumference / 6.28, Simply Sewing and Cottage Mama tutorials); the fitted shoulder-dart cape is described only in retail and book scans (DRCOS Short Cape 2 to 4: front, side sections and back, with darts on the side sections that define the shoulder line, Short Cape 3 without darts, Short Cape 4 with side darts and a stand collar).
  Circle cape: a half-circle (or three-quarter circle) with an inner neck circle of radius r = neck circumference / (2 pi) (for an 18 inch (46 cm) neck about 7.3 cm; rounded up and plus the seam allowance); the outer hem radius = r + cape length. The front edges are straight radii; CB is on the fold. The neck edge sews to a facing or collar. Arm slits are cut in the front panels at about 10 below the neck on the sides (position unverified). The fitted version: back panel, side panels with dart(s) forming the shoulder shape, front panel.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Measure the neck circumference N and the length L (shoulder to hem).
  2. r = N / 6.28; hem radius R = r + L.
  3. Draw a quarter circle of radius r (neck) and R (hem) for the half, double on the fold.
  4. For a shoulder fit, trim the neck curve 1 to 2 and add shoulder darts 3 to 4 in the front and back (estimate to confirm on a toile).
  5. Add a neck facing 5 to 6 wide cut from the neck curve.
  6. Hem: stabilise the bias at the hem and hang the cape for 24 hours before hemming.
- **Sewing order**: sew the CB seam if any, neck facing, lining to the facing, hem, arm slits bound, closure
- **Finishes and notions**: hook and eye or frog at the neck, optional tie, hem binding
- **Fabrics**: wool, velvet, satin; the bias area stretches, so hang before hemming; lining cut as the shell
- **Fit faults**: cape slides back -> shorten the neck curve, add a closure; folds uneven -> hem after hanging; neck gapes -> reduce the neck radius
- **Variants**: #capelet, #cloak, #opera-coat, #ulster-coat
- **CLO3D**: draw as a flat half-circle with a neck hole, pin at the shoulders; mesh 8 mm; cloth with a good drape setting
- **Confidence**: circle formula verified (online tutorials, geometric); fitted version described by retail sources only; numbers for darts are estimates; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
