# Shacket / overshirt {#shacket}

- **Aliases**: shirt-jacket, overshirt, workshirt jacket, CPO jacket; fr: surchemise, chemise-veste
- **Family**: casual jackets
- **Recognize**: heavy shirt cut like a jacket: collar with or without stand, front placket with buttons, chest and sometimes hip patch pockets, dropped shoulder, curved hem, worn open over a T-shirt
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 on fold, with yoke 1 or 2), sleeve (2) + cuff (2) + placket (2), collar (2), stand (2, optional), patch pockets (2 to 4), flaps optional, button placket (cut on)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (shirt construction per the shirt entries in menswear-workwear-sport.md, e.g., #work-shirt, #casual-shirt-mens). 
  Yoke at the back (curved or straight), sleeve set into a dropped shoulder (cap ease 0 to 1), body length 70 to 80, side vents optional, curved hem, heavy fabric means no pleats at the yoke.
- **Drafting**: shirt block with ease 24 to 40 (oversize), dropped shoulder 2 to 6 (TO 16.3), sleeve cuff 6 to 7 wide; patch pockets 16 to 19 wide by 18 to 22 deep (TO 8.5)
- **Sewing order**: yoke, pockets, shoulder, sleeves flat, side and sleeve seams, collar, cuff, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: flat-felled or overlocked seams, topstitching, buttons 15 to 18 mm
- **Fabrics**: wool melton, flannel, heavy twill; thick fabric needs fewer layers at the collar
- **Fit faults**: collar stand rolls -> increase stand; hem rides up -> lengthen
- **Variants**: #work-shirt, #casual-shirt-mens, #denim-trucker-jacket, #safari-jacket
- **CLO3D**: shirt pieces with a button placket; mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: from memory (shirt-based); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
