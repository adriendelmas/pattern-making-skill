# Waxed jacket {#waxed-jacket}

- **Aliases**: wax cotton jacket, Barbour-type jacket, oilskin jacket, waxed Bedale; fr: veste cirée, veste en coton ciré
- **Family**: countryside outerwear
- **Recognize**: waxed cotton shell, corduroy collar, patch or bellows pockets with flaps, a storm flap with snaps, a tartan or check lining optional, a hood optional, pocket hand warmers, a belted version (Bedale/Beaufort differ)
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2, set-in or 2-piece), collar (1 + corduroy undercollar), facing (2), pockets (patch 2 + flaps 2 + hand warmers), storm flap (1), cuff tabs, lining (optional)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (no sheet; uses #bellows-pocket and #storm-flap).
  Hip-length body with a back yoke and a set-in sleeve (cap ease 1 to 2); wax cloth does not ease well so the cap ease is minimal; seams flat-felled or taped; corduroy collar with snaps.
- **Drafting**: ease 24 to 34 over knit, pockets per TO 8.5 and #bellows-pocket, storm flap 3 to 4.5, hem 4 to 5, seam allowance 1.5 for felled seams
- **Sewing order**: pockets, yoke, shoulders, sleeves, collar, felled seams, hem, snaps; use a wide stitch length 3.0 to 3.5 (holes show in wax)
- **Finishes and notions**: brass snaps and zip, corduroy collar, tartan lining
- **Fabrics**: waxed cotton, oilskin; hand-sewing needles damage the wax, so do not rip stitches; no pressing (cold-set seams with a mallet)
- **Fit faults**: stiff cloth creases at the elbow -> add elbow ease; stitch holes leak -> tape or re-wax
- **Variants**: #quilted-jacket, #field-jacket-m65, #raincoat-mac
- **CLO3D**: stiff coated cotton property; mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: from memory; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
