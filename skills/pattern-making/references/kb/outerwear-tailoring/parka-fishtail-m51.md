# Parka, fishtail (M-51) {#parka-fishtail-m51}

- **Aliases**: fishtail parka, M-1951 parka, M51, Mods parka; fr: parka M51, parka queue de poisson
- **Family**: military and cold-weather outerwear
- **Recognize**: hip- to thigh-length hooded parka whose back is longer than the front and ends in a fishtail (a point with a small V) that can be folded up between the legs and snapped; waist and hem drawcords; button-in body liner and hood liner; fur trim on the detachable hood liner (retail and Heddels descriptions)
- **Pieces**: front (2, with a yoke), back (1) with a tail extension, yoke (1), sleeve (2), hood (2) + hood liner (2), hood trim (1), pocket patches (2) + flaps (2), zip storm flap (2) + inner flap (1), facing (2), waist channel (1 to 2), lining front (2), back (1), sleeve (2), wadding (TO 14.2), inner cuff (2), fishtail tail (1, optional)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified as an outline; descriptions from museum and retail pages (M-51: back longer than front and curving to a point with a small V; shell buttons around sides, neck, hood and cuffs to attach liners; later models with one cuff button; museum record: back length 42 inch, underarm width 31 inch for one 1953 shell; reproduction size-medium chart: front length 36.5 inch, shoulder 24.25, chest 59, sleeve 25; the basis of these garment measurements is not stated) – these are single examples.
  Body: front hem straight, back extended into the tail (length beyond the front hem not known); hip pockets patch with flap (18 to 22 by 20 to 24 with a 2 bellows, TO 14.3), waist channel 3.5 to 4 wide with drawcord and eyelets 8 mm, front zip with a storm flap 5 to 6 wide (TO 14.3), hood per #hood-two-piece/#hood-three-piece sewn to the neckline and detachable fur trim. Snap tab at the tail to fasten it between the legs.
- **Drafting**: TO 14.3: ease 24 to 32, underarm lowered 3 to 5, length 85 to 100 at the front, tail extension (estimate to confirm on a toile), sleeves set-in 2-piece or 1-piece with a curve, bicep ease 16 to 24, cuff 14 to 16 flat, inner cuff 8 to 10 wide cut at 85 percent
- **Sewing order**: pockets, yokes, zip and flaps, shoulders and sides, sleeves, hood, channel, tail, buttons for liners
- **Finishes and notions**: drawcords, snaps, buttons for liners, fur trim (faux)
- **Fabrics**: cotton sateen (first model), poplin; wadding liner with button-in lining
- **Fit faults**: TO 14.4: rain at the zip -> storm flap and chin guard; shell drags on lining -> lining 1 to 2 larger on every seam; hood sags -> stay tape on the neck seam
- **Variants**: #field-jacket-m65, #anorak, #puffer-jacket, #hood-two-piece
- **CLO3D**: back tail as a separate extension piece or integral; hood sewn to the neckline, mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: TO 14 (trade practice) and web descriptions of the M-51; tail dimensions not known; outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
