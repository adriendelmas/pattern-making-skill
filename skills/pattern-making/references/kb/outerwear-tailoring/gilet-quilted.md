- **Aliases**: bodywarmer, quilted vest, puffer vest, down vest; fr: doudoune sans manches
- **Family**: padded sleeveless outerwear
- **Recognize**: sleeveless puffer or quilted jacket, front zip, high collar or stand, quilt channels, binding at the armholes
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), collar (1), facing (2), pocket welts or zip pockets (2), armhole binding (2), lining (3)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (trade construction; channel mathematics from TO 15.2).
  As the puffer body without sleeves: front panels with channels parallel to the hem, continuous across side seams, armhole depth 2 to 3 lower than the block to leave room under the arm, neck with a stand collar 6 to 12 high.
- **Drafting**: ease per TO 1.2 (puffer 30 to 45; gilet 24 to 36 typical because worn over a knit), add 6.3 x loft rule (TO 1.1), channel width 8 to 12, take-up per TO 15.2 (5 to 10 percent synthetic sew-through)
- **Sewing order**: quilt panels, assemble, bind armholes, zip, collar, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: zip number 5, binding at the armholes, snap storm flap optional
- **Fabrics**: ripstop nylon, polyester, with wadding 60 to 100 g/m2
- **Fit faults**: armhole gaps -> raise the armhole 1; garment shortens -> add the take-up
