# Quilted jacket {#quilted-jacket}

- **Aliases**: barn jacket (quilted), diamond-quilted jacket, wadded jacket, Liddesdale; fr: veste matelassée
- **Family**: padded outerwear
- **Recognize**: jacket with a quilted shell (diamond or channel stitch) over wadding, stand or shirt collar, zip or snap front, welt or flap pockets, corduroy collar optional
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), collar (1), facing (2), pockets, lining (3) (as the puffer, TO 15.3)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 15.2 for sew-through take-up).
  Pre-quilted yardage cut as a normal jacket, with stitch lines passing across the seams; take-up 5 to 8 percent for channels 8 to 12 wide with synthetic wadding 60 to 100 g/m2 (TO 15.2); diamonds 6 to 8 lose 6 to 10 percent in length and in width. Cut pieces from fabric after quilting and trim the layers before seaming.
- **Drafting**: ease 24 to 36; mark channel lines at the finished spacing; pre-quilt larger blanks and cut the final shape; seam allowance 1.0 to 1.5
- **Sewing order**: quilt blanks, cut, assemble with bound or taped seams, collar, zip, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: bound seams, corduroy collar, snaps
- **Fabrics**: cotton or nylon with wadding; wash and pre-shrink
- **Fit faults**: shortens after quilting -> add take-up; bulky seams -> trim wadding from the allowances
- **Variants**: #puffer-jacket, #gilet-quilted, #waxed-jacket
- **CLO3D**: padding fabric property; stitch lines as internal lines
- **Confidence**: TO 15.2 (formula geometric, take-up trade values); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
