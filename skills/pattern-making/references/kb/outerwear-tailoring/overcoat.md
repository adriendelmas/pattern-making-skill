# Overcoat {#overcoat}

- **Aliases**: topcoat, greatcoat (military), top coat, winter coat (generic); fr: pardessus, manteau d'hiver
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: knee-length or longer coat worn over a jacket, notch lapels, set-in sleeves or raglan, flap or slit pockets, back vent, usually single-breasted (TO 10.1)
- **Pieces**: front (2), side front (2, optional), back (2, CB seam), side back (2), top sleeve (2), under sleeve (2), top collar (1), undercollar (2, felt), front facing (2), back neck facing (1), pocket flaps (2 to 4), welts (2), bags (4), chest welt (1), canvas and chest pieces (2), lining (2 front, 1 or 2 back, 4 sleeve), interlining optional, shoulder pad (1 pair), hem canvas (TO 10.2)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 10.2, 10.3).
  Body: straight or slightly fitted, side seams flared 1.5 to 2.5 each from the hip line, hem sweep 130 to 160 (full round). Front: CF + wrap 3, DB wrap 8 to 10. Back: CB seam (shaped slightly if fitted) with the vent or a CB box pleat 4 to 6 (optional); back vent or pleat length 28 to 40. Armhole lowered 3.0 to 4.0 from the block; shoulder widened 1.0 to 1.5 and raised pad + 0.5; sleeve two-piece with coat values. Collar and lapel #notched-lapel with the stand 3 to 4. Pockets: flap 17 to 19, slit pockets at the side seam angled 2 to 3.
- **Drafting**: TO 10.3 (steps 1 to 9): block adapted, length 100 to 110, waist suppression 5 to 10 (none for a Balmacaan), front, collar, sleeve, pockets, vent, facing
- **Sewing order**: TO 2.8, with a wide hand-hemmed hem (5 to 7), optional hem weight, lining with jump pleat 3
- **Finishes and notions**: canvas, shoulder pad 12 to 20, buttons 28 to 35 mm, hem tape, lining 
- **Fabrics**: wool melton, cashmere, tweed, flannel; heavy cloths need larger turn of cloth (0.5 to 0.8)
- **Fit faults**: TO 10.5 (drag across the back, collar curl, hem swing, sleeve tight over jacket)
- **Variants**: #chesterfield-coat, #crombie-coat, #ulster-coat, #polo-coat, #tailored-coat
- **CLO3D**: mesh 10 to 15 mm for loose coats; layers: lining separate; melton fabric property thick and stiff
- **Confidence**: TO 10 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
