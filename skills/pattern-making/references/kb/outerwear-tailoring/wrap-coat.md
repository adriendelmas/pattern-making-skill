# Wrap coat {#wrap-coat}

- **Aliases**: belted wrap coat, robe coat, dressing-gown coat; fr: manteau portefeuille, manteau cache-cœur, manteau peignoir
- **Family**: coats
- **Recognize**: no buttons or hidden snaps; the front crosses 20 to 30 at the waist and is closed by a belt or tie; shawl or wide notch collar; side-seam pockets (TO 12.2)
- **Pieces**: front (2, the left front much wider), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), collar or shawl collar (1 to 2), facing (2), belt (1), belt loops (2), pocket bags in the side seams (TO 12.2)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 12.2).
  Front overlap 20 to 30 at the waist beyond CF, the front edge drawn as a soft curve from the neck to the hem; the right front goes to (or close to) the left side seam, the left front is wide and crosses over to the right side seam; an inside tie at the waist on the left side secures the underlap; belt 6 to 8 wide. Neckline: shawl collar per #shawl-collar or wide notch. Sleeves set-in, raglan or dropped shoulder. The facing runs along the front edge and shaped hem.
- **Drafting**: ease 20 to 30; overlap 20 to 30 at the waist; inside tie; belt 6 to 8 wide, length waist + 50 to 80 (typical); collar #shawl-collar; hem curve
- **Sewing order**: facing, collar, shoulder seams, sleeves, side seams with pockets, belt and loops, hem
- **Finishes and notions**: inside tie, belt, hook-and-eye or snap at the wrap, optional hidden button
- **Fabrics**: wool, cashmere, double-faced wool (unlined, bound seams), camel hair
- **Fit faults**: coat opens at the waist -> add a snap or inside tie, increase overlap; bunching at the belt -> reduce the wrap at the waist
- **Variants**: #shawl-collar, #tailored-coat, #kimono-jacket
- **CLO3D**: inside tie as a short strip pinned; belt as a separate piece; wrap front flat, symmetrical pieces are not mirrored
- **Confidence**: TO 12.2 (trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
