# Waistcoat, suit {#waistcoat-suit}

- **Aliases**: vest (US), suit waistcoat, formal waistcoat; fr: gilet de costume
- **Family**: tailoring, sleeveless
- **Recognize**: sleeveless, V-front buttoning (4 to 6 buttons), pointed or straight hem, welt pockets on the front, adjustable back strap with buckle, back in lining fabric or shell, narrow shoulders
- **Pieces**: front (2) in shell, back (2) in lining or shell with CB seam and strap, front facing (2, or lining the front completely), welt pockets (2 or 4), pocket bags (2 to 4), back strap (2) with buckle, lining front (2), back lining (2) (FreeSewing Wahid: fronts, backs, front facings, pocket welts, pocket facings in main; front linings, backs, pocket bags in lining; fusible fronts and pocket interfacing)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (trade construction; piece list from the FreeSewing Wahid cutting list).
  Front: a V-neck cut from the shoulder (neck point to the shoulder tip about 3 to 5 wide, to confirm) down the front edge to the button stance; armhole cut in (raised, small), side seam sewn to the back side edge, front edge below the V is straight or slightly curved to a point at the hem (point 2 to 5 below the side hem). Back: two pieces joined by a CB seam, the back neck is a narrow curve; the CB waist seam is split above the waist and a strap crosses the gap at WL.
- **Drafting**:
  1. Chest ease 6 to 10 (Mueller & Sohn waistcoat set cites 8.0, to confirm), length from nape to hem point 55 to 65 (typical), shoulder seam shifted and narrowed.
  2. Raise and cut in the armhole 1 to 2 (typical) so the shirt does not show.
  3. Front: draw the V to the first button (BP near sternum), button stance 5 to 6 buttons spaced evenly 7 to 9 apart (typical), overlap 1.5 to 2.0.
  4. Back strap: 2 pieces, 2.5 to 3 wide, length to span 8 to 12 adjustment.
  5. Welt pockets: mouth 10 to 11.5 at a slight slant, at the pocket line.
- **Sewing order**: darts, welt pockets on the front, interface, sew front to lining along the front edge and armhole, turn through the shoulder or back opening, side seams, back strap, hem, buttonholes
- **Finishes and notions**: bound or faced fronts, bridle tape along front edge and hem (Victorian practice), buttons 15 to 20 mm, metal buckle 20 to 25 mm
- **Fabrics**: suiting, flannel, linen; the back in satin or cotton lining
- **Fit faults**: V front gapes -> shorten the front edge 0.5 to 1.0 or add a dart; armhole gapes -> take in or tape; hem lifts -> add length at CF
- **Variants**: #western-vest, #gilet-quilted, #suit-jacket-single-breasted
- **CLO3D**: sleeveless; sew the back strap at CB; mesh 8 mm
- **Confidence**: piece set verified (FreeSewing Wahid, Minerva guides described on the web); outline and numbers trade practice; Mueller & Sohn ease 8.0 from a retail description
- **Status**: unverified
