# Puffer / down jacket {#puffer-jacket}

- **Aliases**: down jacket, doudoune, baffle jacket, quilted jacket (synthetic fill); fr: doudoune, veste matelassée, parka duvet
- **Family**: padded outerwear
- **Recognize**: horizontal channels (baffles) stitched across body and sleeves, high collar, front zip, elasticated cuffs, a hood optional (TO 15.1)
- **Pieces**: shell front (2), shell back (1), sleeve (2, 2-piece or one-piece), hood (2) + lining (2), collar (1), facing (2), pocket bags (2 pairs), baffle wall for each channel (front 2, back 1, sleeve 2), lining front (2), back (1), sleeve (2), zip, storm flap (2) (TO 15.3)
- **Shape**: Source: unverified (TO 15.2 to 15.4; no drawn pattern read).
  Channels horizontal on the body at the same level front and back for continuity at the side seams; side seams located at the end of a channel or on a stitch line; sleeve channels at right angles to the sleeve axis or in a spiral; cap height reduced 1.5 and cap ease 1 to 2. Shell panels are cut longer across the channels by the take-up (sew-through synthetic 5 to 8 percent; down baffle 10 to 17 percent; thick loft baffle 15 to 20 percent, TO 15.2). Arc length of a filled channel: `arc = w + 8 s^2 / (3 w)` with w the stitched width and s the rise of each face (TO 15.2).
- **Drafting**: TO 15.4: ease 30 to 45 (or add 6.3 x loft), underarm lowered 4 to 6, shoulder drop 1 to 3, neck widened 2 and raised 1; channel width 8 to 14 (down), 6 to 12 (synthetic); seam allowance 1.0 to 1.5; funnel collar 10 to 14
- **Sewing order**: TO 15.5 (baffled): sew baffle walls to shell and lining, join shell channels to the baffle wall leaving the ends open, fill each channel by weight, close channels, side seams and shoulder seams, sleeves, collar, hood, zip, hem and cuffs, seal stitching holes for waterproof garments
- **Finishes and notions**: zip No. 5, elastic cuffs, storm flap, seam tape for waterproof versions (TO 17.3)
- **Fabrics**: ripstop nylon or polyester (down-proof), down or synthetic wadding 60 to 100 g/m2; test a swatch for take-up
- **Fit faults**: TO 15.6 (down migrates -> narrower channels or baffles; garment short after fill -> add take-up; seams burst -> larger allowance and shorter stitch; sleeves tight -> add bicep ease 2 x loft; cold lines at the stitch -> baffles)
- **Variants**: #quilted-jacket, #gilet-quilted, #ski-jacket, #parka-fishtail-m51
- **CLO3D**: pressure/padding setting for channels, channel lines as internal lines, mesh 5 to 6 mm (TO 18)
- **Confidence**: TO 15 (formulas geometric; channel widths trade practice); outline unverified
- **Status**: unverified
