# Rugby Shirt {#rugby-shirt}

- **Aliases**: rugby jersey, rugger shirt, hooped rugby top, heavyweight polo jersey (fr: maillot de rugby, polo rugby); (de: Rugbytrikot)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit tops
- **Recognize**: a heavy jersey with broad horizontal stripes, a contrasting twill collar (white or plain) with a short placket and rubber buttons, ribbed cuffs, side vents.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2), collar (upper 1, under 1), collar stand (optional), placket (2), cuffs (2), neck tape (1), side vent facings (2, optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Retail listings describe a twill collar, a three-button placket (concealed in some models), rubber buttons that are meant to come undone, ribbed cuffs, twill tape at the neck and side vents or underarm gussets (see Sources). General shape: polo-shirt placket structure (see #polo-shirt-knit) with a woven collar, a long-sleeve set-in or raglan sleeve, chest ease 10 to 18 cm (typical), side vents 6 to 10 cm, hem slightly longer at the back (2 to 3 cm). Stripes: place the pattern so that the stitching lines fall at the middle of the dominant stripe, and the buttonholes run in the direction of the stripe (sewing guidance found in the sources). Sew rule: as the polo; collar sewn to the neckline; twill tape covers the neck seam.
- **Drafting**: as #polo-shirt-knit with a woven collar, 10 to 12 cm placket, 3 buttons; cuffs from technique sheet 3.5.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders taped; placket; collar; sleeves; side seams and vents; cuffs; hem; buttons.
- **Finishes and notions**: rubber buttons, shoulder tape, twill tape at the neck, bartacks at the placket base.
- **Fabrics**: heavy cotton jersey, interlock, piqué, yarn-dyed stripes; the woven collar is cotton twill.
- **Fit faults**: collar stands away -> collar too long; stripes mismatch at the side seams -> cut with an allowance.
- **Variants**: #polo-shirt-knit, #golf-polo, #football-jersey.
- **CLO3D**: stripes as a texture or yarn-dyed print aligned to pattern pieces; collar a separate woven layer.
- **Confidence**: features: retail listings and a pattern note (Sources). Numbers: typical, unverified.
