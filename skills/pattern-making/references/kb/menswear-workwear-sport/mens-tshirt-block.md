# Men's T-Shirt Block {#mens-tshirt-block}

- **Aliases**: tee, crew neck tee, men's knit block (fr: tee-shirt homme, base tee-shirt)
- **Family**: knit tops
- **Recognize**: pullover with a crew-neck band, set-in short sleeves, side seams, a straight hem with a coverstitch.
- **Pieces**: front (1 on fold), back (1 on fold), sleeve (2), neckband (1), shoulder tape (optional).
- **Shape**: drawn pattern: not available; follows the technique sheet 4.1. Front: a rectangle with a shaped armhole (depth measured from SNP: man's armhole depth is greater than the woman's), shoulder slope 15 to 20 degrees (typical, unverified), front neck drop 7 to 8 cm and back neck drop 2 cm (technique sheet, women's size 38; men's slightly wider neck: half-width 8 to 9 cm, typical), a straight side seam with a hem drop of 1 cm at the side. Sleeve: a low cap 10 to 13 cm and cap ease 0 to 1 cm. Sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder (taped); neckband ring -> neckline; sleeve -> armhole flat; underarm and side seam in one run.
- **Drafting**: chest ease 4 to 8 cm for a relaxed tee on moderate jersey (technique sheet); reduction F = 3 percent on the body for a closer fit; band ratio 0.85 for self-jersey (0.75 for rib); hem allowance 2 cm.
- **Sewing order**: stabilize shoulders; sew shoulders; sew the band ring; attach band; sleeves flat; side seam; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: shoulder tape 6 mm, coverstitch hem, differential feed 1.0 to 1.5.
- **Fabrics**: single jersey, interlock; stretch drives ease.
- **Fit faults**: see technique sheet 4.1 (neckline gapes -> shorten the band 5 percent; neckline waves -> lengthen the band 3 to 5 percent; shoulder seam rolls forward -> tape it).
- **Variants**: polo #polo-shirt-knit, Henley #henley-placket-shirt, sweatshirt (technique sheet 4.3).
- **CLO3D**: knit preset with weft/warp stretch as measured; mesh 8 mm; band as a separate piece shorter than the neckline.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet 4.1 (from published practice, typical).
