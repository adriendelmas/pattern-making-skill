- Technique sheets in this skill: `techniques/skirts-trousers.md` (B1 trouser block, B3 jeans, B4 shorts, B5 leggings, B6 jumpsuits, B7 elastic waists, B8 pockets, B9 zip fly), `techniques/sleeves-collars.md` (collars, cuffs, plackets), `techniques/knits-lingerie-swimwear.md` (knit blocks, bands, T-shirt 4.1, polo 4.2, hoodie 4.3, leggings 6.4, compression 6.6, activewear 6.7).
- EN ISO 20471 minimum areas (fluorescent background 0.14 / 0.50 / 0.80 m2 and retroreflective 0.10 / 0.13 / 0.20 m2 for classes 1 / 2 / 3), from supplier and testing-lab pages: Centexbel (centexbel.be, "High visibility clothing according to EN ISO 20471:2013+A1:2016"), Fristads (fristads.com, "EN ISO 20471"), Elis (elis.com, "High-visibility ISO 20471:2013+A1:2016"), HSA Ireland (hsa.ie, "High visibility clothing for use around moving vehicles"). The standard text itself was not read.
- IBJJF uniform measurements (lapel 1.3 cm, collar width 5 cm, sleeve opening 7 cm): ibjjf.com/uniform, and elitesports.com ("What are the IBJJF legal competition gi requirements"), Wikipedia "Brazilian jiu-jitsu gi". Sleeve and trouser tolerances (2 cm versus 5 cm) differ between secondary sources; the current IBJJF rule book was not opened.
- Wetsuit panels, thickness zones, seam methods (GBS, flatlock, tape): srface.com ("Wetsuit design"), theinertia.com, surfertoday.com, ultimatespearfishing.com, patents.justia.com (US 2007/0294797), cleanlinesurf.com glossary.

- Scrubs: lizhaywood.com.au (zero-waste scrub top), allsaintsgrayswood.org (free scrubs instructions), so-sew-easy.com, stylearc.com (Flossy woven scrubs), folkwear.com (elastic and drawstring), lbas.lboro.ac.uk (drawstring pants).
- Rugby shirt: taylorstitch.com, jpeterman.com, vintagepatterns.fandom.com (Kwik Sew 704), Merchant & Mills rugby pattern, ellieandmac.com (stripe matching).
- Ski pants and salopettes: Rab, Black Crows, Roxy, Volcom, Toni Sailer retail pages (features only); threadloop.app (Green Pepper ski pants).
- Camp collar: dresspatternmaking.com ("Basic collar (camp collar)"), one-piece convertible collar drafted from the bodice neck curves; used for the construction logic only, no drawn pattern was reproduced.
- Section-level confidence: shapes of all workwear, sportswear and the shirt-family entries are trade practice and typical values; no entry in this file is taken from a drawn published pattern, and each says "unverified" where that applies.
