# Military Tunic (Basic Service Tunic) {#military-tunic}

- **Aliases**: service tunic, battledress blouse, field tunic, four-pocket tunic, uniform jacket (fr: vareuse militaire, veste d'uniforme); (de: Waffenrock, Feldbluse)
- **Family**: uniforms, men's jackets
- **Recognize**: a hip-length jacket with a stand-fall or stand collar, a button front concealed or open, four pockets (two chest, two hip, often with box pleats and flaps), shoulder straps (epaulettes), set-in two-piece sleeves, belt hooks or a belt.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2 with CB seam), side body (2, optional), sleeve (2 pieces each, upper and under), collar (upper and under 2), chest pockets (2, with pleat and flap), hip pockets (2, with pleat and flap), shoulder straps (2), front facing (2), lining (front 2, back 2, sleeve 2), cuff tabs (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. No specific national pattern was used; this is a generic description from trade practice. Body: a fitted jacket block with a CB seam and side panels, waist suppression 4 to 8 cm (typical), skirt flared 6 to 10 cm per side; chest pockets 11 to 13 cm wide x 14 to 16 cm tall with a box pleat and a pointed flap; hip pockets 15 to 17 cm x 16 to 18 cm; shoulder strap 5 cm wide x 14 to 16 cm long, sewn at the armhole and buttoned at the neck end; collar stand 4 to 5 cm; a plain stand collar for combat tunics, a stand-fall for dress. Sew rule: as the tailored jacket (see `techniques/tailoring-outerwear.md` and #blazer-mens): front shoulder -> back shoulder; two-piece sleeve -> armhole.
- **Drafting**: 1. Men's jacket block with chest ease 12 to 18 cm (fitted) or 20 to 26 cm (battledress). 2. Add pocket positions and pleats. 3. Lengthen to mid-hip. 4. Add strap at the shoulder point. 5. Collar draft as a jacket stand-fall.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; shoulder straps; back and side seams; front and facing; collar; lining; sleeves; hem; buttons.
- **Finishes and notions**: interfacing in front and collar; shoulder straps interfaced; lining; brass or plastic buttons.
- **Fabrics**: wool serge, gabardine, cotton drill, rip-stop; permanent-press blends.
- **Fit faults**: collar chokes -> N plus 1 to 2 cm; sleeve twists -> pitch; pocket flaps flip -> adjust the stiffener.
- **Variants**: #police-shirt, #blazer-mens, #work-jacket, #field-jacket-m65.
- **CLO3D**: layered shell and lining; heavy wool; pocket flaps as layered patterns; epaulettes as separate pieces.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; generic, unverified.
