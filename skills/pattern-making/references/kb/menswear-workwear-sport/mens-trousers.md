# Men's Trousers (Basic) {#mens-trousers}

- **Aliases**: pants, slacks, flat-front trousers, chinos (fr: pantalon homme)
- **Family**: trousers, men's
- **Recognize**: a fly front with a zipper, a waistband with belt loops, side-seam or slanted pockets, two jetted back pockets, a center crease on dress styles, a hem straight or with a cuff.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1, with a CF extension), curtain/waistband lining (1), fly extension on the right front (grown-on), fly facing (1, left front), fly shield (1, under the zipper), pocket bag (2 pairs: facing + bag), back pocket jetting (2 sets), belt loops (5 to 7), hem facing (optional), interfacing.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Based on the technique sheet B1 (women's block, numbers differ for men: crotch extension front H/20 to H/16, back H/10 to H/8, textbook ranges; men's seat has a larger back extension and a longer rise). The front piece has a CF edge that extends above the crotch to the waist as the fly line; the left front carries the fly facing sewn to the CF edge and the right front carries the extension with the fly shield; the crotch curve front is a J or L curve and the back is a deeper curve with the back crotch point beyond the front. Sew rules: front crotch seams are stitched to the fly stop and then both are joined; back crotch seam (both backs) -> from the fly stop around to CB; side seams and inseams -> equal lengths; waistband -> waist seam.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with ease H + 8 to 12 cm for a classic men's cut (typical, unverified); the technique sheet B1 methods A and B for the grid. 2. Fly: zipper 16 to 18 cm for men (sheet B3); extension 3.5 to 4 cm; shield 6 to 8 cm wide. 3. Waistband: length = waist + ease + extension; height 3.5 to 4.5 cm (typical). 4. Belt loops 1 to 1.3 cm finished, 5 to 7 loops. 5. Hem allowance 3 to 4 cm.
- **Sewing order**: darts or pleats and pockets; fly; back pockets; front crotch seams to the fly stop; back crotch seam; side seams; inseams; waistband with curtain; belt loops; hem; buttons, hook and bar.
- **Finishes and notions**: overlocked or bound seams, pressed open; zipper (metal or nylon coil, 16 to 18 cm); button for the waistband; hook and bar; curtain/waistband lining with a grip strip.
- **Fabrics**: wool, cotton twill, gabardine, linen; stretch woven needs lower seat ease; napped fabrics need all pieces cut in one direction.
- **Fit faults**: see technique sheet B1 (smile lines -> crotch short; whiskers -> thigh tight; baggy seat -> back extension long).
- **Variants**: dress trousers #dress-trousers, jeans (technique sheet B3), shorts #mens-shorts, pleated fronts (technique sheet B2).
- **CLO3D**: sew front to back at the side and inseam, then the crotch; start from the A-pose; the fly as a layered piece.
- **Confidence**: verified (technique sheet B1 methods, derived from published drafts); men's ease and fly numbers: typical.
