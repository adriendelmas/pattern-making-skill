# Tennis Skirt {#tennis-skirt}

- **Aliases**: sport skort, tennis skort, pleated tennis skirt, padel skirt (fr: jupe de tennis, jupe-short); (de: Tennisrock)
- **Family**: sportswear, skirts
- **Recognize**: a short pleated or flared skirt, an elastic or wide-band waist, a built-in short or brief, sometimes a ball pocket, side seam panels.
- **Pieces**: skirt front and back (2) or wrap panel, waistband (1), inner short front and back (2), ball pockets (optional), pleat inserts (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. Pleated version: a flat panel with knife pleats 2 to 3 cm deep (typical, technique sheet A6) pressed or stitched down to the hip; flared version: a short A-line or skater (see #a-line-skirt, #skater-skirt). Length 30 to 40 cm (typical). The inner short has leg openings finished with elastic at 0.90 to 0.95. Sew rule: skirt top -> waistband with inner short.
- **Drafting**: A-line block (technique sheet A2) shortened, or a pleat intake as A6; inner short from the leggings draft.
- **Sewing order**: pleats; side seams; inner short; waistband; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic, coverstitch hem, optional side slits.
- **Fabrics**: polyester or nylon with elastane; pleated polyester with heat-set pleats.
- **Fit faults**: pleats gape -> intake too wide for the hip; skort rides up -> inner short too short.
- **Variants**: #tennis-dress, #skater-skirt, #a-line-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: skirt layer above the inner short layer; pleats via fold arrangement or a sewing ratio.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
