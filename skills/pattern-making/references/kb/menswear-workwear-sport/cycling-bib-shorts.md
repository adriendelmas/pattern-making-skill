# Cycling Bib Shorts (With Chamois) {#cycling-bib-shorts}

- **Aliases**: bib shorts, bibs, bib tights (fr: cuissard à bretelles, cuissard cycliste); (de: Trägerhose, Radhose)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit trousers
- **Recognize**: close-fitting shorts with shoulder straps in a stretch mesh or knit that meet the waistband at the front and back, a padded insert (chamois) in the crotch, leg grippers at the hem, flat seams.
- **Pieces**: leg panels (typically 6 to 8 panels for a high-quality short; a seamless design uses fewer), front panels (2), back panels (2), side panels (2), crotch or gusset panel (1), chamois pad (1, pre-made), straps (2, often one piece each), bib front (1), bib back (1), leg gripper bands (2), gripper elastic (silicone).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Retail and pattern listings found online say high-quality shorts use roughly 6 to 8 panels, the chamois is a shaped multi-density foam insert sewn in with its own gusset, the straps pass through the midsection to hold the pad in place and the legs end in silicone gripper elastic; a hobby pattern describes a 6-panel short with an optional padded crotch, in 4-way stretch fabric with at least 40 percent stretch (see Sources). The panel boundaries and dimensions were not available. General shape (trade practice, unverified): each leg is made of a front panel, a back panel and an inside or outside panel cut to follow the thigh, with seams placed away from the inner thigh; the crotch panel is a diamond or a long oval; the pad is a separate laminate whose edges are sewn flat to the crotch panel, running from the front crotch up to the back, with the widest part under the sit bones (about 15 to 17 cm, typical, unverified). The front bib is a V- or U-shaped piece with mesh and straps leading from the top corners over the shoulders to the back, where they meet the waist panel or a mesh back panel. Sew rule: pad -> crotch panel before the legs; leg panels -> side seams; straps -> waist at front and back.
- **Drafting**: 1. Leg block from leggings (technique sheet 6.4, reduction per fabric stretch; ankle replaced by the thigh gripper). 2. Panel lines placed from the crotch to the hem, avoiding the inner thigh. 3. Crotch gusset diamond 8 to 10 cm wide x 12 to 15 cm long (technique sheet 6.4) or matched to the pad outline. 4. Strap length: measured from the front waist over the shoulder to the back waist while the rider bends forward, then reduced by 8 to 12 percent. 5. Leg gripper band 5 to 8 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pad to the crotch panel; panels to legs; crotch seam; waist and straps; leg grippers; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock seams; silicone gripper elastic; chamois pad (cut-to-shape moulded or laminated foam); reflective trim.
- **Fabrics**: nylon-elastane or polyester-elastane Lycra with 30 to 40 percent minimum stretch; mesh straps; compression grades for the thigh.
- **Fit faults**: chamois bunches -> pad too wide or fabric tension uneven; leg grippers cut into the thigh -> band too tight; straps pull down at the front when riding -> straps too short.
- **Variants**: #sport-leggings, #cycling-jersey, #running-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: stretch fabric with tight fit; pad as a separate thick fabric layer; straps with a fixed length; start from the avatar in a seated pose.
