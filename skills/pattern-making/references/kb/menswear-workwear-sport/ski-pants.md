# Ski Pants {#ski-pants}

- **Aliases**: snow pants, shell trousers, insulated ski trousers (fr: pantalon de ski); (de: Skihose)
- **Family**: sportswear, outerwear trousers
- **Recognize**: waterproof, insulated or shell trousers with a waist closure and adjusters, side zips for ventilation, a reinforced inner hem, snow gaiters, articulated knees, belt loops and often removable suspenders.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), knee panels (2, articulated), waistband (1), thigh vent zip (2), boot gaiter (2), hem reinforcement (2), pocket bags (2 pairs), lining (front 2, back 2), insulation layers, suspender loops.
- **Shape**: Unverified. Based on a trouser block with extra ease 12 to 20 cm at the hip to allow layering (typical) and a pre-bent knee: a knee dart or a curved seam above and below the knee so the leg is cut with a slight forward bend; the back crotch is deeper; the hem flat width 24 to 30 cm to fit over a boot. Retail listings describe zipped hem gussets with an internal boot gaiter, Cordura hem reinforcement and articulated knees (see Sources). Sew rule: seams taped for waterproofing; zip to the thigh vent; reinforcement panel sewn to the hem and inner leg.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Add layering ease. 3. Knee seam or dart. 4. Hem cut with a longer back (2 to 3 cm). 5. Gaiter: a tube 18 to 22 cm tall with elastic at the bottom (typical). 6. Insulation lining 1 to 2 cm smaller.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; knee panels; seams; tape; lining; zips; waistband; gaiter; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: seam tape, waterproof zips, YKK-type zippers, elastic, Cordura patches, snaps.
- **Fabrics**: 2- or 3-layer waterproof breathable shells, softshell, ripstop nylon; insulation.
- **Fit faults**: knees pull when squatting -> knee articulation insufficient; hem drags -> gaiter or hem too long.
- **Variants**: #salopettes-ski, #sweatpants, #coverall-boiler-suit.
- **CLO3D**: stiff shell fabric, an inner insulation layer with a thickness; zips as trims.
- **Confidence**: features: retail listings (Sources). Drafting: trade practice; typical, unverified.
