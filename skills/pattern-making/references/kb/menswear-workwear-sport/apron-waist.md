# Waist Apron {#apron-waist}

- **Aliases**: half apron, bistro apron, server apron, bartender apron (fr: tablier taille, tablier de serveur); (de: Vorbinder)
- **Family**: workwear, aprons
- **Recognize**: a rectangle that hangs from a waist tie, usually with one to three pockets, no bib.
- **Pieces**: apron body (1), waistband with ties (1 long strip, or 3 pieces: band 1 and ties 2), pocket (1 to 3).
- **Shape**: Unverified. A rectangle 45 to 70 cm wide x 35 to 80 cm long (typical), the waist edge gathered or pleated into a waistband 2.5 to 4 cm finished whose ends continue into the ties (total strip length 150 to 220 cm). The waist edge is gathered at about 1.3 to 1.5:1 or box-pleated, or flat with the tie sewn along the top. Server aprons often have a split pocket (a vertical seam dividing it into two sections) 15 to 20 cm wide. Sew rule: side hems 0.6 to 1 cm; waistband centre aligned with the apron CF.
- **Drafting**: 1. Cut width = finished width x gather ratio (1 for flat). 2. Pocket 15 to 20 cm square, 5 to 8 cm below the waist. 3. Tie length per side = about 40 to 60 cm plus half the waist.
- **Sewing order**: hem the sides and bottom; pocket; gather; waistband; bar-tack the pocket.
- **Finishes and notions**: bar tacks; rivets at the pocket corners (optional).
- **Fabrics**: canvas, drill, linen, poly-cotton; denim for bar work.
- **Fit faults**: apron drifts up -> band too narrow; band rolls -> interface the band.
- **Variants**: #apron-bib, #apron-cross-back.
- **CLO3D**: rectangle with a sewing ratio on the waist edge, tie ends left free.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
