# Henley Shirt {#henley-placket-shirt}

- **Aliases**: grandad shirt, collarless placket shirt, baseball henley (fr: tee-shirt tunisien, col tunisien)
- **Family**: knit tops
- **Recognize**: a collarless round neckline with a short placket (3 to 5 buttons), often long sleeves with rib cuffs, a waffle or jersey knit.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2), neckband (1), placket (1 strip or a facing 2), cuffs (2, optional), interfacing for placket.
- **Shape**: Unverified. T-shirt block with a CF slit of 10 to 14 cm (typical) and a placket applied as a strip folded in two, or a facing; the neckband is a ring, ratio 0.85 to 0.90 of the neckline (technique sheet 3.1 for self-jersey bands); the front neckline depth 8 to 10 cm. Sew rule: the placket wraps the CF slit; the neckband joins the neckline to the top of the placket and ends at the CF edges; shoulders are taped.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-tshirt-block with the slit; placket strip finished width 2.5 to 3 cm (cut 2x + 2 SA).
- **Sewing order**: shoulder tapes; placket; shoulder seams; neckband; sleeves flat; side and underarm; cuffs; hem; buttonholes.
- **Finishes and notions**: 4 to 5 buttons 10 to 12 mm; interfacing on the placket; coverstitch hem and neck.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey, waffle, slub; avoid very heavy knits for the placket.
- **Fit faults**: placket rippling -> placket stretched; neckband stands away -> too short a ratio or too wide a neckline.
- **Variants**: polo (#polo-shirt-knit), grandad shirt woven with a band collar.
- **CLO3D**: same as the T-shirt with a placket piece layered above the front.
- **Confidence**: derived from technique sheet 4.1, 4.2: typical values.
