# Men's Underwear (link) {#mens-underwear}

- **Aliases**: boxer briefs, briefs, boxers, trunks (fr: sous-vêtements homme, caleçon, slip)
- **Family**: lingerie / underwear
- **Recognize**: boxer briefs: knit short with a wide waistband; briefs (Y-front): a front pouch with a Y overlap; boxers: woven loose shorts with a button fly.
- **Pieces**: boxer brief: front (1 or 2 with a CF seam), back (1 or 2), gusset (1), waistband elastic (1), leg band (optional); briefs: front (1), pouch (1), back (1), leg elastics (2), waistband; boxers: front (2), back (2), waistband (1), fly facing (1), elastic.
- **Shape**: Unverified; see the lingerie file (`techniques/knits-lingerie-swimwear.md` section 5.7 for briefs). Boxer brief: a leg that continues to mid-thigh with a curved crotch, a pouch seam at the front and a center seam at the back; sew rules: front -> back at the side seam, gusset -> crotch. Elastic ratio: waist 0.85 to 0.90, leg 0.90 to 0.95 (technique sheet).
- **Drafting**: see 5.7 for briefs; for boxer briefs, the leg length 8 to 15 cm below the crotch (typical).
- **Sewing order**: gusset; side seams; leg hems; waistband.
- **Finishes and notions**: flat elastic 25 to 40 mm for the waistband; 3-step zigzag or coverstitch.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey with elastane, modal; woven cotton for boxers.
- **Fit faults**: rides up -> leg too tight; gaps at the leg -> elastic too long.
- **Variants**: trunks, jockstrap (not detailed).
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; elastic via sewing ratio.
- **Confidence**: link entry; typical.
- **Status**: unverified
