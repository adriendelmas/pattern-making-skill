# Basketball Shorts {#basketball-shorts}

- **Aliases**: hoop shorts, loose athletic shorts, mesh shorts (fr: short de basket); (de: Basketballshorts)
- **Family**: sportswear, shorts
- **Recognize**: long loose shorts reaching the knee or just above, an elastic waistband with drawstring, an open hem, side vents, a built-in brief or none, often side stripes.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1), cord (1), side stripe (2, optional), pockets (0 to 2), inner brief (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. A trouser block shortened to above the knee (inseam 20 to 30 cm, typical) with a wide leg (hem flat 30 to 38 cm, typical); a straight or slightly curved hem; a side vent 6 to 10 cm; the waistband a casing for 38 to 50 mm elastic. Sew rule: as #mens-shorts with a casing waist.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-shorts with extra width and elastic waist.
- **Sewing order**: side seams; inseam; crotch; waistband; hem; vents.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic, cord, coverstitch, bar tacks.
- **Fabrics**: polyester mesh, dazzle fabric, double knit.
- **Fit faults**: hem swings -> too wide; waistband rolls -> narrower elastic.
- **Variants**: #running-shorts, #mens-shorts, #boxing-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: lightweight preset; elastic waist as a sewn ratio.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
