# Martial Arts Gi (Judo, Karate, Jiu-Jitsu) {#martial-arts-gi}

- **Aliases**: kimono, keikogi, dogi, uniform (fr: kimono de judo, kimono de karaté, judogi, karategi); (de: Kampfsportanzug)
- **Family**: sportswear, uniforms
- **Recognize**: a wide, cross-fronted jacket (uwagi) with a thick collar and a belt, wide straight sleeves, plus loose trousers with a drawstring and a reinforced knee.
- **Pieces**: jacket body (front left and right 2, back 1, usually with a CB seam), sleeve (2), collar (lapel strip, 2 or 1 long strip), skirt reinforcement (2), shoulder reinforcement (2), sleeve cuff reinforcement (2), trousers front and back (2 pairs), knee reinforcement (2), crotch gusset (1, often), drawstring (1), belt (1).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. IBJJF rules (BJJ) give measured limits (see Sources): lapel thickness 1.3 cm; collar width 5 cm; sleeve opening 7 cm at full extension; the jacket reaches the thigh; sleeves end at most 2 cm from the wrist with the arm extended (some sources say 5 cm); trousers end at most 2 cm above the ankle bone (other sources say 5 cm); fabric must be woven cotton or cotton-like. General shape (trade practice): a kimono-type jacket whose fronts cross left over right as worn, with a wide collar strip running along the front edges from the hem across the neckline to the opposite hem (a continuous lapel strip stitched on each front), reinforced with quilting; sleeves are wide and straight with a short cap and sewn into a deep armhole (kimono-style gusset, see `kimono-from-block-gusset` in methods-cutting); trousers are straight with a drawstring waist and a gusset at the crotch. Sew rule: lapel strips -> front edges; sleeve -> armhole; skirt reinforcement along the side slits.
- **Drafting**: 1. Wide jacket block, chest ease 25 to 40 cm (typical), length to the mid-thigh. 2. Add the crossing: each front extends past CF to the opposite side by 25 to 30 cm (typical). 3. Collar strip 5 cm finished width, thick (quilted) at 1.3 cm. 4. Sleeve length per the rule above, width at the opening at least 7 cm of free air around the forearm. 5. Trouser: wide straight leg with inseam ending near the ankle bone, crotch gusset.
- **Sewing order**: collar and lapel assembly; reinforcements; shoulder seams; sleeves; side seams and skirt; trousers; drawstring.
- **Finishes and notions**: heavy rows of stitching (3 to 5 cm apart) for reinforcement, cotton drawstring.
- **Fabrics**: heavy cotton weave (pearl weave, ripstop, canvas), pre-shrunk; sizes allow for shrinkage.
- **Fit faults**: sleeves too long for the competition rule -> shorten to the limits above; lapel flips -> thickness inadequate; trouser gusset binds -> reduce rise.
- **Variants**: #mens-trousers, #chef-jacket, #boxing-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: heavy cotton; collar as a thick layer; fronts overlap with proper layering.
- **Confidence**: IBJJF measurements: web sources (Sources; sleeve and trouser tolerances differ between sources). Layout: trade practice, unverified.
