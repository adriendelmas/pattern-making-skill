# Chore Coat {#chore-coat}

- **Aliases**: French chore jacket, bleu de travail jacket, utility jacket, shop coat (fr: veste de travail, veste bleu de travail)
- **Family**: workwear jackets, men's
- **Recognize**: a boxy unlined jacket with a button front (3 to 5 buttons), a small collar (flat fall or stand-fall) or none, large patch pockets at the hip and often one chest patch pocket, straight sleeves with button cuffs or a plain hem, a back that may carry a yoke or a box pleat, slightly longer than a shirt.
- **Pieces**: front right and left (2), back (1 on fold or 2 with CB), back yoke (1 or 2, optional), sleeve (2), collar upper and under (2) with stand (optional, 2), grown-on front facing (2) or folded placket, hip pocket (2), chest pocket (1), cuff (2, optional), interfacing.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Retail chore-coat patterns found online describe the three patch pockets and boxy fit but gave no piece drawing; the piece list below is trade practice. Body: a straight boxy front with a grown-on CF facing (finished width 2.5 to 3 cm, folded twice), a shirt-like neckline, shoulder dropped 2 to 5 cm and a flat sleeve cap of 6 to 10 cm; the back may carry a yoke (as #mens-shirt-yoke) or a CB box pleat released at mid-back. Hip patch pockets 17 to 22 cm wide x 18 to 24 cm tall (typical), square or slightly rounded at the lower corners, top edge 8 to 12 cm below the waist line; sew rule: pocket hem folded and stitched, side and bottom edges turned and edge-stitched to the front, bar tack at the top corners. Collar: a flat fall 6 to 8 cm at CB, or a shirt collar with a short stand (#mens-shirt-collar-stand).
- **Drafting**: 1. Men's shirt block or jacket block with chest ease 24 to 36 cm (typical), dropped shoulder. 2. Length at the hip or just below (typical 70 to 78 cm from the shoulder for size M). 3. Straight sleeve with cuff or hem at the wrist plus 2 to 3 cm. 4. Pockets and yoke placed as above. 5. Hem allowance 2.5 to 3 cm.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pockets. 2. Yoke and back pleat. 3. Shoulders. 4. Collar. 5. Front facings. 6. Sleeves flat. 7. Side and underarm seams. 8. Hem. 9. Buttonholes and buttons.
- **Finishes and notions**: flat-felled or bound seams, 15 to 20 mm buttons, bar tacks on pocket corners, tape over the neck seam.
- **Fabrics**: cotton drill, moleskin, herringbone twill, denim, linen, wool serge; heavy fabrics need a thinner stand interfacing.
- **Fit faults**: pockets pull open -> pocket cut too narrow; shoulder slope drag -> adjust the drop; collar rolls off -> stand too short.
- **Variants**: #work-shirt (shirt-weight), #work-jacket, #denim-trucker-jacket, #field-jacket-m65.
- **CLO3D**: patch pockets layered on the front; stiff woven; collar sewn by segment; hem fold shown as topstitch.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical, unverified.
