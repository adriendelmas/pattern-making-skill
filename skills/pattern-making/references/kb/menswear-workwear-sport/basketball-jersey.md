# Basketball Jersey {#basketball-jersey}

- **Aliases**: sleeveless jersey, tank jersey, game jersey, vest jersey (fr: maillot de basket, maillot sans manches); (de: Basketballtrikot)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit tops
- **Recognize**: a loose sleeveless top with deep U- or V-shaped armholes, a wide neckline (round or V), a straight or slightly curved hem, contrast binding around the armholes and neck, a number on the front and back.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), neck binding (1), armhole bindings (2), hem band (optional), side mesh (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. Armhole cut-away: the underarm point dropped 2 to 4 cm below the usual armhole depth and the shoulder strap reduced to 6 to 10 cm width (typical); neckline round, drop 8 to 12 cm; the back neck drop 3 to 5 cm; the side seams straight with a loose fit of 10 to 18 cm ease (typical). Sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder; binding stretched slightly at the armhole and neck (ratio 0.92 to 0.95, typical).
- **Drafting**: 1. T-shirt block without sleeves. 2. Cut the armhole deeper and move the shoulder end toward the neck. 3. Bindings from the ratios in technique sheet 3.1.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders; neck binding; armhole bindings; side seams; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch, binding, heat-transfer numbers.
- **Fabrics**: polyester mesh, double-knit mesh, dimpled mesh.
- **Fit faults**: armhole gapes -> bindings too long; shoulder slips -> strap too wide near the neck.
- **Variants**: #football-jersey, #basketball-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; bindings as separate stretch pieces.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
