# Rash Guard {#rash-guard}

- **Aliases**: rashie, rash vest, swim shirt, UV shirt, lycra top (fr: top anti-UV, lycra de surf, maillot anti-UV); (de: Lycra-Shirt)
- **Family**: swimwear and water sports
- **Recognize**: a close-fitting long- or short-sleeve top in a thin 4-way stretch polyester or nylon with flatlock seams, a crew or a mock neck, a longer back hem, UPF label.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2, set-in or raglan), neck binding or collar (1), hem band (optional), logo panels.
- **Shape**: Unverified. Close-fit knit block: negative ease 8 to 12 percent on the body for strong stretch fabrics (technique sheet 6.1); hem longer at the back by 3 to 6 cm so it does not ride up; sleeve with an elbow shape; neck depth 5 to 8 cm; raglan seams common (seam from neck to underarm). Sew rule: flatlock or overlock seams, shoulders taped; neck binding stretched to 0.90 to 0.95 of the neckline.
- **Drafting**: knit block (technique sheet 2.2) with the reduction above; sleeve cap shortened; add 1 to 2 cm at the back hem.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders; neck binding; sleeves; side seams; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock seams (reduce rub), tag-free labels, UPF 50+ fabrics.
- **Fabrics**: 80/20 nylon-elastane or polyester-elastane, UPF fabrics.
- **Fit faults**: rides up in the water -> back too short; neck gapes -> binding too long.
- **Variants**: #wetsuit-neoprene, #cycling-jersey, #sport-leggings.
- **CLO3D**: stretch knit with measured stretch; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet 6.1 (typical); structure trade practice.
