# Boxing Shorts {#boxing-shorts}

- **Aliases**: boxing trunks, fight shorts, Muay Thai shorts (fr: short de boxe);
- **Family**: sportswear, shorts
- **Recognize**: satin or polyester shorts with a wide elastic waistband (visible, 5 to 8 cm), a loose leg above or at mid-thigh, side slits, contrast waistband and trim, sometimes a built-in brief.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1, elastic or fabric), inner brief (optional), side-slit facing (2), piping (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. A trouser block cut to mid-thigh, hem flat width 28 to 34 cm (typical) with side slits 6 to 10 cm; a high-rise waistband wide enough to cover the waist (5 to 8 cm); the crotch seat cut generously for kicking. The waistband is a casing for the elastic, an elastic waistband with fabric outer layer. Sew rule: as #mens-shorts.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-shorts with extra width at the thigh (hem flat 28 to 34 cm), a high waist, and a side slit.
- **Sewing order**: side seams; inseam; crotch; waistband; hem; slits.
- **Finishes and notions**: wide elastic 50 to 75 mm, piping, bartacks at the slit tops.
- **Fabrics**: satin, polyester, ripstop; lightweight.
- **Fit faults**: waist rolls -> elastic too narrow; shorts ride up -> hem too tight.
- **Variants**: #mens-shorts, #basketball-shorts, #running-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: satin preset; elastic waistband via ratio.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
