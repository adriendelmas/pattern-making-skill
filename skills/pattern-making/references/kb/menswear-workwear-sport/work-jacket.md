# Work Jacket (Blouson and Mechanic Jacket) {#work-jacket}

- **Aliases**: blouson, Eisenhower-style work jacket, mechanic's jacket, canvas work jacket, barn jacket (fr: blouson de travail, veste de chantier)
- **Family**: workwear jackets
- **Recognize**: a short jacket ending at the waist or hip, a front zip or snap front, a collar (flat, stand or corduroy-faced), set-in or raglan sleeves with strap, rib or button cuffs, hand-warmer or flap pockets, a waistband or knit rib at the hem, often with a quilted lining.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), back yoke (1, optional action back), sleeve (2, one- or two-piece), collar (2), pocket bags (2 pairs), pocket flaps (2), waistband or hem rib (1), cuff rib or strap (2), lining (front 2, back 1, sleeve 2), facing (2), zip.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Body: a jacket block shortened to the waist plus 8 to 14 cm (typical), chest ease 20 to 32 cm; back yoke with an action pleat of 2 to 3 cm; sleeve with a lower cap and elbow ease (see `techniques/tailoring-outerwear.md` for jackets). Pockets: slant hand-warmer pockets with the opening 15 to 20 degrees off the vertical, 16 to 20 cm long (typical), or patch pockets with a flap 14 to 16 cm wide. Hem: a waistband or knit rib 6 to 8 cm wide at 80 to 90 percent of the body hem width (technique sheet 4.3 for rib ratios). Sew rule: front shoulder -> yoke/back shoulder; rib -> hem edge, stretched to fit the body hem.
- **Drafting**: 1. Jacket block, armhole depth +2 to 4 cm, shoulder extended 1 to 2 cm. 2. Shorten to the chosen length. 3. Add yoke and pleat. 4. Draft the pocket opening and bag on the front. 5. Lining from the shell pieces, 1 cm shorter at the hem plus a back pleat of 1.5 cm at CB.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Pockets. 2. Yoke, back pleat. 3. Shoulders. 4. Zip and facing. 5. Collar. 6. Sleeves, side and underarm seams. 7. Lining and hem rib or waistband. 8. Cuffs.
- **Finishes and notions**: heavy zip or snaps, bar tacks, flat-felled seams; a quilted lining needs a shell 1 cm larger at the hem to avoid pulling.
- **Fabrics**: canvas duck, drill, denim, flannel, quilted nylon; waxed cotton needs longer stitches and no needle holes in pressed folds.
- **Fit faults**: back pulls when reaching -> add an action pleat; hem rides up -> rib too tight; sleeves twist -> check the sleeve pitch.
- **Variants**: #chore-coat, #denim-trucker-jacket, #field-jacket-m65, #tracksuit-jacket.
- **CLO3D**: shell and lining layers, rib as a stretch knit, zip as a trim.
- **Confidence**: trade practice, numbers typical, unverified.
