# Men's Shorts {#mens-shorts}

- **Aliases**: chino shorts, Bermudas, cargo shorts (fr: bermuda, short homme)
- **Family**: trousers (shortened)
- **Recognize**: trouser construction with a short leg: fly, waistband, belt loops, side pockets.
- **Pieces**: as #mens-trousers (front 2, back 2, waistband, fly parts, pockets), plus cargo pockets optional.
- **Shape**: Unverified. The trouser block cut at the chosen inseam (technique sheet B4: Bermuda 25 to 35 cm, mid-thigh 10 to 18 cm, knee-length 40 to 45 cm). The leg opening is wider than at the same height on a trouser: hem flat width at least (thigh circumference at the hem level + 4) / 2, per the sheet. Sew rules identical to the trousers.
- **Drafting**: cut the block at the length; widen the hem 2 to 4 cm for a loose look; hem allowance 2 to 4 cm; add 1 cm of crotch depth if seated comfort is wanted.
- **Sewing order**: as #mens-trousers.
- **Finishes and notions**: as trousers; hem 3 to 4 cm; cuffs optional.
- **Fabrics**: cotton twill, linen, seersucker.
- **Fit faults**: rides up the crotch -> hem too tight at the thigh; widen 1 to 3 cm.
- **Variants**: running shorts #running-shorts, basketball shorts #basketball-shorts, boxing shorts #boxing-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: as trousers, with a short leg.
- **Confidence**: technique sheet B4 (typical).
