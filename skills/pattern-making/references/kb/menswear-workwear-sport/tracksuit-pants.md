# Tracksuit Pants {#tracksuit-pants}

- **Aliases**: track pants, training pants, warm-up pants, zip-ankle pants (fr: pantalon de survêtement, bas de jogging); (de: Trainingshose)
- **Family**: sportswear, trousers
- **Recognize**: elastic waist with drawstring, side seam stripes, a straight or tapered leg, side pockets, an open hem or ankle zips.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1 or 2, with an elastic channel), drawstring (1), pocket bags (2 pairs), side stripes (2, optional), ankle zip (2, optional), hem band (2, optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. A trouser block with ease 10 to 18 cm at the hip (typical), the leg slightly tapered; waistband cut as a tube of width = elastic + 2 cm with a CF buttonhole for the cord; the stripe is a strip along the side seam. For a zipped ankle, the hem has a slit of 12 to 18 cm in the side seam (typical). Sew rule: pocket bag -> front side seam; front crotch -> back crotch; waistband (elastic) -> waist edge.
- **Drafting**: as #sweatpants with the stripe; elastic 25 to 40 mm at 85 to 90 percent of the waist (technique sheet 6.7).
- **Sewing order**: pockets; stripes; side and inseams; crotch; waistband; elastic; hem or zips.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch, bar tacks, cord with stoppers.
- **Fabrics**: tricot, double-knit, woven polyester.
- **Fit faults**: waistband loose -> shorten elastic; knees bag -> narrow the leg.
- **Variants**: #sweatpants, #joggers, #scrubs-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: as the sweatpants.
- **Confidence**: trade practice, typical, unverified.
