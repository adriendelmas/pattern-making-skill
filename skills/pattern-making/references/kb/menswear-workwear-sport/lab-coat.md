# Lab Coat {#lab-coat}

- **Aliases**: laboratory coat, white coat, doctor's coat, dust coat (fr: blouse de laboratoire, blouse blanche); (de: Laborkittel, Arztkittel)
- **Family**: workwear, protective and medical coats
- **Recognize**: a knee-length or hip-length coat with a notch lapel or a stand collar, button or snap front, three pockets (one chest, two hip), set-in sleeves with cuffs that may be elasticated or knit, a vent at CB.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), collar upper and under (2), front facing (2, or fold), hip pocket (2), chest pocket (1), cuff (2, optional), back belt or half-belt (1, optional), interfacing.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. The structure is a shirt-jacket: a straight body with a CF facing, a notch collar and short lapel or a stand collar, hip patch pockets with a straight or slightly curved lower edge, a CB vent of 25 to 35 cm (typical), no waist shaping. Sew rule: as the jacket blocks (see `techniques/tailoring-outerwear.md`); front facing -> CF; collar -> neckline.
- **Drafting**: 1. Shirt or light jacket block with chest ease 20 to 30 cm (typical). 2. Length hip (80 to 90 cm) or knee (100 to 115 cm) (typical). 3. Shoulder extended 1 to 2 cm, sleeve cap 10 to 13 cm. 4. Hip pockets 17 to 20 cm wide x 18 to 22 cm tall, positioned 8 to 12 cm below the waist. 5. CB vent depth 25 to 35 cm, overlap 4 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; shoulders; collar; facing; sleeves; side seams; CB vent; hem; buttonholes.
- **Finishes and notions**: bar tacks at pocket corners, flat-felled seams, 15 to 20 mm buttons or snaps, side slit openings (optional).
- **Fabrics**: cotton twill, poly-cotton, disposable SMS non-woven for single use; wash-and-wear finishes.
- **Fit faults**: sleeves too short with raised arms -> add 2 to 3 cm; gapes at the front when seated -> vent or overlap too small; collar stands away -> shorten the collar neck edge.
- **Variants**: #scrubs-top, #chef-jacket, #work-shirt.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset; collar as separate layered pieces; vent as an overlap.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
