# Salopettes (Ski Bib Pants) {#salopettes-ski}

- **Aliases**: ski bib, bib pants, snow bibs, ski dungarees (fr: salopette de ski); (de: Skilatzhose)
- **Family**: sportswear, outerwear trousers
- **Recognize**: ski trousers with a high bib over the chest and adjustable suspenders, side zips for putting on, a zipped front or rear drop-seat, a snow guard at the waist, articulated knees and hem gaiters.
- **Pieces**: ski pants pieces as #ski-pants plus bib front (1), bib back (1), shoulder straps (2), strap adjusters (2), side opening zip (1 or 2), drop-seat panel (1, optional), snow guard (1), lining and insulation.
- **Shape**: Unverified. A ski-pants draft with a high bib: the bib is 25 to 35 cm tall with a chest panel and shoulder straps that cross at the back or attach with buckles; side zips of 25 to 40 cm run from the waist to the hip for entry; a rear drop-seat zip, where fitted, opens the rear panel for the toilet. Listings found online mention an enlarged side zip, a rear drop-seat zip, an internal snow guard and a non-slip snow skirt (see Sources). Sew rule: bib to waistband; straps to bib corners and back waist; side opening zip to the side seam.
- **Drafting**: as #ski-pants with a bib and strap draft per #bib-overalls; keep the waist ease at least 8 to 12 cm for layers.
- **Sewing order**: as ski pants, with the bib after the waist.
- **Finishes and notions**: waterproof zips, tape, buckles, adjusters.
- **Fabrics**: as ski pants.
- **Fit faults**: straps pull on the shoulders when sitting -> torso length short, as #coverall-boiler-suit.
- **Variants**: #ski-pants, #bib-overalls.
- **CLO3D**: shell and insulation layers; straps as fixed-length pieces.
- **Confidence**: features: retail listings (Sources). Drafting: typical, unverified.
