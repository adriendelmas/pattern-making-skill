# Tracksuit Jacket {#tracksuit-jacket}

- **Aliases**: track jacket, warm-up jacket, zip-up training jacket, sports jacket (fr: veste de survêtement, veste de jogging); (de: Trainingsjacke)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit and woven jackets
- **Recognize**: a full-length CF zip, a rib or fabric collar (stand), a rib or elastic hem and cuffs, set-in or raglan sleeves, side pockets (zipped or open), often contrast stripes down the sleeves and sides.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2, set-in or raglan with a shoulder seam), collar (rib 1, or stand 2), hem band (rib 1, or in 2 pieces), cuffs (2), zip facings (2), pocket bags (2 pairs), pocket welts (2), stripes (optional, 4).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Body: a loose jacket block with chest ease 12 to 20 cm (relaxed knit) (technique sheet 4.3 for sweatshirt ease), shoulder dropped 1 to 3 cm; for a raglan, the armhole is split into a diagonal seam from the neck to the underarm on both front and back and the sleeve carries a shoulder dart or seam; length to the hip, about 62 to 72 cm from the shoulder (typical). Rib hem band at 0.75 to 0.85 of the body hem width, cuffs 0.65 to 0.75 of the sleeve opening, collar rib ratio 0.70 to 0.80 (technique sheet 4.3). Zip opening runs from the neck edge to the hem band; zip tape sewn to a folded facing on each front. Side pocket: on-seam pocket opening 14 to 18 cm or a zipped welt pocket 14 to 16 cm. Sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder; sleeve -> armhole flat; rib hem -> body hem stretched evenly; zip tape -> CF edge.
- **Drafting**: 1. T-shirt/sweatshirt block with the dropped shoulder and chest ease above. 2. Add the CF facing (2 to 2.5 cm) for the zip. 3. Sleeve length to the wrist minus the cuff height, plus 1 to 2 cm blousing. 4. Rib bands from the ratios above. 5. Stripe pieces: draft as an inset cut from the sleeve and side panels (stripe 2 to 4 cm wide, typical).
- **Sewing order**: stabilize shoulders; pockets; shoulder seams; collar rib; sleeves flat; side seams; cuffs; hem rib; zip; topstitch.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch seams, zip with a locking slider, tape at the shoulders, bar tack zip bottom, zipper pull.
- **Fabrics**: tricot polyester, double-knit, fleece, French terry, cotton-poly; stretch drives ease; tricot with 20 to 40 percent stretch.
- **Fit faults**: hem band flares -> ratio too tight, lengthen 5 percent; collar stands away -> rib too long; shoulders drag with raglan -> sleeve pitch.
- **Variants**: #tracksuit-pants, #work-jacket, hoodie (technique sheet 4.3).
- **CLO3D**: knit preset with the measured stretch; rib bands via sewing ratio; zip as a trim; stripes as separate patterns or as a graphic.
- **Confidence**: ratios: technique sheet 4.3 (published practice). Other values typical, unverified as a drawn pattern.
