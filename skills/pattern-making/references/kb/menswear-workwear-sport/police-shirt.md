# Police Shirt (Uniform Shirt) {#police-shirt}

- **Aliases**: law-enforcement shirt, duty shirt, security shirt, uniform shirt with epaulettes (fr: chemise d'uniforme, chemise de police); (de: Diensthemd)
- **Family**: uniforms, men's shirts
- **Recognize**: a shirt with epaulettes (shoulder tabs), two chest pockets with flaps, a pen slot on the left sleeve, a tie or open collar, shoulder badges (patch locations), collar with a stand, optional mic loops, creased pleats or sewn-down chest pockets.
- **Pieces**: front right and left (2), back (1), yoke (2), sleeve (2), collar stand and fall (4), cuff (4), placket (2, or grown-on), chest pockets (2, with flaps 2 to 4), epaulettes (2), sleeve pocket (1), patch positions (marked), interfacing.
- **Shape**: Unverified. Layout as the dress shirt (#mens-dress-shirt) with: epaulettes 5 to 6 cm wide x 14 to 16 cm long sewn at the armhole end, tapered to a point, buttoned at the neck end; chest pockets 11 to 13 cm wide x 12 to 14 cm tall with a pointed flap 4.5 to 5.5 cm; a sleeve pocket 5 x 14 cm on the left upper sleeve; a straight or short curved hem. Sew rule: as the dress shirt; epaulettes caught in the sleeve cap seam.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-dress-shirt with chest ease 14 to 22 cm (regular) and a tail 4 to 6 cm longer for tucking in.
- **Sewing order**: as the dress shirt, with epaulettes and pockets applied before the yoke.
- **Finishes and notions**: bar tacks; buttons 10 to 12 mm; collar stays; reinforced shoulders.
- **Fabrics**: poly-cotton poplin, twill; permanent press; flame-resistant for some roles.
- **Fit faults**: epaulette lifts -> position at the shoulder end; chest pocket gapes -> flap too narrow.
- **Variants**: #mens-dress-shirt, #work-shirt, #military-tunic.
- **CLO3D**: as the dress shirt; epaulettes and pockets as layered pieces.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
