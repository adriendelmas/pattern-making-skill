# Tennis Dress {#tennis-dress}

- **Aliases**: sports dress, athletic dress, racerback tennis dress, padel dress (fr: robe de tennis); (de: Tenniskleid)
- **Family**: sportswear, dresses
- **Recognize**: a fitted stretch top (racerback, V-neck or round neck, sleeveless or short-sleeve) joined at or above the natural waist to a short skater, flared or pleated skirt, with built-in shorts or briefs underneath and sometimes a pocket for balls inside the shorts.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (1, or 2 with princess seams), bodice back (1, racerback), skirt (1 or 2 panels), inner shorts front and back (2), straps or sleeves, binding for armhole and neck (2), waist seam elastic (1), ball pocket (0 to 2).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Top: close-fit knit block with negative ease 3 to 8 percent (technique sheet 2.2, 6.1), racerback cut with the back armholes drawn toward the CB (strap 4 to 6 cm wide at the shoulder, typical); a shelf bra or a lining; waist seam with clear elastic. Skirt: a skater circle (see #skater-skirt) or a pleated skirt (technique sheet A6) with a hem 30 to 40 cm below the waist (typical), a slight curve at the hem. Inner shorts: a brief with a short leg (see #running-shorts), sewn into the waist seam. Sew rule: bodice lower edge -> skirt waist and inner short waist together; armhole and neck bindings stretched 0.90 to 0.95 (typical).
- **Drafting**: 1. Knit bodice block. 2. Waist seam position at the natural waist or 3 to 5 cm above. 3. Skirt width: circle skirt radius from the waist formula in the technique sheet A4 or pleat intake per A6. 4. Inner shorts from a leggings draft cut at the thigh.
- **Sewing order**: bodice darts or seams; bindings; side seams; inner shorts; skirt; waist seam with elastic; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch, clear elastic at the waist seam, optional pad pockets, bonded edges.
- **Fabrics**: nylon-elastane or polyester-elastane, moisture-wicking knits; skirt in lighter woven or knit.
- **Fit faults**: skirt rides up -> flare too wide for the movement; bodice gapes -> bindings too long.
- **Variants**: #tennis-skirt, #sport-leggings, #skater-skirt.
- **CLO3D**: stretch fabric for the top; skirt a lighter fabric with the pleat via a sewing ratio; inner shorts as a separate pattern.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
