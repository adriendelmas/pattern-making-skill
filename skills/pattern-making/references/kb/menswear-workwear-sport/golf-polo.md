# Golf Polo {#golf-polo}

- **Aliases**: performance polo, wicking polo, golf shirt (fr: polo de golf); (de: Golf-Poloshirt)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit tops
- **Recognize**: a performance polo with a wicking fabric, a flat-knit collar or fabric collar, a 2- or 3-button placket, sleeves cut for swing, often side vents and a longer back, raglan-style shoulder or gusset.
- **Pieces**: front (1, with placket slit), back (1), sleeve (2), collar (flat-knit ready-made 1 or 2 fabric pieces), placket (1 or 2 strips), sleeve hem (2), neck tape (1), side vent facings (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. As #polo-shirt-knit with a sleeve cap lowered to 8 to 11 cm (typical, to allow the swing) and the sleeve width increased 2 to 3 cm; the sleeve cap may have a gusset or a raglan seam; chest ease 8 to 14 cm (typical); hem back longer by 3 to 5 cm. The collar is cut to 0.97 to 1.0 of the neckline (technique sheet 4.2). Sew rule: as the polo.
- **Drafting**: polo block (technique sheet 4.2) with a shallow cap and wider sleeve; sleeve length measured to the elbow plus 2 to 4 cm if the short sleeve is wide.
- **Sewing order**: as #polo-shirt-knit.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch hem, bonded vents, 12 to 14 mm buttons.
- **Fabrics**: polyester piqué, mesh, wicking jersey, UV-protective fabrics; 2 to 5 percent elastane for mobility.
- **Fit faults**: sleeves pull in the swing -> deepen the armhole or widen the cap; collar curls -> add a collar stay or interface.
- **Variants**: #polo-shirt-knit, #rugby-shirt, #mens-tshirt-block.
- **CLO3D**: as the polo shirt.
- **Confidence**: from technique sheet 4.2 (typical); numbers typical, unverified.
