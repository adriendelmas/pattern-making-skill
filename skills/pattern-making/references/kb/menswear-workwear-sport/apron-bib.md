# Bib Apron {#apron-bib}

- **Aliases**: kitchen apron, butcher's apron, barista apron, pinafore apron (fr: tablier à bavette, tablier de cuisine); (de: Latzschürze)
- **Family**: workwear, aprons
- **Recognize**: a rectangle with a bib that covers the chest, a neck strap, waist ties, usually a front pocket, no sleeves.
- **Pieces**: apron body (1 piece: bib plus skirt, or 2 pieces joined at a waist seam), neck strap (1), waist ties (2), pocket (1 or 2), bib facing (optional), D-rings or a buckle (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Typical proportions: total length 75 to 95 cm from the top edge to the hem; skirt width 60 to 80 cm at the hem, straight or slightly gathered into the waist seam; bib width 30 to 40 cm at the waist and 20 to 30 cm at the top, the sides curving inward toward the top (concave arm curve); bib height 25 to 35 cm; the top edge straight or with a shallow curve. The neck strap is 55 to 70 cm cut length, 2.5 to 3.5 cm finished width, sewn to the two top corners of the bib; the waist ties are 60 to 90 cm each side, 2.5 to 3 cm finished width, set into the side edges at the waist line. Sew rule: bib and skirt side edges are hemmed (narrow 0.6 to 1 cm); neck strap -> bib top corners with a bar tack. Pocket: patch pocket 15 to 20 cm wide x 15 to 20 cm tall on the skirt, 8 to 12 cm below the waist.
- **Drafting**: 1. Draw a rectangle for the skirt (width x length). 2. Draw the bib above the waist with the side curves. 3. Position the pocket as above. 4. Strap length from the bib top to the nape, measured, plus 10 to 20 cm for adjustment. 5. Hem allowance 1 cm on the sides, 1.5 to 2 cm at the hem.
- **Sewing order**: 1. Hem the sides and top. 2. Pocket. 3. Waist ties. 4. Neck strap. 5. Bar tacks.
- **Finishes and notions**: bar tacks at strap and tie attachments, adjustable D-rings or slider buckle (optional), rivets at the pocket corners (heavy canvas or leather).
- **Fabrics**: cotton drill, canvas, denim, poly-cotton, coated fabrics for wet work; leather needs a heavy needle and no folded seams.
- **Fit faults**: strap digs into the neck -> use the cross-back version (#apron-cross-back); bib gapes at the sides -> bib wider than the chest.
- **Variants**: #apron-waist, #apron-cross-back.
- **CLO3D**: flat piece, strap sewn to the corners, placed on the avatar at neck and waist; mesh 10 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical, unverified.
