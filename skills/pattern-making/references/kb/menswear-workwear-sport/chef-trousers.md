# Chef Trousers {#chef-trousers}

- **Aliases**: chef pants, kitchen trousers, baggy pants, checked chef trousers (fr: pantalon de cuisine); (de: Kochhose)
- **Family**: workwear, kitchen uniforms
- **Recognize**: loose straight-leg trousers with an elastic or drawstring waist (sometimes elastic-and-drawstring), often a checked or striped print, side or back pockets, no fly on the elastic model.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband with elastic casing (1), drawstring (1), pocket bags (2 pairs), back pocket (0, 1 or 2), cargo pocket (optional), interfacing none.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Trouser block with a loose silhouette: hip ease 20 to 30 cm (typical), straight or slightly tapered leg, flat hem width 22 to 28 cm (typical), rise lowered 1 to 2 cm for comfort; the waist cut straight with an elastic casing (see `techniques/skirts-trousers.md` B7) of the elastic width + 1.5 to 2.5 cm, and a drawstring buttonhole at CF. Sew rule: front crotch -> back crotch as a continuous seam; inseams equal length; casing turned and stitched.
- **Drafting**: 1. Block with extra width on the side seam and inseam. 2. Raise the waist by the casing allowance (2 x elastic width + 1 cm). 3. Elastic 25 to 40 mm at 85 to 90 percent of the waist (technique sheet 6.7). 4. Hem allowance 3 to 4 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; inseams; crotch; side seams; casing; elastic; drawstring; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 25 to 40 mm; cord; double-needle hem; bar tacks at the pocket tops.
- **Fabrics**: cotton or poly-cotton in houndstooth or small checks (hides stains), lightweight to medium.
- **Fit faults**: waist too loose -> elastic too long; crotch drops -> rise too long; legs too tight to squat -> add thigh width.
- **Variants**: #scrubs-trousers, #mens-trousers, #sweatpants.
- **CLO3D**: elastic waist as a sewn ratio; loose fabric preset; mesh 10 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice, typical, unverified.
