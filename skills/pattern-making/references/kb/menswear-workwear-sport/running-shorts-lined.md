# Running Shorts, Lined and Split (Sport) {#running-shorts-lined}

- **Aliases**: split shorts, racing shorts, lined running shorts, track shorts (fr: short de running, short de course); (de: Laufshorts)
- **Family**: sportswear, shorts
- **Recognize**: very light shorts with a wide elastic waist, a built-in brief or a liner, side splits (a notch at the side seam), a small key pocket at the waistband, a short inseam (5 to 12 cm typical).
- **Pieces**: outer shell front (2) and back (2) or one piece each side, inner brief front (1) and back (1) with gusset (1), waistband (1), elastic (1), key pocket (1), leg trim (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Outer: a short, wide leg with a flared side seam and a curved hem (higher at the side, the split); inseam 5 to 12 cm (typical, technique sheet B4 mid-thigh range); side split 3 to 7 cm. Inner: a brief draft with a diamond gusset (technique sheet 6.4 and 5.7). Sew rule: outer and liner both stitched into the waistband; liner leg openings with elastic at 0.90 to 0.95.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block cut at the inseam. 2. Widen the leg 2 to 4 cm, curve the hem up at the side. 3. Brief liner with reduction per stretch category. 4. Waist elastic 25 to 50 mm at 85 to 90 percent (technique sheet 6.7).
- **Sewing order**: liner; outer seams; waistband; hem; key pocket.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock seams where chafing matters; reflective trim.
- **Fabrics**: lightweight polyester or nylon ripstop; polyester mesh liner; small amounts of elastane.
- **Fit faults**: chafing -> flatlock or smoother seams; liner rides up -> leg elastic too tight.
- **Variants**: #mens-shorts, general running shorts in the bottoms file #running-shorts, #basketball-shorts, #boxing-shorts, #sport-leggings.
- **CLO3D**: lightweight woven preset; liner as a layered stretch fabric.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
