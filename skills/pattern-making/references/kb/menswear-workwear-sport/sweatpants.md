# Sweatpants {#sweatpants}

- **Aliases**: sweats, jogging bottoms, trackie bottoms, lounge pants (fr: pantalon de jogging, jogging); (de: Jogginghose)
- **Family**: sportswear, trousers
- **Recognize**: elastic waist with a drawstring, a straight or slightly tapered leg with an open hem or rib cuff, side seam or slant pockets, a loose fit.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1), cord (1), pocket bags (2 pairs), cuffs (2, optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified. A trouser block with hip ease 12 to 20 cm and a straight leg 20 to 26 cm flat at the hem (typical), reduced crotch seat curve, the waistband cut as a flat band or a casing; the same elastic channel as in #tracksuit-pants. Sew rule: as #joggers without the strong taper.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with extra ease. 2. Waistband of the elastic width. 3. Hem allowance 3 cm (coverstitch).
- **Sewing order**: as #joggers.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch hem, elastic 25 to 40 mm, eyelets or buttonholes.
- **Fabrics**: terry, fleece, cotton jersey.
- **Fit faults**: sagging seat -> back rise too long; hem rides up -> pant too short.
- **Variants**: #joggers, #tracksuit-pants, #chef-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; elastic waist via a ratio.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
