# Sport Leggings {#sport-leggings}

- **Aliases**: gym leggings, running tights, compression tights, yoga pants (fr: legging de sport, collant de running); (de: Sportleggings)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit trousers
- **Recognize**: close-fitting 4-way stretch trousers with a wide waistband, flatlock seams, a gusset at the crotch, often side or waistband pockets, ankle zips or reflective details.
- **Pieces**: leg (2, or front and back panels), gusset (1), waistband (1, cut on fold), pocket (optional), panels (mesh, optional).
- **Shape**: drawn pattern: not available; the construction follows `techniques/knits-lingerie-swimwear.md` 6.4 and 6.7 (leggings with a diamond gusset, waistband, flatlock). Reduction 10 to 15 percent horizontal on 50 to 75 percent stretch fabric, 15 to 20 percent for 76 to 100 percent stretch; inseam and rise reduced by 3 to 5 percent; gusset diamond 8 to 10 cm wide x 12 to 15 cm long; waistband x 0.80 to 0.90 of the waist, 4 to 6 cm folded height (technique sheet). Sew rule: crotch seam, inseam, gusset into the diamond, side seams, waistband.
- **Drafting**: see the technique sheet 6.4; add pocket stays; for compression use the 6.6 reduction (15 to 25 percent on 100 to 150 percent stretch) and verify with the pressure model.
- **Sewing order**: see technique sheet 6.4.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock or bonded seams; waistband elastic; reflective details.
- **Fabrics**: nylon-elastane, polyester-elastane, power mesh; check the stretch with the 1.1 test.
- **Fit faults**: see technique sheet 6.4 (camel toe, knee bags, back-knee wrinkles, waistband rolls).
- **Variants**: #cycling-bib-shorts, #running-shorts, #tennis-dress, #rash-guard.
- **CLO3D**: stretch fabric with measured stretch; gusset in the mesh; waistband via ratio.
- **Confidence**: verified (technique sheet 6.4, 6.6, 6.7, published practice).
