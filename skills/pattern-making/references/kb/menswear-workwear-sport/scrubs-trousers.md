# Scrub Trousers {#scrubs-trousers}

- **Aliases**: scrub pants, medical trousers, hospital uniform pants (fr: pantalon médical, pantalon de bloc); (de: OP-Hose)
- **Family**: workwear, medical uniforms
- **Recognize**: straight or tapered pants with a drawstring and/or elastic waist, side pockets (angled, on-seam), back patch pockets, sometimes leg cargo pockets, a plain hem or a cuff.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1, casing), drawstring (1), pocket bags (2 pairs), back pocket (0 to 2), leg cargo pocket (0 to 2).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Free patterns found online (see Sources) show front and back legs, side-seam pockets, back patch pockets and a drawstring waist; one uses 38 mm elastic and 6.5 cm bias strips for a drawstring of about 1.5 m. Typical geometry: trouser block with hip ease 12 to 20 cm, straight leg with hem flat 20 to 26 cm, a tapered or cuffed leg for jogger styles; the waist edge is straight with a casing (elastic width + 2 cm), a drawstring buttonhole 1.5 to 2 cm at CF; angled pocket opening 16 to 20 cm. Sew rule: front crotch -> back crotch as one curve; pocket bag -> front side seam; casing turned and stitched.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block with extra ease. 2. Casing allowance 3 to 5 cm above the waist. 3. Elastic 25 to 40 mm at 85 to 90 percent of the waist. 4. Drawstring 100 to 150 cm. 5. Hem 3 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; side and inseam; crotch; casing; elastic; drawstring; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic, cord, eyelets or buttonholes, bar tacks.
- **Fabrics**: poly-cotton, stretch twill, antimicrobial finish; stretch reduces ease by 2 to 4 cm.
- **Fit faults**: waist gapes at the back -> add elastic length or a shaped back; rise drags when squatting -> add 1 to 2 cm to the back rise.
- **Variants**: #chef-trousers, #joggers, #sweatpants.
- **CLO3D**: elastic waist as a sewn ratio; side pocket as layered pocket bags.
- **Confidence**: piece structure: cited free patterns. Numbers: typical, unverified.
