# High-Visibility Vest {#hi-vis-vest}

- **Aliases**: hi-vis vest, safety vest, reflective vest, warning vest, high-viz tabard (fr: gilet haute visibilité, gilet de signalisation); (de: Warnweste)
- **Family**: workwear, PPE
- **Recognize**: a sleeveless vest in fluorescent yellow, orange or red with retroreflective horizontal bands around the torso and vertical bands over the shoulders; a front closure by zip, hook-and-loop, or a snap; sometimes mesh sides, an ID pocket or radio loops.
- **Pieces**: vest front (2), vest back (1 or 2), side panels or mesh (2, optional), retroreflective tape (horizontal bands x 2 front/back as one length, vertical bands x 2), closure tape (hook-and-loop, 2), binding (armhole and neck, 1 to 2), pocket (0 to 2), label.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern, but the area requirements come from EN ISO 20471: minimum fluorescent background material 0.14 m2 (class 1), 0.50 m2 (class 2), 0.80 m2 (class 3); minimum retroreflective material 0.10 m2 (class 1), 0.13 m2 (class 2), 0.20 m2 (class 3) (values from supplier and testing-lab pages, see Sources). A class 3 garment must also have sleeves or full-length leg bands (a vest alone does not reach class 3 unless combined with trousers or sleeves). The bands must be distributed evenly between front and back (maximum discrepancy 10 percent in one source). Typical layout (trade practice): body width front 40 to 50 cm each half at the chest, length 60 to 75 cm; horizontal retroreflective bands 50 mm wide (the standard sets a minimum band width; the commonly used width is 50 mm, check the standard) placed at about 18 cm below the arm and at the hem; two vertical bands from the waist band over the shoulder to the back waist band, forming a V or an X at the back. Sew rule: bands sewn on both edges flush; armhole and neck bound; side seams open or closed by elastic or mesh.
- **Drafting**: 1. Vest block (waistcoat) with ease 18 to 28 cm at the chest, armhole depth +4 cm to allow layering. 2. Neck cut wide, front opening straight. 3. Compute the band lengths: horizontal bands = chest girth x 2 minus overlap; verify the area of the fluorescent material after the bands are applied (class area minus covered area). 4. Closure: hook-and-loop 50 mm wide, 12 to 15 cm long.
- **Sewing order**: bands applied to flat pieces; shoulder and side seams; bind the neck and armholes; closure; label.
- **Finishes and notions**: retroreflective tape (sew-on, 50 mm), fluorescent polyester or mesh fabric, hook-and-loop, label with the standard and class.
- **Fabrics**: fluorescent polyester woven or knit, mesh; must keep colorfastness in washing; certified bands cannot be hidden by logos or pockets.
- **Fit faults**: bands slide up the body -> vest too wide or too short; vest swings open -> closure too short; band area falls below the minimum -> recompute the areas.
- **Variants**: coverall with bands #coverall-boiler-suit, jacket versions #work-jacket.
- **CLO3D**: fluorescent fabric as a texture; bands as separate layered patterns; do not use for compliance verification.
- **Confidence**: areas: EN ISO 20471 per supplier and testing-lab pages (Sources). Layout and dimensions: trade practice, typical.
