# Joggers {#joggers}

- **Aliases**: jogger pants, tapered sweatpants, cuffed track pants, cargo joggers (fr: jogging fuselé, pantalon jogger)
- **Family**: sportswear, trousers
- **Recognize**: elastic waist, a tapered leg closing in a rib cuff at the ankle, side pockets, sometimes zip pockets and back pockets, a gusset or low-slung crotch.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1 or 2), cord (1), ankle cuffs (2, rib), pocket bags (2 pairs), back pocket (0 to 2).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Trouser block cut with hip ease 8 to 16 cm (typical) and a strongly tapered leg: knee width 20 to 24 cm flat, ankle opening 14 to 18 cm flat before the cuff (typical), cuffs 6 to 8 cm tall at 0.65 to 0.75 of the ankle opening (technique sheet 3.5 and 4.3). Crotch curve deeper than a classic trouser by 1 to 2 cm for comfort. Sew rule: cuffs sewn after the inseam; the cuff ring stretched to the leg opening.
- **Drafting**: 1. Trouser block. 2. Taper the leg from the knee to the ankle. 3. Extend the hem to allow blousing 3 to 5 cm over the cuff. 4. Add cuff pieces from the ratios.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; side seams; inseams; crotch; waistband; cuffs; cord.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch, elastic, cord, zip pockets.
- **Fabrics**: French terry, fleece, double knit; woven stretch for tech joggers.
- **Fit faults**: cuff pulls the leg -> too tight a cuff ratio; leg too tight over the calf -> add width.
- **Variants**: #sweatpants, #tracksuit-pants.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset; cuffs via sewing ratio; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: ratios: technique sheet 4.3; leg widths typical, unverified.
