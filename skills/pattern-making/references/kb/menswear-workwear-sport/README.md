# Menswear, workwear and sportswear

KB file. Format: see `FORMAT.md`. Method sheets used as the numeric base: `techniques/sleeves-collars.md` (collars 2.3, 2.4; cuffs 1.19; plackets 1.20), `techniques/skirts-trousers.md` (B1 trouser block, B3 jeans, B4 shorts, B5 leggings, B6 jumpsuits, B7 elastic waists), `techniques/knits-lingerie-swimwear.md` (stretch classes, reduction, band ratios, T-shirt 4.1, polo 4.2, hoodie 4.3, swim 6.x).

How to read the numbers in this file: "typical" = textbook or trade starting value, not a standard; confirm on a toile. "Source" in a Shape field says where the outline comes from. No drawn pattern from a published book was available online for any entry in this file, so most Shape fields say "unverified" for the proportions; the piece list, the seam join order and the numbers marked "verified" were checked against the cited sources (see `## Sources`). Convention: men's shirts lap right front over left as worn (buttons on the wearer's right); men's trouser flies conventionally lap left over right as worn (opposite to the shirt). Always check the reference garment.
