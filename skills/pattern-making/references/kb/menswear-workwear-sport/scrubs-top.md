# Scrub Top {#scrubs-top}

- **Aliases**: scrub shirt, medical top, hospital uniform top, V-neck scrub (fr: blouse médicale, tunique médicale, haut de bloc); (de: OP-Kasack, Kasack)
- **Family**: workwear, medical uniforms
- **Recognize**: a loose pullover with a V-neck (or round neck), short sleeves (set-in or kimono/grown-on), one to three patch pockets (chest and lower hip), side vents, a hem slightly curved or straight, trim or facing at the neck.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2, or grown-on), neckband or neck facing (front 1, back 1), chest pocket (1), lower pockets (2), side vent facings (optional), interfacing for the facing.
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Free patterns found online (see Sources) describe: six main pieces (front with V-neck, back with round neck, front and back of each sleeve) in a basic layout; a grown-on or set-in sleeve; front and back neck facings interfaced and joined at the shoulders; patch pockets attached late in construction; side slits. A zero-waste version uses a central body piece with neckbands cut from opened-out fabric. Typical geometry: front neck drop 12 to 18 cm for a V (typical), back neck drop 2 cm; shoulder dropped 1 to 3 cm; chest ease 14 to 24 cm (typical); the side seam splits into vents 8 to 12 cm. Sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder; neck facing outer edge -> neckline; sleeve -> armhole (set-in) or grown-on underarm seam; side seam stops at the vent.
- **Drafting**: 1. Men's or women's T-shirt/shirt block with a larger armhole and wider shoulder. 2. V-neck: front neck drop 12 to 18 cm, width 8 to 9 cm half. 3. Facing 4 to 5 cm wide from the neck edge. 4. Pockets: chest 11 to 13 cm wide x 12 to 14 cm tall; lower 15 to 17 cm x 16 to 18 cm (typical). 5. Hem allowance 1.5 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets; shoulders; neck facing or binding; sleeves; side seams to the vent; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: interfaced facing, bar tacks at the pocket corners, topstitching, overlocked seams.
- **Fabrics**: poly-cotton, cotton, stretch polyester-viscose; antimicrobial finishes; stretch woven reduces ease by 2 to 4 cm.
- **Fit faults**: V-neck gapes -> facing not interfaced or neck cut too deep; sleeves pull when lifting -> deepen the armhole or use a gusset.
- **Variants**: #scrubs-trousers, #chef-jacket, #lab-coat, #mens-tshirt-block.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset; neck facing as an internal layer; pockets layered on the front.
- **Confidence**: piece structure: cited free patterns. Numbers: typical, unverified.
