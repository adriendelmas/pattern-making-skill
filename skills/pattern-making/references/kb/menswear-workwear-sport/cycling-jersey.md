# Cycling Jersey {#cycling-jersey}

- **Aliases**: bike jersey, road jersey, cycling top (fr: maillot de cyclisme, maillot vélo); (de: Radtrikot)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit tops
- **Recognize**: a close-fitting short- or long-sleeve top with a half or full CF zip, a longer back than front (drop tail) with a gripper elastic at the hem, three open pockets across the low back, a low stand collar, set-in sleeves cut pre-curved for the riding position.
- **Pieces**: front (1 or 2 with a zip seam), back (1), sleeve (2), collar (1), zip placket (2 strips), back pocket panel (1 outer plus 1 hem band), hem gripper (1), sleeve hem bands (2), reflective trim (optional).
- **Shape**: Unverified as a drawn pattern. Body: close fit on 4-way stretch knit (negative ease 0 to 5 percent on the body, technique sheet 6.1 for reduction); front length shorter than back by 6 to 12 cm (typical) with the hem curved; the back is cut with a rise at the pocket area; the three pockets are formed by a pocket panel (a rectangular piece folded and sewn to the back with two vertical seams) of about 40 to 45 cm width x 15 to 18 cm height (typical); a stand collar 3 to 4 cm at the neck; the front opening a 15 to 30 cm zip (half) or a full-length CF zip. Sleeve: set-in with the sleeve cap cut with the elbow bend forward for a riding position (pre-bent sleeve). Sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder; collar -> neck with the zip; sleeve -> armhole flat; pocket panel -> back with the bottom seam closed; hem gripper elastic sewn inside the hem.
- **Drafting**: 1. Close-fit knit block with 3 to 8 percent reduction, hips extended by the gripper hem. 2. Back length + 8 to 12 cm over the front. 3. Pocket panel drafted on the back. 4. Sleeve length measured with the arms on the bars (bent elbow) and shortened in the armscye. 5. Zip length 20 to 30 cm or full.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders taped; pockets; zip; collar; sleeves flat; side seams; hem gripper; sleeve hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock or covered seams, silicone gripper tape on the hem, reflective piping, zip garage at the collar.
- **Fabrics**: polyester jersey with elastane, mesh panels under the arms or back, merino blends; sublimation prints are applied to the flat pieces before cutting.
- **Fit faults**: hem rides up -> gripper too narrow or too tight; zip rubs the neck -> missing zip garage; pockets sag -> panel too wide or no elastic.
- **Variants**: #cycling-bib-shorts, #rugby-shirt, #football-jersey.
- **CLO3D**: stretch knit preset with negative ease; pockets as layered patches; zip as a trim.
- **Confidence**: features: trade practice. Numbers: typical, unverified.
