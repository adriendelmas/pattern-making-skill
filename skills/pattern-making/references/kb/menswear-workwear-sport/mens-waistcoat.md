# Men's Waistcoat (link) {#mens-waistcoat}

- **Aliases**: vest (US), gilet de costume (fr: gilet de costume)
- **Family**: tailoring, men's
- **Recognize**: a sleeveless garment with a V-front, 5 to 6 buttons, welt pockets, a self-fabric front and a lining-fabric back with a back strap and buckle (adjuster).
- **Pieces**: front (2, self), back (2 lining, 2 self optional), front facing (self or lining), pocket welts (2 to 4), buckle strap (1 pair), interfacing.
- **Shape**: Unverified; see the tailoring file (`techniques/tailoring-outerwear.md`). The front is a short, fitted piece from the shoulder to the waist with a pointed or straight hem, a V neckline reaching the waist button, and a curved armhole; the back is lining fabric with a CB seam and straps; sew rule: front shoulder -> back shoulder; side seam; front facing sewn to the front edges; buckle strap at the CB hem.
- **Drafting**: the men's jacket block cut short; neck depth to the waist; button spacing 7 to 9 cm (typical).
- **Sewing order**: interfacing; welt pockets; shoulder seams; edge sewing with the lining; turning through the shoulder or the side seam; buttonholes.
- **Finishes and notions**: 5 to 6 buttons, lining.
- **Fabrics**: wool, wool-blend, silk lining at the back.
- **Fit faults**: gapes at the armhole -> take in the front; hem cups -> shorter.
- **Variants**: single or double breasted; with a lapel or collar.
- **CLO3D**: layered shell and lining.
- **Confidence**: link entry; trade practice.
