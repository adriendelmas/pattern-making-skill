# Football (Soccer) Jersey {#football-jersey}

- **Aliases**: soccer shirt, replica shirt, match jersey, kit shirt (fr: maillot de football); (de: Fußballtrikot)
- **Family**: sportswear, knit tops
- **Recognize**: a short-sleeved polyester top with a crew, V-neck or collared neck, set-in or raglan sleeves, side or underarm mesh panels, sublimated or woven patterns, sponsor logos, the shirt tails evenly across the hip.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2), collar or neck rib (1), side panels (2, mesh, optional), back yoke (1, mesh, optional), hem band (optional), logo tags and heat-transfers.
- **Shape**: Unverified. Body: a T-shirt block with moderate ease (4 to 12 cm, typical), armholes cut higher for mobility; a collared version adds a polo-like placket; the hem is straight or has a slight curve; side mesh inserts 4 to 8 cm wide run from the underarm to the hem. Sew rule: as a T-shirt: front shoulder -> back shoulder; sleeve -> armhole; neck rib -> neckline.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-tshirt-block with sleeve cap 10 to 13 cm; if raglan, the seam from neck to underarm replaces the armhole; for pre-printed sublimation the pattern pieces are laid out on the roll and the print aligned.
- **Sewing order**: print pieces; shoulders; neck; sleeves; side seams; hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock, coverstitched hem, heat-transfer badges.
- **Fabrics**: polyester jersey, piqué mesh, dimpled mesh; 100 percent polyester for sublimation.
- **Fit faults**: print mismatch at the seams -> cut allowance; armpit pulls -> deeper armhole.
- **Variants**: #basketball-jersey, #rugby-shirt, #cycling-jersey, #mens-tshirt-block.
- **CLO3D**: knit preset, print as a graphic aligned to the pattern pieces.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical, unverified.
