# Halter Bikini {#bikini-halter}

- **Aliases**: halter top, neck-tie bikini; fr: bikini dos nu, bikini à nouer au cou
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: bikini top with a strap that goes round the neck and a band that ties at the back; cups with a curved neckline.
- **Pieces**: two cups (2, each lined), halter strap (2 or 1 piece with a neck tie), back band (1, with ties), lining, foam (optional).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Cups: triangle or moulded half-cup shape with the top outer corner extended into the neck strap; the base edge sews to the back band; the band runs round the back and ties at CB; the neck straps may be elastic bound, tied at the nape. Straight strap length from the cup top corner to the nape knot: measured on the body (not a standard figure).
- **Drafting**: as #bikini-triangle with strap points lifted to the neck; strap length = measured value + 20 cm for tying.
- **Sewing order**: cups, band, elastic, straps, ties.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic, rings, sliders, ties.
- **Fabrics**: swim knit with lining.
- **Fit faults**: cups gape at the top: shorten the cup edges; pulls at the neck: lengthen straps.
- **Variants**: #bikini-triangle, #swimsuit-built-in-cups.
- **CLO3D**: straps tied by pins; mesh 4 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
