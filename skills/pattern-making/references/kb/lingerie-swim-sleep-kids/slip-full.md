# Full Slip {#slip-full}

- **Aliases**: dress slip, bias slip, camisole slip, petticoat (UK usage); fr: combinaison (sous-vêtement), fond de robe, robe de dessous
- **Family**: lingerie, slips
- **Recognize**: a slim underdress with thin straps, a bodice with a bust shaped by darts, seams or bias cut, falling to mid-thigh or knee; a lace hem or lace bust trim; often cut on the bias.
- **Pieces**: techniques 5.8: front (1), back (1), strap (2), facing (1) for the top edge, optional lace at the hem. A bias slip may have a CF seam, side seams and bust darts or a French dart.
- **Shape**: text source techniques 5.8 for pieces; shape from trade practice, unverified: front is a straight-sided column with a slight A-line flare, the top edge is a curved line (straight, V or scoop) between the two strap points; the bust is shaped by a French dart or a bias cut that stretches (ease 4 to 8 cm round at the bust and hip on the bias, techniques 5.8); side seams straight from underarm to hem; back is similar with a lower top edge. Facing: a curved band of the same shape as the top edge, 3 to 4 cm tall, sewn to it. Straps sewn at the shoulder points with a 6 to 10 mm width (techniques 5.8).
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft a close-fitting column from the sloper with 4 to 8 cm total ease at bust and hip on the bias (techniques 5.8). 2. Length: shoulder to mid-thigh or knee. 3. Mark the straight grain at CF; for the bias cut, rotate the pattern 45 degrees on the fabric. 4. Top edge: shape and depth to taste; facing 3 to 4 cm. 5. Hang the cut pieces 24 hours before hemming (bias drops up to 3 cm, techniques 5.8).
- **Sewing order**: darts, side seams (French seams), straps, facing, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: 6 to 10 mm spaghetti straps, narrow rolled hem or lace, French seams.
- **Fabrics**: silk charmeuse, satin, viscose; a bias cut drapes; use a firm fabric for a straight cut.
- **Fit faults**: bust gapes: pinch out of the top edge; clings at the hip: increase ease; hem uneven: hang before hemming.
- **Variants**: #slip-half, #chemise, #nightgown.
- **CLO3D**: bias fabric with grain line rotated 45 degrees; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: pieces from techniques 5.8 (trade practice); outline unverified.
