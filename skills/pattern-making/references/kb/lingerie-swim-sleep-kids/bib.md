# Baby Bib {#bib}

- **Aliases**: feeding bib, dribble bib, bandana bib, apron bib; fr: bavoir
- **Family**: babywear, accessories
- **Recognize**: a small shaped piece covering the chest with a neck opening and a closure (snap, hook-and-loop or ties at the nape); front often in absorbent fabric with a waterproof backing; a bandana bib is a triangle.
- **Pieces**: front (1), backing (1), neck binding (1) or closure tabs (2), snaps or hook-and-loop (1 set), optional crumb pocket (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Standard bib: a rounded keyhole form: neck opening a semicircle 6 to 8 cm radius (typical) at the top edge, bottom edge rounded, side edges curved; width at the chest 20 to 25 cm and length 20 to 25 cm (typical for a baby). Closure tabs extend from the back neck edge. Bandana bib: a triangle with the apex at the bottom, the long edge at the neck with ties.
- **Drafting**: measure the baby's neck circumference; make the neck opening about 1.5 to 2 cm larger than the neck; the closure overlap 2.5 cm; no cords longer than the safety limits (see #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers for cord safety); use snaps rather than ties for baby bibs.
- **Sewing order**: front to backing, turn, topstitch, binding, closure.
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps (2), hook-and-loop, bias binding, waterproof laminate backing.
- **Fabrics**: terry, cotton flannel front; PUL or silicone backing.
- **Fit faults**: bib folds at the neck: neck opening too small; gapes: shorten.
- **Variants**: #baby-bonnet.
- **CLO3D**: small flat pieces, mesh 5 mm; baby avatar.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
