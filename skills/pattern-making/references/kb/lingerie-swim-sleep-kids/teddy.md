# Teddy {#teddy}

- **Aliases**: camiknickers, camisole body, lace teddy; fr: body-teddy, combinaison-culotte
- **Family**: lingerie, one-piece
- **Recognize**: a camisole top joined to a loose brief or short in one piece, with a snap crotch closure; lace-trimmed; straps at the shoulders.
- **Pieces**: front body (1), back body (1), crotch gusset (1 outer, 1 lining), straps (2), lace trim, snap tape (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. The torso is a loose rectangular tube with a straight or curved top edge; below the waist it narrows into a brief shape, cut as a continuous panel so that the front and back are single pieces from top to crotch; the crotch fall forms a flap with snaps. As in #bodysuit, the torso length T (shoulder, front, crotch, back) governs the length. Looser than a bodysuit: ease 6 to 10 cm at bust and hip (typical).
- **Drafting**: torso loop T and the reduction as in #bodysuit (techniques 5.8); in a woven fabric add 2 to 4 cm to the torso length; crotch width 8 to 10 cm with snaps, overlap 2.5 to 3 cm (techniques 5.8).
- **Sewing order**: side seams, top edge facing or lace, straps, gusset, snap tape.
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps (3 or 4), lace, straps.
- **Fabrics**: satin, charmeuse, lace over net, soft jersey.
- **Fit faults**: crotch pulls: lengthen T; gapes at the bust: pinch out of the top edge.
- **Variants**: #bodysuit, #slip-full, #chemise.
- **CLO3D**: one-piece pattern with the gusset; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: figures from techniques 5.8; outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
