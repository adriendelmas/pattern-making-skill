# Garter Belt {#garter-belt}

- **Aliases**: suspender belt, garter belt with suspenders, suspender girdle (wide version); fr: porte-jarretelles, ceinture à jarretelles
- **Family**: lingerie, foundations
- **Recognize**: a waist or hip band (4 to 15 cm tall, narrow or wide) with 4 or 6 suspender straps hanging from it, each ending in a clip (metal or plastic with a rubber stopper) that holds a stocking top; closure by hooks at CB or CF.
- **Pieces**: belt panel front (1) and back (1) or one length (1) with a CB join, suspender straps (4 or 6) in elastic with a slider/adjuster, garter clips (4 or 6), hook-and-eye tape (1), elastic or lining (1 optional).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. The belt is a curved band: its upper edge fits the waist or high hip and its lower edge the lower hip, so each half is a slightly curved trapezoid, shorter at the top than at the bottom (waist smaller than hip) and rises a little at the front. The suspender straps attach to the lower edge: front pair at about the line of the thigh front (a few cm either side of the centre, positions not known as numbers), side pair or back pair on the lower edge at the back; the strap is a narrow elastic with an adjuster and clip, 4 to 6 per belt. Sewn: belt panels to each other at the side seams, strap tops bartacked to the lower edge, hook-and-eye tape to the back edges.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take waist, high hip, hip, and a front length from the belt line to the stocking top (stocking top height is a measure to take on the body in standing posture). 2. Belt height 4 to 10 cm for a narrow belt; make the lower edge reach the high hip. 3. Belt length: non-stretch fabric = body measurement at that line plus a closure overlap of 2.5 to 4 cm (hook-and-eye overlap, techniques 5.9) and 0 to 2 cm ease; in elastic or power net, body measurement x 0.85 to 0.95 (typical). 4. Strap lengths: measured on the body from the belt edge to the stocking top, standing, then add the adjuster travel (value not known). 5. Attach straps at the bottom edge with a reinforcing bar tack over a strengthening patch.
- **Sewing order**: belt layers, seams, bone or stay if used, straps with sliders, hook-and-eye at the back, edge elastic.
- **Finishes and notions**: 4 to 6 garter clips, sliders, elastic 10 to 15 mm for the straps, plush elastic at the belt edge, hook-and-eye tape 2 to 3 rows.
- **Fabrics**: satin, lace over net, power net for a firm belt; non-stretch lining at the strap points.
- **Fit faults**: belt rolls: too narrow, widen it; straps slip off: clips not fitted or too loose; stocking pulls the belt up: shorten the straps.
- **Variants**: #shapewear, #corset-modern (with suspender tabs).
- **CLO3D**: model the belt with its straps as narrow elastic strips attached at the lower edge; clips cannot be simulated; the user must pin the strap ends to a stocking layer if one is modelled.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; strap positions unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
