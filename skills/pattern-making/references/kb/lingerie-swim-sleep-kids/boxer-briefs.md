# Boxer Briefs {#boxer-briefs}

- **Aliases**: trunks, boxer-brief, long-leg briefs; fr: boxer (ajusté), caleçon ajusté
- **Family**: menswear underwear (knit)
- **Recognize**: fitted tube legs ending at mid-thigh, pouch at the front with or without a fly, wide elastic waistband, flatlock or coverstitched seams.
- **Pieces**: front (1, or 2 with a CF seam), back (1, or 2 with a CB seam), crotch gusset (optional, 1), pouch lining (1), waistband elastic (1), leg hems (2).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified outlines. Front and back are short fitted trousers: the leg extends from the crotch to mid-thigh (about 12 to 20 cm below the crotch, typical), the inseam is a nearly straight line from crotch C down to the hem H and the side seam from waist W straight to the hem. The pouch is an outward curve of the front crotch (see #mens-briefs). The seat curve is deeper at the back. Front inseam is sewn to back inseam; side seams front to back; the crotch curve front to back (or via a gusset).
- **Drafting**: take a short fitted trouser block, reduce horizontally by 10 to 15 percent on a 4-way stretch jersey; leg length 12 to 20 cm below the crotch; waistband 30 to 40 mm elastic at 0.85.
- **Sewing order**: front to back at the side seams, inseam, crotch, pouch lining, waistband, hems.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic waistband, flatlock or coverstitch, optional front fly.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey with elastane, modal, microfibre; good recovery so the legs do not ride up.
- **Fit faults**: rides up: legs too short or too wide: lengthen and narrow; too tight at the thigh: widen the hem.
- **Variants**: #mens-briefs, #boyshort, #boxer-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: as tight trousers: mesh 6 mm, knit fabric preset.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; lengths typical.
- **Status**: unverified
