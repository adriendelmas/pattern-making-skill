# Children's Elastic-Waist Trousers {#childrens-elastic-waist-trousers}

- **Aliases**: kids' pull-on trousers, jogging bottoms, sweatpants (children), toddler pants; fr: pantalon enfant à taille élastiquée
- **Family**: childrenswear
- **Recognize**: pull-on trousers with an elastic waist (no fly), straight legs, often a cuff at the ankle or a growth cuff, optional side pockets and drawstring.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband casing (cut-on or separate, 1), waist elastic, cuff (2, optional), pocket bags (2 to 4).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified numbers. A simple trouser block with a shallow crotch curve: the crotch depth of children is lower (rise short; the child's rise is measured); waist and hip are almost the same size in young children so the pattern is nearly straight from waist to hip; back crotch curve deeper than front. Legs straight or tapered; hem 4 to 6 cm of growth allowance (techniques 11.5). Waist casing 3.5 cm + elastic.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take waist, hip, rise (sitting measure), inseam, height. 2. Hip ease 8 to 14 cm (loose; 8 to 12 per techniques 11.5 for chest). 3. Rise = measured sitting rise + 2 cm ease (typical). 4. Waist: elastic at 0.80 to 0.90 of the waist (typical for children), casing 2 cm above elastic. 5. Growth: add 4 to 6 cm to the hem or a deep cuff (techniques 11.5). 6. Drawstrings: EN 14682: cords and drawstrings at the waist: free ends of cords at the chest or waist not longer than 140 mm (secondary source), no cords in the hood and neck area for under 7 years; check the standard before use.
- **Sewing order**: pockets, inseams, crotch, side seams, casing, elastic, hems.
- **Finishes and notions**: 20 to 30 mm elastic, narrow hem, flat or clean-finished casing, bartack at stress points.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, fleece, French terry, twill; shrinkage allowance.
- **Fit faults**: drops at the back: raise the back rise; too tight at the thigh: widen; waist leaves marks: lengthen elastic.
- **Variants**: #kids-dungarees, #pajama-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: child avatar; elastic waist ratio 0.85; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: ratios techniques 11.5 and a secondary-source EN 14682 summary; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
