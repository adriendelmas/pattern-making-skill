# Baby Bonnet {#baby-bonnet}

- **Aliases**: christening bonnet, sun bonnet, baby cap, pixie bonnet; fr: bonnet de bébé, béguin, capeline
- **Family**: babywear, accessories
- **Recognize**: a close-fitting hood-like cap tied under the chin; types: a two-piece brimmed bonnet (crown plus brim), a simple one-piece cap with a back seam, a knit cap.
- **Pieces**: crown (2 pieces joined at the back, or 4), crown lining (matching), brim (1 plus brim lining 1, interfaced), ties (2), optional ruffle or lace trim (1), neckband (1).
- **Shape**: text source (tutorials): the crown is made of 2 or 4 pieces sewn pairwise and then to each other (one tutorial uses four crown pieces; another tapes 2 crown pattern pieces together); the brim is sewn to the brim lining along the curved edge, turned and interfaced; ties of 1/4 inch bias tape cut as one 40 inch piece pinned 1/4 inch in from the straight edge of the brim with the extra length split equally on each side; seam allowance 3/8 inch (0.95 cm) in one tutorial; one pioneer-style bonnet has a 7 x 14 inch crown rectangle and ties 2 x 18 inches. No source gives a head-circumference-based draft (unverified). General shape (unverified): the crown is a half-oval gathered into the back edge; the brim is a curved band of 5 to 8 cm wide, curved to wrap the face.
- **Drafting**: no source numbers: measure the head circumference (newborn typically 33 to 37 cm, an approximate range to be checked against a size table), crown width = half head circumference + 1 to 2 cm of ease, crown height from the front hairline over the head to the nape, brim length = half head circumference; test a muslin before final cloth.
- **Sewing order**: crown pieces, lining, brim assembled, brim to crown, ties, finish.
- **Finishes and notions**: bias binding, soft ties (very short or ribbon sewn in; see safety), lace.
- **Fabrics**: cotton lawn, batiste, jersey, linen; interfaced brim in woven.
- **Fit faults**: too tight: widen crown by 1 cm; brim blocks view: shorten.
- **Variants**: #bib.
- **CLO3D**: small curved pieces; mesh 4 mm; baby avatar head.
- **Confidence**: construction text source (tutorials); drafting numbers unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
