# Thong {#thong}

- **Aliases**: thong panty; fr: string, tanga (partial)
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: small front triangle or panel, a narrow back strip that goes between the buttocks, a gusset in the crotch, leg elastics that continue up to the waist elastic.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1, narrow), gusset outer and lining (1 each), one continuous elastic or separate leg and waist elastics.
- **Shape**: text source techniques 5.7: take the brief draft; trim the back to a narrow strip: width at the crotch 4 to 5 cm, tapering to 1.5 to 2 cm at the waist; the leg curve rises to meet the waist at 9 to 11 cm from the CB; gusset width at the crotch 5 to 6 cm; elastic around the whole contour at 0.85 to 0.90. Outline of the front is trade practice, unverified: a triangle or shallow trapezoid; BP is a long strip, wider at the crotch end than at the waist end.
- **Drafting**: as techniques 5.7 thong drafting. Back strip length from waist to crotch = body back rise x (1 - 0.03 to 0.05) (vertical reduction, typical).
- **Sewing order**: gusset to the back strip, front to gusset, elastic applied in one pass around the contour, waist elastic joined.
- **Finishes and notions**: fold-over or picot elastic 6 to 10 mm.
- **Fabrics**: stretch lace, microfibre, cotton jersey; high stretch.
- **Fit faults**: rides into the body: strip too narrow or too short; side bands loose: shorten elastic 3 percent.
- **Variants**: #g-string, #basic-brief.
- **CLO3D**: elastic contour with the ratio; mesh 4 mm so the strip does not tear in the simulation.
- **Confidence**: figures from techniques 5.7 (typical); front outline unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
