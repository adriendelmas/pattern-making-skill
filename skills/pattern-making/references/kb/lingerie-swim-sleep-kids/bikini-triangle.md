# Triangle Bikini {#bikini-triangle}

- **Aliases**: triangle top and bottom, string bikini, slide-triangle bikini; fr: bikini triangle, maillot deux pièces triangle
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: top: two triangles tied at the neck and back with strings, or sliding triangles on a cord; bottoms: low-rise brief with tie sides or narrow sides, lined gusset.
- **Pieces**: top: triangle (2, with lining), ties (2 neck, 2 back), tube casing or channel (2), bottom: front (1), back (1), gusset outer and lining (1 each), tie sides or side strips (2), elastics.
- **Shape**: text source techniques 6.3: top triangle with a base about 14 to 17 cm (bust width), a height of 12 to 16 cm and a side length; tie lengths 40 to 70 cm; elastic around the bust; lining; foam optional. Bottom: as a brief (techniques 5.7) with swim ratios, side width 3 to 8 cm for a tie-side, high leg curve, crotch width 7 to 8 cm double layer. A published free method (Sewguide) draws a triangle from two rectangles 8.5 x 12 inches (about 21.6 x 30.5 cm) folded at the middle; ties are tubes from strips about 1.5 inches wide, one 48 inches and two 24 inches. A body-measurement method (McCunn's book) takes the centre-front-to-band distance, top-to-apex and apex-to-side distances (text description in a search excerpt; the book's own diagrams are the source). Anchors: A inner base, B outer base, C top (strap point); AB attaches to the band (tie), AC and BC finished with elastic or bound.
- **Drafting**: 1. Measure bust width, underbust, apex to side, top to apex. 2. Draw the triangle with base AB, apex C; curve AB convex downward by a few mm. 3. Elastic or binding on all three edges at 0.85 to 0.95; ties 40 to 70 cm (techniques 6.3). 4. Bottom: draft a low-rise brief with the side seam height 3 to 8 cm and a high leg; use swim reductions 8 to 12 percent at the hip. 5. Foam optional; cut lining the same.
- **Sewing order**: top: lining to outer, elastic on edges, channels for slide triangles, ties; bottom: gusset to back, sides, gusset to front, leg elastic, waist or tie-side edges.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 6 to 10 mm, rings, sliders, cord or tube ties.
- **Fabrics**: nylon/elastane 80/20, 4-way stretch, lining in swim lining; techniques 6.1 negative ease.
- **Fit faults**: cup gapes: shorten AC/BC; cup spills at the bottom: base too narrow; strings dig: widen.
- **Variants**: #bikini-bandeau, #bikini-halter, #bikini-brief for underwear.
- **CLO3D**: triangle with elastic seams; ties as narrow strips; mesh 4 mm; pin ties to the avatar to keep them tied.
- **Confidence**: techniques 6.3 and Sewguide for the free-size method; body-measure method per McCunn (not reproduced); outline of bottoms trade practice.
- **Status**: practice
