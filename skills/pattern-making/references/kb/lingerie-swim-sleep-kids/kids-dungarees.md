# Kids' Dungarees {#kids-dungarees}

- **Aliases**: overalls, pinafore trousers, bib-and-brace, salopette; fr: salopette enfant
- **Family**: childrenswear
- **Recognize**: trousers with a bib at the front, shoulder straps (crossed or straight), buttons or snaps at the bib/strap junction, side seam pockets or bib pocket, elastic or straps adjusted by buckles.
- **Pieces**: trouser front (2), trouser back (2), bib front (1 plus facing 1), back waistband or yoke (1), straps (2 plus facings), buttons or buckles, pocket (1 bib, 2 side), CB or side opening.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Trousers with a high waist; the bib is a rectangle with slightly curved sides, width 16 to 20 cm and height 14 to 20 cm for a child (typical guess), joined at the front waist; straps from the top corners crossing at the back (or straight) and buttoned to the back waistband; the side opening is a placket or a side seam zip; waist back elastic. Crotch depth increased for the diaper in toddlers (+3 to 5 cm, typical).
- **Drafting**: trousers per #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers with a raised waist; bib width = chest/3 (typical), strap length adjusted by buttons at multiple positions to allow growth; strap width 3 to 4 cm.
- **Sewing order**: pockets, trousers, bib with facing, straps, join bib to waistband, back elastic, button/hardware.
- **Finishes and notions**: buttons (reinforced), snaps or buckles, elastic back waist 20 to 25 mm, hem allowance 4 to 6 cm, cuffs.
- **Fabrics**: denim, twill, corduroy, cotton; denim shrinkage 3 percent.
- **Fit faults**: straps slide off: cross them or add a back clip; crotch low: raise the crotch; bib gapes: reduce width.
- **Variants**: #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers, #romper.
- **CLO3D**: trousers + bib + straps; child avatar; buttons as trims.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; unverified dimensions.
- **Status**: unverified
