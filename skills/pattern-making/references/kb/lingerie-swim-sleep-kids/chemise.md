# Chemise {#chemise}

- **Aliases**: shift, short nightdress, babydoll (with a gathered skirt), camisole dress; fr: nuisette, chemise de nuit courte, nuisette (US: chemise)
- **Family**: lingerie, nightwear
- **Recognize**: a loose short dress on thin straps, an A-line or gathered skirt to hip or mid-thigh, lace or ribbon at the bust, no sleeves.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (1 or 2), bodice back (1), skirt front (1), skirt back (1), straps (2), lace, facing, optional empire seam.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A simple chemise is an A-line tube in one piece from the bust to the hem with a curved top edge; a babydoll adds a bust yoke and a gathered skirt: the skirt panels are rectangles gathered to the yoke seam with a gathering ratio of 1.5 to 2 (typical). Ease 10 to 16 cm at the bust and 12 to 20 cm at the hem for an A-line.
- **Drafting**: from the sloper without waist suppression; hem width 1.2 to 1.5 x the bust width for A-line (typical); length to taste; top edge facing 3 cm.
- **Sewing order**: bodice darts or yoke, side seams, straps, facing, skirt, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: French seams, lace, narrow hems.
- **Fabrics**: satin, crepe de chine, cotton lawn, lace.
- **Fit faults**: bust gapes: add a small dart; rides up: add length or anchor straps.
- **Variants**: #slip-full, #nightgown, #teddy.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset 8 mm; gathers with the shirring tool.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
