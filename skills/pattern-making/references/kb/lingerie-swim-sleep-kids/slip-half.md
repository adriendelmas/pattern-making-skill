# Half Slip {#slip-half}

- **Aliases**: waist slip, underskirt, petticoat; fr: jupon, sous-jupe
- **Family**: lingerie, slips
- **Recognize**: an underskirt with an elastic or yoke waist that hangs from the waist to above the knee or the ankle; lace trim at the hem; a side or CB slit possible.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), waistband or casing (1), lace trim; optional yoke.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A straight or slightly A-line skirt made of two panels, joined at the side seams; width at the hem is greater than the hip by 4 to 8 cm, the length shorter than the dress by 2 to 5 cm (typical); the top edge is a straight line finished with elastic casing, or a yoke curved at the hip. Slit at CB or side seam 8 to 12 cm (typical).
- **Drafting**: from a skirt block: remove darts into ease at the waist (elastic) and increase side-seam flare 2 to 4 cm; elastic waist 20 to 25 mm; length 5 to 10 cm shorter than the skirt worn on top.
- **Sewing order**: side seams, slit, hem, waistband and elastic.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 20 to 25 mm, lace edge trim, narrow hems.
- **Fabrics**: tricot, satin, polyester lining; anti-static finish.
- **Fit faults**: shows below the dress: shorten; cling: use anti-static or a larger flare.
- **Variants**: #slip-full, #french-knickers.
- **CLO3D**: skirt pattern with elastic waist, mesh 10 mm; under the dress, use layer order.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
