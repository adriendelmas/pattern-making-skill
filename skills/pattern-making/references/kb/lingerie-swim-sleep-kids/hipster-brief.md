# Hipster Brief {#hipster-brief}

- **Aliases**: hip-hugger, low-rise brief with wide side; fr: culotte taille basse
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: sits low on the hip, wide side panel, moderate leg cut, usually full seat coverage; waist edge usually a plush-back elastic.
- **Pieces**: as #basic-brief.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified numbers. As #basic-brief with a lower waist line (low hip) and a wide side (techniques 5.7 says "low rise, wide side"; the side height is not known as a number, about 8 to 12 cm is a starting guess to confirm on a sample). Leg curve moderate, between bikini and brief.
- **Drafting**: as #basic-brief; front rise 10 to 14 cm, back 17 to 22 cm (low rise, techniques 5.7); waist elastic 0.85 to 0.90.
- **Sewing order**: as #basic-brief.
- **Finishes and notions**: 12 to 25 mm waist elastic, 8 to 10 mm leg elastic.
- **Fabrics**: as #basic-brief; seamless knit is common.
- **Fit faults**: rolls at the waist: widen waist elastic or lower the front rise; leg digs: lengthen elastic.
- **Variants**: #bikini-brief, #boyshort.
- **CLO3D**: as #basic-brief.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; side height unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
