# Boardshorts {#boardshorts}

- **Aliases**: board shorts, surf shorts, long swim trunks; fr: boardshort, short de surf
- **Family**: swimwear (sports)
- **Recognize**: longer than swim trunks (knee length), often with a flat waistband and a lace-up or velcro fly, 4-way stretch fabric options, tight flatlock seams, small pocket at the thigh or back, a drawcord, no liner or a mesh liner.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband (1, curved), fly flap (1), inseam gussets (optional), back pocket (1), drawcord.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Knee-length straight trousers cut in woven fabric: crotch curve as for trousers, a gusset (diamond) at the crotch for movement is common, a flat waistband with a curved fly and 2 to 3 closure points (velcro and a tie) - patterns are scarce and not verified; the leg hem is straight. Boardshort lengths quoted by retailers: 15.5 inch outseam (Vans listing); the pattern length for a different style is not known.
- **Drafting**: from short trousers: 14 to 18 cm ease at the hip, crotch depth = rise + 2 to 4 cm, outseam to the knee; waistband straight, 4 to 5 cm wide.
- **Sewing order**: back pocket, crotch and inseams, side seams, fly, waistband, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock or 4-thread overlock seams, covered elastic or flat waistband, velcro.
- **Fabrics**: polyester or recycled polyester, 4-way stretch microfibre, UPF 50.
- **Fit faults**: chafing at the inseam: use flatlock; rides up: lengthen; waist loose: add elastic at the CB.
- **Variants**: #swim-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: as woven shorts with stretch fabrics; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; lengths vary; unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
