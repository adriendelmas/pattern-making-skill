# Bandeau Bikini {#bikini-bandeau}

- **Aliases**: bandeau top, strapless bikini, tube bikini; fr: bikini bandeau, bandeau de bain
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: a straight band across the bust, strapless, often with a twist at CF, ties or removable straps, lined, with or without cups and bones.
- **Pieces**: techniques 6.3: a bandeau x1 (outer), lining, optional cups (2), bones (2 to 4), silicone gripper elastic, optional halter strap (1), CB closure (hook and eye or ties); bottom as #bikini-triangle.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A curved rectangle: length = underbust-to-bust circumference (a measure at the bust) x (1 - 5 to 8 percent), height 8 to 14 cm (typical); the top and bottom edges curve slightly to follow the bust and ribs (top convex, bottom concave); a CF twist is made by an extra tube piece. The top edge finished with gripper elastic, the bottom with elastic.
- **Drafting**: as techniques 6.1 negative ease: bust 5 to 8 percent reduction, cups hold the shape; side seam at the bust level; use boning channels (8 to 12 mm, techniques 5.9) if firm.
- **Sewing order**: lining to outer, cups, bones, elastic top and bottom, CB closure.
- **Finishes and notions**: silicone gripper, flat bones (if any), hook-and-eye.
- **Fabrics**: swim knit with firm lining; power mesh behind.
- **Fit faults**: slides down: shorten the band or add gripper; digs: widen the band.
- **Variants**: #bikini-triangle, #strapless-bra.
- **CLO3D**: model gripper as friction cannot be simulated; use an avatar with bust.
- **Confidence**: figures from techniques 6.1 and 5.9; outline unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
