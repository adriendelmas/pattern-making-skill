# Swimsuit with Built-In Cups {#swimsuit-built-in-cups}

- **Aliases**: padded swimsuit, shelf-bra swimsuit, underwired swimsuit; fr: maillot de bain à bonnets, maillot de bain avec armatures
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: a one-piece or bikini top with cup pockets inside the lining, foam (moulded or flat) cups that lie between the outer and the lining, optionally an underwire channel and a wide under-bust elastic.
- **Pieces**: as #swimsuit-one-piece plus cups (2, foam), cup lining (2), under-bust elastic (1), optional wire channel (2), wires (2), strap adjusters.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Cup is sandwiched: the outer fabric covers the foam, and the lining is cut with a cup pocket (the cup edge stitched to the lining along an arc, open at the top or side to insert the foam). With an underwire: the cup and wire line follow #underwire-full-cup-bra. A shelf-bra variant uses a lining with a under-bust elastic (techniques 6.2: under-bust elastic 10 to 12 mm at 0.80 to 0.85, in the lining).
- **Drafting**: techniques 6.2: cups placed 2.5 to 3 cm apart; bust width reduced 5 to 8 percent (cups hold the shape); under-bust elastic 0.80 to 0.85. Foam cup shape comes from a purchased cup (the shape is not drafted from the body); the lining around it is trimmed to the cup trim line.
- **Sewing order**: lining with cup pockets, outer, body seams, under-bust elastic, neck/armhole elastics, leg elastics, insert cups.
- **Finishes and notions**: foam cups, optional wires and 10 to 12 mm channels, hook-and-eye for halter ties.
- **Fabrics**: as #swimsuit-one-piece, lining of power mesh at the bust.
- **Fit faults**: cups wrinkle: size wrong; gap at the neckline: shorten the neck elastic; cups migrate: stitch the pocket edge.
- **Variants**: #swimsuit-one-piece, #bikini-triangle, #underwire-full-cup-bra.
- **CLO3D**: cups as stiff pieces; fit on an avatar with breasts; mesh 4 mm.
- **Confidence**: figures techniques 6.2 (typical); outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
