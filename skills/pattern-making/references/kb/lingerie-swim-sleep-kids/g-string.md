# G-String {#g-string}

- **Aliases**: g string, string thong; fr: string (à ficelle)
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: small front triangle or pouch, a single string at the back (about 1 cm wide), strings or elastics at the sides; minimal fabric.
- **Pieces**: front pouch (1, outer plus lining), back string (1), side strings (2).
- **Shape**: text source techniques 5.7: front triangle plus a back strip of about 1 cm. Further outlines unverified: the pouch is a triangle with its apex at the crotch; its top edge is sewn to the waist string and its two sides to the side strings.
- **Drafting**: pouch height not known as a ratio (try 0.5 to 0.7 of the front rise on a toile, a guess); string length = half hip x 0.85 each side (elastic ratio from techniques 5.7).
- **Sewing order**: pouch outer and lining sewn together, strings slid through and bartacked.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 6 to 10 mm, string, cord or chain.
- **Fabrics**: stretch lace, microfibre, satin with elastane.
- **Fit faults**: string cuts: use a wider elastic; pouch gapes: shorten the waist string.
- **Variants**: #thong.
- **CLO3D**: pouch with elastic edges; strings as narrow strips.
- **Confidence**: figures from techniques 5.7; the rest unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
