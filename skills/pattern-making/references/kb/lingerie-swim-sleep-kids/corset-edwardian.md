# Edwardian Corset {#corset-edwardian}

- **Aliases**: S-bend corset, straight-front corset, 1900s corset; fr: corset 1900, corset droit-devant, corset « sablier » (S-bend)
- **Family**: corsetry, historical
- **Recognize**: a long, rigid front busk (straight, 13 inch in one reproduction), a long line over the abdomen and low bust, a long hip, small waist reduction, elastic garters at the front and the sides; lower and straighter front than the Victorian curve, with hip pads added in the S-bend look.
- **Pieces**: documented reproductions: 7 pieces per side (14 total, no gussets) in Sew Historically's straight-front linen corset; Truly Victorian's 1903 pattern uses hip gore pieces and centre backs; simplified commercial patterns of 5 panels without gussets. Busk (1 pair, wide steel), bone casings, lacing strips, binding, garters.
- **Shape**: text source: the straight front is created by a rigid wide busk and metal boning at the front V, with width added at hip and bust and the waist reduced; the gores of earlier patterns are removed so all seams run top to bottom (Sew Historically). The Truly Victorian 1903 pattern: low full bust and long hips, with hip pads; it lists 16 spring steel bones 8 mm wide of lengths 32 to 36 cm and a pair of 13 inch busks and 30 size 00 grommets (Farthingales). Exact outlines of the panels are not in these sources (unverified). Garters sewn to the front and sides keep the corset down.
- **Drafting**: as #corset-modern with: a straight CF line (flat busk), a long front panel, lower bust, wider hip spring (more than in the Victorian; value not known), reduction smaller than for a training corset (period marketing as a health corset; no number found). Bone lengths = channel length minus about 2.5 to 3.8 cm (techniques 7.2). Early 1900s patterns are available as period drafts; use a published reproduction as the starting point.
- **Sewing order**: as #corset-modern, with garter attachments last.
- **Finishes and notions**: wide steel busk 11 to 13 inch (Past Patterns by size: 11 inch for sizes 8-10, 12 inch for 14-20, 13 inch for 22-26), 8 mm spring steel, 30 eyelets, lace or ribbon trim, elastic garters.
- **Fabrics**: coutil, herringbone cotton-linen, silk; single layer of coutil with casing tape is documented (Truly Victorian).
- **Fit faults**: pushes the abdomen forward: lower the top edge of the busk panel; the hips look flat: add a hip pad; corset rides up: add garters.
- **Variants**: #corset-victorian, #waist-cincher.
- **CLO3D**: as #corset-modern; add a hip pad as a separate fabric volume; check the straight-front line on the avatar.
- **Confidence**: documentation sources (Sew Historically, Farthingales, Past Patterns); panel outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
