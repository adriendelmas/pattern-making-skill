# Tankini {#tankini}

- **Aliases**: tankini top, two-piece swimsuit with a tank top, swim top; fr: tankini
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: a swim top like a tank/camisole in swim fabric that reaches the waist or hip, worn with separate bottoms; built-in bra lining, often a shelf bra, hem elastic or a plain hem.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), front lining/bra lining (1), back lining (1), shelf elastic (1), cups (2, optional), neck/armhole bindings or elastics, hem (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A tank pattern in swim fabric: bust, waist and hip reduced by the swim percentages (techniques 6.1: bust 5 to 8, waist 10 to 15, hip 8 to 12 percent), with the shelf bra built in: the lining is cut like the outer to the underbust line and an under-bust elastic (10 to 12 mm at 0.80 to 0.85, techniques 6.2) is sewn to the lining. The hem: straight or curved at the sides; optional ruching; length to the hip or just above the bottom's waist.
- **Drafting**: from a knit tank block with 4-way stretch fabrics reduced as techniques 6.1; hem length to the high hip; cups placed 2.5 to 3 cm apart; elastic ratio 0.85 to 0.90 at neck and armholes.
- **Sewing order**: lining to outer at the neck, side seams, shelf elastic, armholes, neck, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch hem, elastic, ball-point needle.
- **Fabrics**: nylon/elastane swim, lining in power mesh.
- **Fit faults**: rides up: lengthen; gapes at the neck: shorten elastic.
- **Variants**: #swimsuit-one-piece, #swimsuit-built-in-cups.
- **CLO3D**: knit tank with swim fabric.
- **Confidence**: figures techniques 6.1 and 6.2; outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
