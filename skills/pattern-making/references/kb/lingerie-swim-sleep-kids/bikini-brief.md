# Bikini Brief {#bikini-brief}

- **Aliases**: bikini panties, low-rise brief, string-side brief; fr: culotte bikini, slip bikini
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: low rise, high leg curve, narrow side (a thin band at the side seam), often with ties or a thin elastic side; shows the hip.
- **Pieces**: as #basic-brief: front (1), back (1), gusset outer and lining (1 each), elastic.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified outlines. As #basic-brief with the side seam height reduced to 5 to 8 cm (techniques 5.7 table) and the leg curve rising higher on the side; the waist edge is cut lower, about at the low hip; back coverage moderate. Side-tie versions replace the side seam with a short tie sewn into the pointed side corner. Gusset as #basic-brief.
- **Drafting**: as #basic-brief with low rise (front rise 10 to 14 cm, back 17 to 22 cm, techniques 5.7) and a leg curve up to 8 to 10 cm above the crotch line at the side seam for the high-cut look.
- **Sewing order**: as #basic-brief.
- **Finishes and notions**: narrow elastic or lace, 6 to 10 mm.
- **Fabrics**: as #basic-brief; lighter, more stretch is common.
- **Fit faults**: slips down: raise the waist edge 1 cm and shorten the waist elastic; side digs: widen the side to 6 to 8 cm.
- **Variants**: #basic-brief, #hipster-brief; swimwear equivalent in #bikini-triangle.
- **CLO3D**: as #basic-brief.
- **Confidence**: numbers from techniques 5.7 (typical); outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
