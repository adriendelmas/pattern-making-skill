# Burkini {#burkini}

- **Aliases**: modest swimwear, full-coverage swimsuit, sun-protection swimsuit; fr: burkini, maillot de bain couvrant
- **Family**: swimwear (modest)
- **Recognize**: a set of a long-sleeved tunic, leggings (or trousers) and a head covering (hood or cap), in quick-dry swim fabric, flatlock seams and often a built-in lining at the bust and crotch.
- **Pieces**: tunic front (1), tunic back (1), sleeve (2), neck/hood (hood: 2 side pieces or 1 with a CB seam), leggings (2), crotch gusset (1), lining at bust and crotch (1), cuffs, elastic.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A tunic-over-leggings construction: the tunic is a relaxed fit (more ease than a swimsuit) to hip or mid-thigh, so it floats without clinging; the leggings follow the leggings with gusset in the techniques sheet (techniques 6.4: reductions 10 to 15 percent for 50 to 75 percent stretch). The hood: a close-fitting hood with a face opening and a neck drape, or a separate cap; hood neck edge length equals the tunic neck edge length.
- **Drafting**: 1. Tunic: loose knit block with 6 to 12 cm ease at the bust and hip (typical). 2. Leggings per techniques 6.4. 3. Sleeve with cuff at 0.80 to 0.90 of the wrist. 4. Hood: head circumference + ease 4 to 6 cm (typical). 5. Lining in the bust area. 6. Check the strain at the shoulders and the crotch.
- **Sewing order**: lining, tunic body, sleeves, hood, leggings with gusset, cuffs and hems.
- **Finishes and notions**: flatlock or coverstitch seams (sources mention flatlock as common), elastic at the leg and waist, zip at the back neck (optional).
- **Fabrics**: polyester-heavy swim knits with elastane (chlorine resistant); UPF 50+ fabric; avoid transparency when wet.
- **Fit faults**: tunic floats up in water: add length or a vent; hood slips: elastic at the face edge.
- **Variants**: #swimsuit-one-piece, #tankini.
- **CLO3D**: layered pieces (tunic over leggings); swim fabric preset; mesh 6 mm; set layer order.
- **Confidence**: no source found with burkini drafting; construction from general practice and the leggings sheet; unverified shapes.
- **Status**: unverified
