# Kimono Robe {#kimono-robe}

- **Aliases**: Western kimono robe, wrap kimono, happi (similar), yukata-style robe; fr: kimono (peignoir), robe kimono
- **Family**: nightwear, loungewear
- **Recognize**: T-shaped wrap garment: straight body panels, wide rectangular sleeves, a straight collar band running around the neck and down both fronts, a wide sash (obi or tie) at the waist; the left side wraps over the right in daywear.
- **Pieces**: a traditional kimono (Japanese construction): body panels (migoro): 2, each running from the front hem over the shoulder to the back hem, joined at CB; front overlap panels (okumi): 2, sleeves (sode): 2, collar (eri): 1, overlap collar (kake-eri) optional, sash. For a Western robe: back (1 or 2), front (2), sleeve (2 rectangles), front band/collar (1), belt (1).
- **Shape**: text source for the principle (traditional kimono is cut from straight panels of a bolt about 36 to 38 cm wide with no curves; unverified for the exact current figure): all pieces are rectangles; the body is cut in one length from the front hem over the shoulder to the back hem with a neck slit; the sleeves are rectangles sewn to the body panel's side edge at the shoulder line. In the Western robe: back and fronts rectangles slightly shaped at the neck; a front band (collar) is a long rectangle 7 to 10 cm wide sewn around the neck and down both fronts; sleeve: a rectangle 40 to 60 cm wide (full width) and 30 to 45 cm long, sewn to the body's side edge with the armscye opening; the underarm is open or sewn.
- **Drafting**: 1. Body width (each half) = half the chest + 12 to 20 cm ease (typical, with straight sides). 2. Sleeve length from the shoulder line; width of the sleeve rectangle (the 'sode' width). 3. Neck opening: slit width 8 to 10 cm (typical) per side from CB. 4. Front overlap: right front crosses over left; front bands 7 to 10 cm. 5. Length to knee or ankle.
- **Sewing order**: body seams, sleeve rectangles to shoulders, side seams (leaving armhole), collar band, hem, belt.
- **Finishes and notions**: tie belt, optional inside ties, narrow hems, collar interfacing.
- **Fabrics**: silk, satin, cotton, jersey, viscose; straight grain prints.
- **Fit faults**: sleeves too wide for the arm: shorten the sleeve rectangle; collar rolls: interface; gapes at the neck: add overlap.
- **Variants**: #robe, #pajama-top.
- **CLO3D**: rectangles with straight sewing; the sleeve rectangle sewn to the body edge; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: construction principle text source (kimono is straight cut); Western dimensions typical; widths of bolts unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
