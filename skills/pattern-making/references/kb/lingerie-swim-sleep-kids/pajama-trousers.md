# Pajama Trousers {#pajama-trousers}

- **Aliases**: pyjama bottoms, sleep pants, lounge pants; fr: pantalon de pyjama
- **Family**: nightwear
- **Recognize**: loose straight trousers with an elastic waist (or a drawstring), no pockets or side pockets, optional front fly, straight leg ending at the ankle or a cuff.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband casing (cut-on or separate, 1), elastic, cuff (2, optional), pocket bag (2, optional).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Trouser block without darts: each piece a straight-sided shape; crotch curves: front crotch shorter, back crotch longer with more depth (as trousers, see other file), side seams straight; waist top is straight, cut with a 3 to 4 cm casing. Leg width at the hem 24 to 30 cm each (full leg 48 to 60 cm round, typical guess). Seam allowance 1.5 cm.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take hip, waist, rise, inseam. 2. Hip ease 16 to 24 cm total (typical). 3. Crotch depth = body rise + 2 to 3 cm. 4. Elastic casing 2.5 to 3 cm + 1 cm; elastic 25 to 40 mm at 0.75 to 0.85 of the waist (typical). 5. Fly optional: add a 3 cm wide overlap.
- **Sewing order**: inseams, crotch, side seams, casing, elastic, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 25 to 40 mm, drawstring optional (children: see #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers for safety), French seams.
- **Fabrics**: cotton, flannel, silk, satin, jersey.
- **Fit faults**: crotch pulls: lengthen the rise 1 to 2 cm; waist slips: shorten elastic or add a drawstring.
- **Variants**: #boxer-shorts, #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: trousers with elastic waist seam at 0.80 ratio; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
