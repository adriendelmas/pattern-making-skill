# Swim Briefs {#swim-briefs}

- **Aliases**: Speedo-style briefs, racing briefs, swim slip, men's swim brief; fr: slip de bain
- **Family**: swimwear (menswear)
- **Recognize**: a small stretch brief in swim fabric with a lined front, a drawstring or an elastic waist, high leg openings, no side seams or minimal seams.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), front lining (1), back lining (1), gusset (optional), waist elastic, leg elastic, drawcord.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. As #mens-briefs but flatter: no fly; a double-layer front (outer and lining); the leg curve high; the front of the brief a smooth contour with a seam from the crotch to the waist or a gentle curve in the front pouch.
- **Drafting**: as #mens-briefs with swim reductions (techniques 6.1: hip 8 to 12 percent, waist 10 to 15 percent), leg elastic 0.90 (front 0.95, back 0.85, techniques 6.1), waist elastic 0.85 to 0.90 plus a drawcord.
- **Sewing order**: lining to outer, side seams, crotch seams, leg elastic, waist elastic and cord.
- **Finishes and notions**: elastic 8 to 12 mm, waist elastic 20 to 30 mm, cord.
- **Fabrics**: nylon/elastane, polyester/PBT chlorine-resistant.
- **Fit faults**: cuts at the leg: lengthen; slips: tighten the cord or shorten the elastic.
- **Variants**: #mens-briefs, #swim-shorts.
- **CLO3D**: swim fabric, mesh 4 to 6 mm.
- **Confidence**: techniques ratios typical; outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
