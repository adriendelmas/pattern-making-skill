# High-Waist Brief {#high-waist-brief}

- **Aliases**: high-rise brief, tummy-control brief, full brief; fr: culotte taille haute
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: waist edge at or above the natural waist, full seat coverage, optionally a double-layer front panel; leg cut moderate or high (retro styles).
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), gusset outer and lining (1 each), front lining panel (optional, 1), waist elastic, leg elastics.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified outlines. As #basic-brief with front and back rise raised to reach the natural waist (the amount is not a standard: take it from the measured waist-to-crotch). Waist edge a straight or gently curved line; side seam taller. Control versions add a two-layer front panel stitched into the side seams and the waist edge, with a power-net lining stiffer than the outer (no number found).
- **Drafting**: front rise = body front rise x (1 - V) with V = 3 to 5 percent (as the bodysuit approach in techniques 5.8); back rise likewise; hip width as #basic-brief. Waist elastic 20 to 40 mm at 0.85 to 0.90.
- **Sewing order**: as #basic-brief, with the lining panel sewn before the side seams.
- **Finishes and notions**: wide plush elastic at the waist; leg elastics 8 to 12 mm.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey with elastane, shaping power net for control.
- **Fit faults**: waist rolls: pattern narrower than the body: add 1 cm; back rides: shorten back rise.
- **Variants**: #basic-brief, #shapewear.
- **CLO3D**: layered pieces: set the panel above the base; strain map for the control zone.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; amounts unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
