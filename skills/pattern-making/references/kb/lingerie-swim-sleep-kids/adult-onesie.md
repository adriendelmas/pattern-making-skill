# Adult Onesie {#adult-onesie}

- **Aliases**: adult sleepsuit, union suit, kigurumi, one-piece pajamas, loungesuit; fr: combinaison pyjama adulte, grenouillère adulte
- **Family**: nightwear, loungewear
- **Recognize**: a one-piece garment covering torso, arms and legs, with a front zip from neck to crotch (or a drop seat), a hood (optional), elastic cuffs and ankles, kangaroo pocket; in plush fleece or jersey.
- **Pieces**: front (2, left and right with a zip opening), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), hood (2 side pieces), hood lining (2), cuffs (2), ankle bands (2), pocket (1), zip (1), optional drop-seat flap (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. The body is a loose jumpsuit block: front and back panels joined at the side seams, inseams and a crotch curve; the front opening runs from the neck to the crotch, with a zip and a placket or a facing. Torso loop T of #bodysuit governs the length: T x (1 - V) plus ease; with loose fit add 6 to 10 cm to T (typical). Hood: two side pieces sewn at the top and the back centre with a curved neck edge; the hood neck seam length equals the neckline length. Sleeve: set-in or raglan, 8 to 12 cm ease at the bicep (typical); ankle cuff or hem with elastic.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take the torso loop T, chest, hip, arm and inseam. 2. Ease 14 to 24 cm at the chest and hip in jersey/fleece (typical). 3. Crotch depth = body rise + 3 to 5 cm. 4. Sleeve and leg: straight, with cuffs of rib sized at 0.75 to 0.85 of the wrist/ankle. 5. Hood: head circumference + 6 to 10 cm ease around the face edge. 6. Pocket 20 x 25 cm.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders, side seams, inseams, crotch, zip, hood, sleeve, cuffs.
- **Finishes and notions**: open-end zipper of 70 to 90 cm (length to be measured on the pattern opening), elastic or rib cuffs, hood lining.
- **Fabrics**: fleece, French terry, jersey, flannel; plush fabrics shrink and need an allowance.
- **Fit faults**: crotch drops: raise the crotch depth; zip gapes at the neck: add overlap.
- **Variants**: #sleepsuit-with-feet, #robe, #bodysuit.
- **CLO3D**: fleece preset, mesh 8 mm, zipper as an open-end zip trim; fit on an avatar with a relaxed pose.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
