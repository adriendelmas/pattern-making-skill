# Shapewear {#shapewear}

- **Aliases**: control garment, body shaper, girdle, compression bodysuit, tummy control shorts; fr: gaine, body gainant, sous-vêtement modelant
- **Family**: lingerie, foundations
- **Recognize**: close-fitting garment in thick powernet or compression knit, double layer in the front panel, flat seams, bonded edges or wide elastic, hook-and-eye or snap crotch in bodysuits.
- **Pieces**: front outer (1), front inner/control panel (1), back (1), side panels (2) optional, gusset outer and lining (1 each), straps (2) or band elastic, hook-and-eye or snap tape, bones or stays (optional).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. A fitted bodysuit or short block: the front panel is double layered at the abdomen, with the control layer shorter and stiffer than the outer so it pulls the abdomen in; side seams are shaped to the waist; the crotch gusset is a lined diamond or rounded rectangle as in #basic-brief; straps and bust cups are as for a bodysuit (#bodysuit). Pressure follows Laplace P = T / r (techniques 6.6).
- **Drafting**: techniques 6.6: reduction 15 to 25 percent horizontally on a fabric with 100 to 150 percent stretch, graduated pressure from the same reduction (a smaller radius gets a higher pressure), do not exceed u = 0.5 of the fabric stretch (techniques 1.3); vertical reduction 3 to 5 percent as in #high-waist-brief and #bodysuit. Check with a pressure tester for medical garments, which use certified garments, not a pattern.
- **Sewing order**: control panel to outer, flat seams, gusset, edge elastic, closures.
- **Finishes and notions**: bonded seams, silicone grip at leg and top edges, 3 to 4 rows of hook-and-eye at the crotch for bodysuits.
- **Fabrics**: powernet, nylon/spandex with 100 to 150 percent stretch, bonded elastics.
- **Fit faults**: rolls at the top: wider band or silicone; digs: reduce the reduction or widen the edge; double layer shifts: bond the panel to the outer.
- **Variants**: #high-waist-brief, #waist-cincher, #bodysuit.
- **CLO3D**: layer two fabrics with different stretch; strain map; collision thickness 0.
- **Confidence**: numbers techniques 6.6 (typical); outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
