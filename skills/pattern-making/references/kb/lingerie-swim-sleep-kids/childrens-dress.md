# Children's Dress {#childrens-dress}

- **Aliases**: girl's dress, toddler dress, yoke dress, party dress; fr: robe d'enfant, robe fillette
- **Family**: childrenswear
- **Recognize**: a bodice with no darts, a gathered or A-line skirt, a yoke or waist seam, short sleeves, a CB zip or button closure.
- **Pieces**: bodice front (1), bodice back (2), skirt front (1), skirt back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), neck facing (1), lining, closure, optional sash or pockets.
- **Shape**: text source (techniques 11.5): use a block with no darts; ease 8 to 12 cm at the chest; neck width = neck / 5 (+0.5 for toddlers); front neck depth = width + 1.5; armhole slightly lower (+1 cm); hem allowance 4 to 6 cm for growth; openings at CB or at the shoulder (envelope neck overlap 2.5 cm) for babies. Skirt is a gathered rectangle or flared panel; the waist seam goes above the natural waist (empire) in younger children because they have little waist definition (typical practice). Outlines unverified as drawn patterns.
- **Drafting**: 1. Take chest, waist, hip, back length, height; use the child's size by height. 2. Block with no darts and 8 to 12 cm chest ease. 3. Neck width = neck/5 (+0.5 toddler), front depth = width + 1.5 (techniques 11.5). 4. Armhole +1 cm. 5. Skirt width = hip + gathering ratio of 1.5 to 2 (typical). 6. Hem allowance 4 to 6 cm; consider growth tuck (see #children-growth-allowances-grading). 7. Safety: no cords at the neck for under 7 years (EN 14682, techniques 11.5).
- **Sewing order**: darts, bodice shoulders, neck facing, sleeves, side seams, skirt, waist seam, CB opening, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip or buttons with covered ends, snaps, narrow hem, clear elastic in the waist seam.
- **Fabrics**: cotton poplin, lawn, jersey, corduroy; wash shrinkage 2 to 4 percent allowed (typical).
- **Fit faults**: neck gapes: reduce neck width; hem uneven: check the CB length; armhole binds: lower by 1 cm.
- **Variants**: #chemise, #childrens-elastic-waist-trousers.
- **CLO3D**: child avatar or a grading size; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: ratios text source (techniques 11.5); outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
