# Sleepsuit with Feet {#sleepsuit-with-feet}

- **Aliases**: footed pajamas, babygro, Baby Grow, sleeper, footie; fr: grenouillère, pyjama bébé avec pieds
- **Family**: babywear (knit)
- **Recognize**: a one-piece with sleeves and legs ending in feet, full length front zip or snaps from the neck to the crotch or ankle, fold-over mitts at the cuffs on newborn sizes.
- **Pieces**: front (2, left and right), back (1 or 2), sleeve (2), foot (2 or 4: upper and sole), neck binding, front facing or placket (2), zip (1) or snap tape (1), cuff (2), optional mitts (2).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Body: loose jumpsuit block with the front opening running from the neck down the front to the crotch and one leg (a diagonal or inseam opening) or straight to the ankles. Feet: each leg ends with a foot piece: a sole (a rounded oval of foot length and width + ease) sewn to the leg end opening, or a front upper plus a sole; foot length for a newborn size is not known as a number and must be taken from the size chart. Zip with a chin guard (tab) at the neck.
- **Drafting**: 1. Body block with 10 to 14 cm ease at the chest (children's ease, techniques 11.5 gives 8 to 12; typical). 2. Leg width tapered to the foot opening. 3. Foot sole length = foot length + 1.5 to 2 cm of growth (typical). 4. Front opening placement; zip length = pattern edge length.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders and sides, inseam, foot soles, zip, neck binding, sleeves, cuffs.
- **Finishes and notions**: zip with chin guard, flat seams, snaps, non-slip sole fabric on walking sizes, tags removed.
- **Fabrics**: cotton interlock, velour, fleece; avoid loose cords.
- **Fit faults**: feet too short: lengthen the sole; zip pulls up the neck: add a guard; sleeves rub: lengthen cuff.
- **Variants**: #romper, #adult-onesie, #baby-sleeping-bag.
- **CLO3D**: knit fabric; baby avatar; zipper trim.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
