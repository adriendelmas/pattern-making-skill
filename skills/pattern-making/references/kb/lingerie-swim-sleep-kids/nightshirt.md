# Nightshirt {#nightshirt}

- **Aliases**: night shirt, sleep shirt, big shirt; fr: chemise de nuit (shirt type), chemise de nuit homme
- **Family**: nightwear
- **Recognize**: shirt-like garment to thigh or knee: shirt collar (optional), partial placket with 2 to 4 buttons, long sleeves, curved shirttail hem, side vents.
- **Pieces**: front (2, left and right) or front (1, pullover), back (1, with box pleat or yoke), yoke (2), sleeve (2), cuff (2), collar (2 collar plus 1 band), placket (1 or 2), hem facings.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. The body is a shirt block extended below the hips: front and back with straight sides flaring by 4 to 8 cm per side, shirttail hem curved up at the sides by 5 to 8 cm (typical). Placket: a half-placket (through the chest), 20 to 25 cm long (typical) with 3.5 cm width. Collar is a band collar or a convertible collar as for the shirt (see a shirt entry in another file). Sleeves with a dropped shoulder or standard set-in.
- **Drafting**: from the shirt block, add 16 to 24 cm total ease at the chest (typical for a nightshirt), lengthen the body to mid-thigh or knee, add the side flare, shape the shirttail hem.
- **Sewing order**: yoke, placket, shoulders, collar, sleeves, side seams, cuffs, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: 4 to 6 buttons, French or flat-fell seams, narrow hem.
- **Fabrics**: cotton poplin, flannel, linen, jersey (for a pullover nightshirt).
- **Fit faults**: rides up: add hem length; binds at the hip: widen the flare.
- **Variants**: #nightgown, #pajama-top.
- **CLO3D**: as shirt; mesh 8 mm; collar layered over the band.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; typical numbers.
- **Status**: unverified
