# Boyshort {#boyshort}

- **Aliases**: boy short, short brief, cheeky short, women's boxer brief; fr: shorty, boxer femme
- **Family**: lingerie, briefs (knit)
- **Recognize**: brief with short legs ending 5 to 10 cm below the crotch; leg opening wider than a brief; plain hem or elastic at the leg.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), gusset outer and lining (1 each); waist elastic; leg hem facing or elastic.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified outlines. Front and back are cut like #basic-brief but the leg extends downward: the inseam is added below the crotch point C, a straight to slightly curved line 5 to 10 cm long (techniques 5.7 table). Inseam of FP is sewn to the inseam of BP; the gusset covers the crotch curve between the two inseam tops. The side seam runs from W straight (or slightly flared) to the hem. Some styles have no side seams (front and back cut as one tube).
- **Drafting**: 1. Draft #basic-brief. 2. Extend the side seam down and draw the inseam; leg width at the hem = thigh width x (1 - R), plus 0.5 cm for a loose hem. 3. Hem: turn 1.5 cm or elastic at 0.90.
- **Sewing order**: gusset, side seams, inseams, hems, waist elastic.
- **Finishes and notions**: coverstitch hem, 10 to 15 mm waist elastic.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey with elastane, microfibre.
- **Fit faults**: legs ride up: narrow the hem or add a silicone grip; thigh binds: widen the hem.
- **Variants**: #basic-brief, #boxer-briefs, #french-knickers.
- **CLO3D**: as #basic-brief; legs need mesh 8 mm or smaller.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; leg width unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
