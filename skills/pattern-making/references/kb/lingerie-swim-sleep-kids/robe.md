# Robe (dressing gown) {#robe}

- **Aliases**: bathrobe, dressing gown, house coat, wrap robe, banyan; fr: robe de chambre, peignoir, peignoir de bain
- **Family**: nightwear, loungewear
- **Recognize**: wrap front closed by a belt (tie belt in belt loops), shawl or notched collar, patch pockets, long sleeves, length from thigh to ankle; a bathrobe is in terry cloth, a dressing gown in wool, silk or fleece.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1, on the fold or with CB seam), sleeve (2), collar (1, shawl, with CB seam) or lapel/collar pair, front facing (2, or the collar and facing in one), belt (1), belt loops (2), patch pockets (2), cuffs (2, optional), hanging loop (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Front pieces overlap at CF by 12 to 20 cm total (typical), each front is cut wider than the back half by the overlap; front edge straight from neck to hem; the shawl collar is one piece: a long band that sits on the neck and runs down the front edges as a continuous roll collar, the outer edge being the collar-lapel edge and the inner edge sewn to the neckline and the front facing; shape of the neck points is not known as numbers. Sleeves: kimono, raglan or dropped set-in with wide hems; the belt is a long strap, 5 to 8 cm wide, length = waist + 90 to 120 cm for the tie (typical); belt loops at side seams at the waist level; pockets are 16 x 18 cm (typical).
- **Drafting**: 1. Use an oversize jacket or coat block (see outerwear), with 20 to 30 cm total ease at the chest (typical for a robe). 2. Front overlap 12 to 20 cm total; neckline lowered to the waist-level V. 3. Front facing 8 to 10 cm wide (typical). 4. Shawl collar: draw the roll line from the front neck point down to the belt level; draw the collar parallel to the front edge; collar width at the CB 7 to 9 cm. 5. Length per style. 6. Belt 5 to 8 cm wide.
- **Sewing order**: pockets, shoulder seams, sleeves, side seams, collar to neckline, facing, hem, belt and loops.
- **Finishes and notions**: belt loops at side seams, interfacing at the collar and the facing, hanging loop at the CB neck.
- **Fabrics**: terry cloth (bathrobe), fleece, wool, silk, cotton, velour; terry needs a larger allowance and shrinks on first wash.
- **Fit faults**: opens at the bust: increase overlap; collar collapses: interface; belt slides: move the loops lower.
- **Variants**: #kimono-robe, #pajama-top, #adult-onesie.
- **CLO3D**: coat template with a belt tied around the waist; fabric preset; wrap front overlap with two layers; add pins to keep the belt in place.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
