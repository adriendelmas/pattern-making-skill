# Bodysuit {#bodysuit}

- **Aliases**: leotard top, body, snap-crotch bodysuit, thong bodysuit; fr: body
- **Family**: lingerie, knits
- **Recognize**: one-piece top and brief, a close fit, a snap or hook-and-eye crotch closure, neck and arm edges finished with elastic or binding; stretch jersey.
- **Pieces**: techniques 5.8: front (1), back (1), gusset outer (1) and lining (1), neck/armhole elastic or binding, snaps.
- **Shape**: text source techniques 5.8 for the pieces and the torso loop T; trade practice for the shape, unverified: front and back pieces run from the shoulder line down the torso and between the legs without a waist seam; each piece has a leg curve at the bottom like #basic-brief; the gusset is joined to the front and back crotch edges; the snaps are on the back crotch flap, overlapping the front gusset by 2.5 to 3 cm.
- **Drafting**: techniques 5.8: torso length T measured from the neck point down the front, between the legs, up the back to the same shoulder point; the sum of front and back lengths = T x (1 - V) with V = 3 to 5 percent for 50 to 70 percent vertical stretch; add 2 to 4 cm for woven fabrics at the crotch; crotch width 8 to 10 cm with snaps; overlap 2.5 to 3 cm. Horizontal reductions as in techniques 2.2.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders and sides, neck/armhole finish, leg openings, gusset, snaps.
- **Finishes and notions**: snap tape or hooks, binding or elastic 10 to 15 mm at the neck and arms.
- **Fabrics**: cotton or modal jersey with elastane, lace stretch, rib.
- **Fit faults**: crotch pulls up: T too short: lengthen 1 to 2 cm; gapes at the neckline: shorten the neck edge.
- **Variants**: #teddy, #shapewear, #newborn-bodysuit for babies.
- **CLO3D**: single pattern pair plus gusset; strain map to check vertical stretch.
- **Confidence**: numbers from techniques 5.8; outline unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
