# Nightgown {#nightgown}

- **Aliases**: nightdress, nightie, long nightdress; fr: chemise de nuit, robe de nuit
- **Family**: nightwear
- **Recognize**: a loose dress of knee, calf or floor length, with a yoke and gathers, or a plain A-line; short or long sleeves, or straps; round, V or keyhole neckline; slit or buttons at the front.
- **Pieces**: yoke front (1, optionally 2 layers), yoke back (1, 2 layers), skirt front (1), skirt back (1), sleeve (2) or straps (2), neck facing or binding, optional button placket (1), sleeve cuffs (2).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Yoke type: the yoke is a shallow bodice piece from the shoulder to just under the bust (front depth about the armhole depth + 6 to 10 cm, typical); the skirt is a rectangle or trapezoid gathered to the yoke seam with a gathering ratio of 1.5 to 2 (typical) so the front yoke seam of the skirt is shorter than the skirt top; sleeves a simple set-in or raglan; the nightgown hem is a large sweep (wider than hip by 20 to 40 cm). Plain type: A-line tube from shoulder to hem cut with a front and back, the side seams flared 5 to 8 cm per side (typical); neckline wide enough to pass over the head without an opening (circumference at least 56 cm for an adult, a typical figure for a head-pass).
- **Drafting**: 1. Use a loose bodice block with 14 to 20 cm total ease at the bust (typical for nightwear). 2. Define the yoke seam. 3. Skirt width: hip + 20 to 40 cm at the hem; gather ratio 1.5 to 2. 4. Length per style; add 2 to 4 cm hem. 5. Neckline wide: depth and width verified by toile.
- **Sewing order**: yoke to yoke lining, shoulders, gather skirts, join to yoke, side seams, sleeves, neckline finish, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: French seams, narrow hem, buttons or snaps if a placket, lace trim.
- **Fabrics**: cotton lawn, voile, flannel, jersey, satin; woven fabrics need more ease than jersey.
- **Fit faults**: hangs from the bust: shorten the yoke; strains at the shoulder: widen the sleeve cap; neckline too small: lower the neck 1 to 2 cm.
- **Variants**: #nightshirt, #chemise, #slip-full.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset 8 to 10 mm; gathers with the shirring tool; use a standing avatar.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
