# Men's Briefs (Y-front) {#mens-briefs}

- **Aliases**: Y-front briefs, slips, jockey briefs; fr: slip homme, slip à ouverture Y
- **Family**: menswear underwear (knit)
- **Recognize**: low to mid rise, supportive pouch, Y-shaped seam at the front (fly overlap) or a seamless front, leg openings with elastic, wide waistband elastic (often exposed).
- **Pieces**: front (1, or left and right with a Y seam: 2), pouch lining (1), fly overlap and facing (1 each), back (1), seat gusset (optional, 1), waist elastic (1), leg elastic (2).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified outlines. Front: a curved rise from the waist down to the crotch with a pouch that is a smooth bulge; the fly: one side is a facing lying over the other, both finished at the Y seam where the leg curves meet. Y seam length is not known. Back: one piece with a CB curve; the seat is cut full. Side seams join front to back; the crotch seam is a gusset or a plain curve; the back leg curve is deeper than the front (techniques 5.7).
- **Drafting**: as #basic-brief with lower waist (low to mid rise); a front pouch added by pushing the front crotch curve outward (typical start 2 to 4 cm over 10 to 12 cm, a guess to be confirmed on a toile); fly overlap strips 3 to 4 cm wide (typical). Reduction as for knits, R = 10 to 15 percent.
- **Sewing order**: pouch lining to front, fly overlap, front to back at the side seams, crotch seam, leg elastic, waist elastic.
- **Finishes and notions**: 25 to 40 mm waist elastic, 10 to 12 mm leg elastic.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey with 5 percent elastane, interlock; pouch lining a lighter jersey.
- **Fit faults**: pouch too tight: add pouch depth; front gapes: raise waist; leg digs: lengthen elastic.
- **Variants**: #boxer-briefs, #basic-brief, #swim-briefs.
- **CLO3D**: build the pouch with an extra dart, mesh 6 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
