# Swim Shorts {#swim-shorts}

- **Aliases**: swim trunks, bathing trunks, board-style short (short length), men's swim shorts; fr: short de bain, maillot de bain boxer
- **Family**: swimwear (menswear)
- **Recognize**: loose shorts in light woven (or stretch) fabric, elastic waist with a drawcord, mesh brief liner, side seam pockets, leg length mid-thigh.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (2), waistband casing (1), elastic (1), drawcord (1), mesh liner front (1) and back (1) or a brief lining, side pockets (2), back pocket (1, optional).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Short trouser shape: front crotch and back crotch curves as in trousers; leg width at the hem wide; side seam straight or slightly curved to the hem, a small vent (hem slit) at the base of each side seam; the mesh liner a brief shape sewn into the waist seam and the leg edges. Waist: casing with elastic and drawcord through eyelets at the front.
- **Drafting**: from a short block with 10 to 20 cm ease at the hip (woven), crotch depth = rise + 2 to 3 cm (typical); casing 3.5 cm; eyelets two at CF; the liner pattern as #swim-briefs.
- **Sewing order**: pockets, liner to waist, side seams, inseam, crotch, casing, elastic, drawcord, hem.
- **Finishes and notions**: covered elastic 25 to 40 mm, drawcord, eyelets, hook-and-loop on a pocket.
- **Fabrics**: nylon or polyester taslan, quick dry, stretch microfibre; UPF fabrics.
- **Fit faults**: liner binds: widen or lengthen it; waist slips: shorten elastic.
- **Variants**: #boardshorts, #swim-briefs.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset, mesh 8 to 10 mm, liner as a separate fabric.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
