# One-Piece Swimsuit {#swimsuit-one-piece}

- **Aliases**: maillot, bathing suit, tank suit; fr: maillot de bain une pièce
- **Family**: swimwear
- **Recognize**: lined one-piece with leg openings in elastic, straps or halter neck, back cut-out sometimes, bust support by a lining or built-in cups.
- **Pieces**: techniques 6.2: front (1, or 2 with a CF seam), back (1, or 2 with a CB seam), crotch/gusset (1), front lining (1), back lining (1), crotch lining (1), cups (2, optional foam), straps (2 or 1), elastic for neck, armholes, legs and back.
- **Shape**: text source techniques 6.2 for the pieces and the dimensions: torso loop T reduced by V = 5 to 10 percent vertically; horizontal reductions 5 to 8 percent at the bust, 10 to 15 percent at the waist, 8 to 12 percent at the hip; leg line highest point on the side seam 10 to 14 cm above the crotch for a low leg, 14 to 18 cm for a high cut; the back leg curve is deeper for a high cut, the front curve lower than the back; crotch width 7 to 9 cm, crotch seam length 11 to 14 cm. Outline of the pieces is trade practice, unverified: front panel from the neckline (or straps) down the torso through the waist (narrowest) to the hip and then the leg curve to the crotch; back is similar with a back neckline.
- **Drafting**: techniques 6.2 steps 1 to 9: torso loop T; pattern length T x (1 - V); width reductions of 6.1; crotch width 7 to 9 cm; leg line; coverage; neckline and armholes with elastic 6 to 10 mm at 85 to 90 percent; cups 2.5 to 3 cm apart; lining cut as outer with the bottom lining 1 to 2 percent smaller.
- **Sewing order**: techniques 6.2: sew lining to outer at the neck (or raw edge), assemble front and back at the side seams, attach the gusset, elastics at the legs, neck and armholes (in that order), straps, cups.
- **Finishes and notions**: 3-step zigzag, ball-point needle 75/11, polyester or nylon thread, elastics 6 to 12 mm, rings and sliders if needed.
- **Fabrics**: nylon (or polyester) 80 / elastane 20, 70 to 100 percent stretch in W and 50 to 80 percent in L (techniques 6.1).
- **Fit faults**: techniques 6.2: leg gaping: shorten leg elastic 5 percent; bust gap: reduce the neckline length; back sag: shorten T by 1 to 2 cm; wrinkles at the crotch: shorten the gusset by 1 cm; straps fall: move inward by 1 cm.
- **Variants**: #swimsuit-built-in-cups, #tankini, #burkini.
- **CLO3D**: swim fabric preset, mesh 4 to 6 mm, elastic seams at the specified ratios, negative ease carried by the pattern.
- **Confidence**: numbers verified in the techniques sheet (typical); outlines unverified.
- **Status**: unverified
