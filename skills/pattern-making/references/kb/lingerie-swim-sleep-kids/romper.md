# Baby Romper {#romper}

- **Aliases**: playsuit, short romper, sunsuit, bubble romper; fr: barboteuse, combishort bébé
- **Family**: babywear
- **Recognize**: a short one-piece with leg openings, an elastic or bound leg, short or no sleeves, an opening by snaps at the crotch or the back; a bubble romper has gathered legs.
- **Pieces**: front (1), back (1), sleeve (2) or binding, neck binding or facing (1), leg bindings or elastic casings (2), crotch snap tape (1), optional bib front (1).
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. The shape is a baby bodysuit block with short legs: front and back continue below the crotch for 8 to 12 cm (typical for a short romper) and end in a leg opening finished with elastic; for a bubble romper the leg is wider than the hem and gathered at the elastic. Closure: crotch flaps with snaps, or a back opening with snaps.
- **Drafting**: as #newborn-bodysuit with the leg extended; elastic at the leg opening 0.85 to 0.90 of the thigh circumference; growth allowance as #children-growth-allowances-grading.
- **Sewing order**: shoulders, side seams, neck, sleeves, leg elastic, crotch snaps.
- **Finishes and notions**: snaps, 6 to 10 mm elastic, binding.
- **Fabrics**: cotton jersey, poplin, seersucker, gauze (woven requires an opening).
- **Fit faults**: crotch too short: add length; leg elastic marks: lengthen elastic by 3 percent.
- **Variants**: #newborn-bodysuit, #sleepsuit-with-feet.
- **CLO3D**: as #newborn-bodysuit; a baby avatar.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; unverified outlines.
- **Status**: unverified
