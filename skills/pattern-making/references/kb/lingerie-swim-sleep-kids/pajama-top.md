# Pajama Top {#pajama-top}

- **Aliases**: pyjama top, pajama shirt, sleep shirt (set), PJ top; fr: haut de pyjama, veste de pyjama
- **Family**: nightwear
- **Recognize**: a loose shirt-like top, notch collar (the classic), full button front, one chest patch pocket, long or short sleeves, straight hem with side vents or a curved shirttail.
- **Pieces**: front (2), back (1), sleeve (2), upper collar (1), under collar (1), collar stand (optional), front facing (2, or cut-on), patch pocket (1), cuff (2, optional), hem facing or hem.
- **Shape**: trade practice, unverified. Block: loose shirt block; collar: a notch (revers) collar with a collar stand lapped to the front opening, or a simple collar with the neck seam at the top of the front facing; the front facing is cut on or applied, 3.5 to 4 cm wide (typical); pocket a flat rectangle 11 x 12 cm with a curved or pointed bottom, positioned at the left chest (typical); shoulder seams slope less than a shirt block; the hem is straight with 6 to 8 cm side vents or curved up at the sides.
- **Drafting**: 1. Use the shirt block or a loose bodice block. 2. Add total ease at the chest of 14 to 20 cm (typical). 3. Button stand 1.5 cm each (as shirt), front facing 3.5 cm. 4. Collar: notch collar from front neck; length of the collar neck edge = the neckline measured on the garment. 5. Pocket location: 8 to 10 cm below the shoulder-neck and 5 cm in from the front edge (typical, unverified).
- **Sewing order**: pocket, shoulder seams, facing, collar, sleeve, side seams, hems, buttonholes.
- **Finishes and notions**: piping (optional on contrast), 5 to 7 buttons, interfacing at the collar.
- **Fabrics**: cotton, flannel, silk, satin.
- **Fit faults**: collar stands away: shorten the collar edge by 0.5 cm; gapes at the bust: add width in the front.
- **Variants**: #nightshirt, #robe.
- **CLO3D**: woven preset; collar layered; mesh 8 mm.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; numbers typical.
- **Status**: unverified
