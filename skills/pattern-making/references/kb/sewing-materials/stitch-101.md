# ISO 4915 stitch 101: single-thread chainstitch {#stitch-101}

- **Aliases**: single-thread chainstitch, hand-stitch imitation, 101; fr: point de chaînette un fil
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 100 (chainstitches)
- **Recognize**: a straight line on the face, a chain of loops on the underside; unravels when the end is pulled; used for temporary stitching, sack closing and some blind stitching (103)
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: single line of stitching; no change to the outline or the seam allowance of the seam type. Source: unverified (class definition from secondary ISO summaries).
- **Drafting**: no change; allowance as for the seam type (ST section 1.2).
- **Sewing order**: single-needle chainstitch machine without bobbin.
- **Finishes and notions**: low seam strength; not used for garment seams that take stress.
- **Fabrics**: temporary seams, basting machines, packaging.
- **Fit faults**: seam unravels -> choose 301 or 401.
- **Variants**: #stitch-301, #stitch-401, #machine-blind-hem (stitch 103 is the blind stitch relative).
- **CLO3D**: not modelled.
- **Confidence**: from memory, consistent with the A&E, Coats and Kohl's ISO sheets.
- **Status**: unverified
