# Cylinder-bed machine {#machine-cylinder-bed}

- **Aliases**: tubular bed machine, cylinder arm, free-arm industrial machine; fr: machine à bras, machine à bras libre, machine à tube
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a sewing machine whose bed is a horizontal cylinder (arm) 4 to 8 cm across; allows sewing the inside of a tube: cuffs, sleeve hems, trouser hems, bags
- **Pieces**: no special piece; tubular parts are finished after the side seam is joined
- **Shape**: lets a cuff, a sleeve hem, a trouser hem or a bag base be sewn after the tube is closed; the pattern can therefore close the side seam first and hem after (hem allowance 2.5 to 4 cm). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: hem allowance as the hem type.
- **Sewing order**: hem or cuff after the seam closing.
- **Finishes and notions**: walking foot.
- **Fabrics**: all, especially heavy fabrics and leather.
- **Fit faults**: hem puckers on narrow tubes -> take a narrower hem and ease the hem edge.
- **Variants**: #machine-post-bed, free-arm home machines.
- **CLO3D**: not relevant.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
