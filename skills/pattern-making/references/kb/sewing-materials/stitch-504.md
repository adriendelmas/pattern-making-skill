# ISO 4915 stitch 504: three-thread overedge {#stitch-504}

- **Aliases**: three-thread overlock, three-thread serger stitch, 504; fr: surjet 3 fils, point de surjet
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 500 (overedge chainstitches)
- **Recognize**: loops of thread wrapped round the cut edge; one needle thread, an upper looper and a lower looper; stretchy, neat edge
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: the knife trims 3 to 5 mm, the SA stays 0.6 to 1 cm (ST section 1.2); seam, trim and finish in one pass. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 0.6 to 1 cm.
- **Sewing order**: knit side and sleeve seams, edge finishing of wovens.
- **Finishes and notions**: serger thread on cones, wooly nylon in the loopers for stretch.
- **Fabrics**: knits and light wovens; for stressed woven seams use 514 or 516.
- **Fit faults**: edge rolls -> narrow the stitch width or change the tension; seam bursts in wovens -> 514, 516 or sew with 301 first.
- **Variants**: #stitch-514, #stitch-516, #machine-overlock; 503 (2-thread) for narrow edges.
- **CLO3D**: not modelled.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides; SA from ST.
- **Status**: unverified
