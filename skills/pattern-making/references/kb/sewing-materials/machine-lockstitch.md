# Lockstitch machine {#machine-lockstitch}

- **Aliases**: single-needle lockstitch machine, plain sewing machine, industrial straight stitch machine, SNLS; fr: machine à point droit, piqueuse plate, machine à coudre industrielle
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a flat bed with one needle, a bobbin and a feed dog; makes stitch 301 (#stitch-301); the general-purpose machine of every workshop
- **Pieces**: no special piece. Needs a pattern with SA wide enough to feed under the foot without a folder (0.6 cm minimum, 1 to 1.5 cm normal)
- **Shape**: no effect on the outline. The seam needs a separate edge finish (overlock or binding). Stitch length 2.5 mm on wovens (ST section 0), 3 to 3.5 mm topstitch, 4 to 5 mm basting; industrial speed typically 4000 to 5000 stitches per minute (from memory). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 1 cm (industry) or 1.5 cm (alterable seams), see ST section 1.2.
- **Sewing order**: seams, darts, topstitching, attaching waistbands, collars, cuffs.
- **Finishes and notions**: needle by fabric (#needles-by-fabric), feet (#presser-feet); needle-feed and walking-foot variants for thick or slippery layers.
- **Fabrics**: all wovens; light knits with a stretch stitch.
- **Fit faults**: puckered seam -> reduce top tension, shorten pressure, change needle; uneven layers -> walking foot.
- **Variants**: #machine-chainstitch, #machine-cylinder-bed, #machine-post-bed.
- **CLO3D**: default seam behaviour.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
