# ISO 4915 stitch 516: five-thread safety stitch {#stitch-516}

- **Aliases**: safety stitch, 401 + 504, 516; 515 (401 + 503, 4 threads) is the lighter version; fr: point de sûreté, surjet de sécurité
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 500
- **Recognize**: a chainstitch (401) line a few mm inside a three-thread overedge (504), formed together in one pass; woven side seams, inseams, shirt seams
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: SA 1 cm (ST section 1.2); the chainstitch sits a few mm inside the overedge (exact distance depends on the machine, not known). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 1 cm, 1.5 cm if the seam may be altered.
- **Sewing order**: one-pass seam and finish for wovens.
- **Finishes and notions**: five threads.
- **Fabrics**: wovens that bear stress.
- **Fit faults**: seam cannot be let out -> 1.5 cm SA.
- **Variants**: 515; #stitch-401, #stitch-504.
- **CLO3D**: not modelled.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides (A&E, Coats).
- **Status**: unverified
