# Bartack machine {#machine-bartack}

- **Aliases**: bar tacker, tack machine, bartacking, reinforcement stitch; fr: bartack, bride, point d'arrêt, machine à brider
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a dense block of zigzag stitches (28 or 42 stitches in a rectangle, 2 to 3 mm wide and 8 to 16 mm long, typical) at stress points: belt loop ends, pocket corners, fly end, strap attachments, vent tops
- **Pieces**: no special piece; the pattern marks the position by a drill hole, a notch or a mark at both ends
- **Shape**: the tack length is chosen to the stress; marks at pocket corners are 3 to 6 mm from the corner, belt loops are tacked at 2 positions per end. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: mark bartack positions on the pattern with a drill hole or a line.
- **Sewing order**: last operation at the pocket corners and fly; first for hidden labels.
- **Finishes and notions**: strong thread (Tex 40 to 60).
- **Fabrics**: denim, twill, workwear, trousers.
- **Fit faults**: pocket tears -> add a tack across the pocket corner; tack too small -> use a longer program.
- **Variants**: eyelets and buttonholes (#machine-buttonhole).
- **CLO3D**: not modelled; mark the position as a note.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; stitch counts and dimensions from memory.
- **Status**: unverified
