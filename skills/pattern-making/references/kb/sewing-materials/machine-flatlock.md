# Flatlock machine {#machine-flatlock}

- **Aliases**: flatseamer, flatbed coverstitch, 607 machine, flat seamer; fr: machine à couture plate, plateuse
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a flat-bed machine making a flat seam; cylinder or flat bed with a built-in feeding system; used for underwear, sport and swimwear, and for ladder-stitch decorative seams
- **Pieces**: no extra piece; the pieces are cut with no allowance (butted) or a 3 to 6 mm overlap
- **Shape**: SA 0 to 0.6 cm. The pattern should have well-matched edges since there is no allowance to trim. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 0.6 cm.
- **Sewing order**: seam, then pull flat.
- **Finishes and notions**: wooly nylon, elastic.
- **Fabrics**: knits, fleece, base layers.
- **Fit faults**: seam widens -> match the seam with a stabiliser tape.
- **Variants**: #stitch-607, #machine-coverstitch.
- **CLO3D**: no direct equivalent.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
