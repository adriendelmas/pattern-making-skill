# Needles by fabric {#needles-by-fabric}

- **Aliases**: sewing machine needles, needle sizes; fr: aiguilles de machine, calibres d'aiguilles
- **Family**: machine accessories
- **Recognize**: a needle is described by its system, point and size (metric NM, e.g. 70 to 110; US 10 to 18)
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: sizes: 60 to 70 for chiffon, organza and lawn; 70 to 80 for silk, light cotton and fine knits; 80 to 90 for wool suiting, medium cotton and jersey; 90 to 100 for denim and canvas; 100 to 110 or more for heavy denim, upholstery and leather (ST section 9 lists 70/10 to 100/16). Source: unverified (sizes from ST and trade practice).
- **Drafting**: not applicable.
- **Sewing order**: change the needle every garment (or after 8 hours' sewing).
- **Finishes and notions**: universal (slightly rounded point, wovens); sharp or microtex (fine point, silk, microfibres, topstitching, plaids); ballpoint or jersey (rounded tip, knits); stretch (special scarf, elastic and lycra); denim or jeans (reinforced shaft, sharp tip); leather or wedge (cutting point, leather and vinyl; never on knits); topstitch (large eye, topstitching); metallic (large eye).
- **Fabrics**: match the needle tip to the fibre: sharp for silk, rounded for knits, cutting for leather.
- **Fit faults**: skipped stitches on knits -> ballpoint or stretch needle; holes or runs in fine knits -> finer ballpoint; thread breaking -> larger eye; punctures along seam in silk -> finer sharp needle.
- **Variants**: #presser-feet, #threads-by-fabric.
- **CLO3D**: not relevant.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
