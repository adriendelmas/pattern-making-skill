# ISO 4915 stitch 605: three-needle coverstitch {#stitch-605}

- **Aliases**: three-needle five-thread coverstitch, triple coverstitch, 605; fr: recouvrement 3 aiguilles
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 600
- **Recognize**: three parallel rows of straight stitching on the face and a cover thread on the back; lap seams, coverseaming and binding on knits
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: three rows at about 3 to 6 mm gauge (typical); used on flat joined seams where two edges are butted or overlapped. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: butt seam SA 0 with a stabilising tape; lap seam 1 cm.
- **Sewing order**: flat joining, binding or waist elastic attachment.
- **Finishes and notions**: five threads.
- **Fabrics**: knits, activewear, elastic.
- **Fit faults**: ridge -> reduce differential feed, lighter thread.
- **Variants**: #stitch-602, #stitch-607.
- **CLO3D**: triple topstitch.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides.
- **Status**: unverified
