# ISO 4915 stitch 602: two-needle coverstitch {#stitch-602}

- **Aliases**: two-needle coverstitch, coverstitch hem, 602; 406 is the two-needle bottom cover version (looper only below); fr: point de recouvrement 2 aiguilles, recouvreuse
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 600 (covering chainstitches)
- **Recognize**: two parallel rows of straight stitches on the face; on the underside a cover of thread (602 has a top cover thread as well, so the stitch is covered above and below); hems, neckbands and waistbands of knits
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: hem allowance 2 to 2.5 cm (ST section 1.2); needle gauge 3 to 6 mm (typical). Source: unverified (class definition verified in secondary guides).
- **Drafting**: knit hem 2 to 2.5 cm; bands follow the folder width.
- **Sewing order**: after the garment is assembled, hem turned once and stitched.
- **Finishes and notions**: stretch thread (textured polyester or wooly nylon in the looper), folders.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, interlock, rib, fleece, elastane blends.
- **Fit faults**: tunnelling between the rows -> reduce tension; skipped stitches -> ballpoint needle.
- **Variants**: #stitch-605, #stitch-607, #machine-coverstitch.
- **CLO3D**: represent with a double topstitch at the same distance.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides; distances typical.
- **Status**: unverified
