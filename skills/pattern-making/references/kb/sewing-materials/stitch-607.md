# ISO 4915 stitch 607: flatlock {#stitch-607}

- **Aliases**: flatlock, flatseamer, four-needle six-thread flat seam, 607; fr: couture plate, assemblage à plat
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 600
- **Recognize**: a flat seam without ridge; two cut edges butted or slightly overlapped; ladder stitching on one side, loops on the other; underwear, activewear, fleece
- **Pieces**: no extra piece; edges cut with no allowance (butted) or with a 3 to 6 mm overlap
- **Shape**: SA 0 to 0.6 cm, trimmed to 3 to 5 mm (ST section 1.2). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 0.6 cm.
- **Sewing order**: join flat, then pull the seam flat so the ladder shows.
- **Finishes and notions**: wooly nylon for softness.
- **Fabrics**: knits, fleece, activewear, base layers; not for stressed woven seams.
- **Fit faults**: seam opens when pulled -> check tension; scratchy -> choose soft thread.
- **Variants**: #stitch-602, #stitch-605, #machine-flatlock.
- **CLO3D**: model as a seam without thickness.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides; allowance from ST.
- **Status**: unverified
