# Post-bed machine {#machine-post-bed}

- **Aliases**: post machine, column machine, post-bed lockstitch, glove machine, boot machine; fr: machine à colonne, machine à poteau
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a machine with a vertical post (column) 15 to 30 cm high rising from a table; used for gloves, hats, boots, shoe uppers and bags where the work wraps around the needle
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: sewing of three-dimensional shapes without flattening: the pattern pieces of gloves (fourchettes, thumb gusset), shoe uppers or hats can be sewn around the post. Seam allowance on gloves is small (3 to 5 mm) on the machine and may be inside the glove or outside (pique/ outseam). Source: unverified (details of glove seam allowances not verified).
- **Drafting**: small allowance, check the glove pattern.
- **Sewing order**: sew fourchettes and thumb gussets last; join the back and palm.
- **Finishes and notions**: top feed or compound feed; leather needle; fine thread (Tex 30 to 40).
- **Fabrics**: leather gloves, hats, stiff sail cloth.
- **Fit faults**: puckering at fourchette corners -> match notches; skipped stitches on leather -> cutting-point needle.
- **Variants**: #machine-cylinder-bed.

- **Confidence**: from memory.
- **Status**: unverified
