# Chainstitch machine {#machine-chainstitch}

- **Aliases**: double-locked chainstitch machine, two-thread chainstitch, 401 machine, multi-needle chainstitch (2 to 4 needles); fr: machine à point de chaînette, piqueuse chaînette
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a machine with a looper instead of a bobbin; makes 401 (#stitch-401) in one or several rows; common for felled seams (jeans), waistbands and shirts' side seams
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: as the felled seam: 1.5 cm both sides, with a felling folder (ST section 1.1). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: as seam type.
- **Sewing order**: long continuous seams.
- **Finishes and notions**: looper thread on cones; waistband folder, felling folder.
- **Fabrics**: denim, twill, drill, shirts.
- **Fit faults**: chain unravels -> back-tack or cross the end.
- **Variants**: #machine-lockstitch, #machine-overlock.
- **CLO3D**: not distinguished.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
