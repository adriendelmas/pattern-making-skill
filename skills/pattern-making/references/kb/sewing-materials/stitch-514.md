# ISO 4915 stitch 514: four-thread overedge {#stitch-514}

- **Aliases**: four-thread overlock, two-needle four-thread overedge, mock safety stitch, 514; fr: surjet 4 fils
- **Family**: ISO 4915 stitch types, class 500 (overedge chainstitches)
- **Recognize**: an overlocked edge with two parallel needle lines inside the loops; broader and stronger than 504
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: SA 1 cm (ST section 1.2); knife trims about 3 to 5 mm. Source: unverified. One source (Kohl's ISO list) shows 514 with a single needle; A&E and Coats guides give two needles and four threads, which is the usual form.
- **Drafting**: SA 1 cm.
- **Sewing order**: one-pass seaming of light-medium wovens and knits.
- **Finishes and notions**: serger thread.
- **Fabrics**: wovens (light-medium), jersey, fleece.
- **Fit faults**: seam pulls out in a stressed woven -> use 516.
- **Variants**: #stitch-504, #stitch-516.
- **CLO3D**: not modelled.
- **Confidence**: structure verified in secondary guides (needle count disagrees between them).
- **Status**: unverified
