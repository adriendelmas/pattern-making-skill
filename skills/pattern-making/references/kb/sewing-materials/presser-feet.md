# Presser feet {#presser-feet}

- **Aliases**: sewing machine feet, foot attachments, folders; fr: pied-de-biche, pieds, guides et plieurs
- **Family**: machine accessories
- **Recognize**: the part holding the fabric on the feed dog; the shape is chosen for the task
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: some feet need pattern allowances: zipper foot (needs about 3 mm between the stitch line and the teeth; a seam allowance of 1.5 to 2 cm at the zip), invisible-zip foot (needs a 1.5 to 2 cm allowance), rolled-hem foot (0 allowance on the edge, loses about 3 mm), blind-hem foot (hem 2.5 to 4 cm), piping foot (piping flange 1.5 cm, ST section 1.1), buttonhole foot (hole length = button diameter + thickness). Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: allowances per foot as above.
- **Sewing order**: not applicable.
- **Finishes and notions**: general foot; zipper foot; invisible zip foot; walking or even-feed foot (slippery layers, quilts, plaids, knits); roller or Teflon foot (leather, vinyl, PU, coated fabrics); edge or stitch-in-the-ditch foot; gathering foot; binder foot; blind hem foot; cording or piping foot; felling and hemmer folders.
- **Fabrics**: Teflon/roller for leather and vinyl; walking foot for knits, velvet and multiple layers; narrow foot for fine silk.
- **Fit faults**: layers creep -> walking foot or less presser pressure; foot marks on velvet -> roller foot or a hand pad.
- **Variants**: see #needles-by-fabric and #threads-by-fabric.
- **CLO3D**: not relevant.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
