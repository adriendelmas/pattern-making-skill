# Coverstitch machine {#machine-coverstitch}

- **Aliases**: coverhem machine, coverseamer, recouvreuse, interlock machine (home name); fr: recouvreuse, machine à point de recouvrement
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: two or three needles and a bottom looper (optionally a top cover thread); makes 406, 602, 605 (#stitch-602, #stitch-605)
- **Pieces**: no special piece; bands and hems cut with folds for the folder
- **Shape**: knit hem allowance 2 to 2.5 cm; neckbands and waistbands run through a binder or a folder; chain-off at the end is finished by tying or overlocking. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: hem 2 to 2.5 cm, see ST section 2.
- **Sewing order**: hems and neckbands after assembly; for a flat neckband seam use the right side as the face.
- **Finishes and notions**: stretch thread, folders.
- **Fabrics**: jersey, interlock, rib, fleece, lycra blends.
- **Fit faults**: hem flares or waves -> differential feed 1.0 to 1.5, press the hem; tunnelling -> needle thread tension lower.
- **Variants**: twin-needle on a home machine (a zigzag cover on the back, not the same stitch).
- **CLO3D**: double topstitch.
- **Confidence**: trade practice.
- **Status**: unverified
