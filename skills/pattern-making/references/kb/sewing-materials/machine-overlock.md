# Overlock (serger) machine {#machine-overlock}

- **Aliases**: serger, overlocker, merrow machine, surjeteuse; fr: surjeteuse, machine à surjet, overlock
- **Family**: machines
- **Recognize**: a bed with a knife, one or two needles, one or two loopers and three to five threads; makes stitches 503, 504, 514, 516 (#stitch-504, #stitch-514, #stitch-516)
- **Pieces**: no special piece
- **Shape**: the knife trims 3 to 5 mm from the cut edge in the same pass, so the pattern allowance is 0.6 to 1 cm; the stitch width is 4 to 5 mm (ST section 1.2). A rolled-hem setting makes a 2 to 3 mm hem on the cut edge: allowance 0 (cut edge is the hem line) plus a small amount lost. Differential feed (typical range 0.7 to 2.0) controls stretching or gathering. Source: unverified.
- **Drafting**: SA 1 cm; for a gathered attachment run the differential to 2.0 on the long edge.
- **Sewing order**: seaming and edge finishing of knits, finishing of woven seams after they are stitched.
- **Finishes and notions**: serger thread on cones, wooly nylon in loopers for swimwear or soft hems.
- **Fabrics**: knits, light wovens, chiffon (rolled hem), fleece.
- **Fit faults**: wavy seam on knits -> reduce differential feed to 0.8 to 1.0 or stabilise; puckered seam on thin fabric -> reduce tension, narrow stitch.
- **Variants**: safety-stitch version (#stitch-516); coverstitch (#machine-coverstitch).
- **CLO3D**: no direct equivalent; model a seam without extra thickness.
- **Confidence**: trade practice; speeds and ranges from memory.
- **Status**: unverified
